Florists pay a subscription to use StemCounts. Wholesalers do not: they
never see the subscription section and are never blocked by it.

The subscription belongs to your **organisation**, so one subscription
covers everyone in your florist business. There are no per-user fees.
It lives in **Settings**, under **Your Organisation**, in the
**Subscription** section.

## The free trial

Every new florist organisation gets a **7-day free trial**. It starts
automatically when the organisation is created at sign-up, and no card
is needed. People who join an existing organisation later do not get a
new trial, because the trial belongs to the organisation.

While it runs:

- You have full access to every feature.
- A banner under the header on every page reads **"X days left in your
  trial"** with a **Subscribe to StemCounts** button.
- The **Subscription** section reads **Trial: X days left** with "Your
  trial ends on [date]."

When it ends without a subscription, the status reads **Trial ended**
and florist pages are paused. See [Trial and paused
access](/billing/trial-and-blocked-access/).

## Plans

There are two florist plans, in Australian dollars: **6 months** and
**12 months**. Each renews automatically at the end of its period until
cancelled. The price is shown beside each plan in **Choose a plan**, and
that is what you pay. StemCounts can set a different price for an
individual business, which then appears in the same place.

headed **Checkout canceled**. Nothing has been charged and nothing has
changed. The same **Subscription** section is shown underneath, so you
can try again straight away.

<Aside type="note">
Subscribing during the trial starts your paid plan from checkout. Your
remaining free days are not added onto the paid period, so there is no
benefit in waiting until the last day, but no need to rush either.
</Aside>

## Subscription statuses

The **Subscription** section shows one status and, where it matters, a
date:

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Trial: X days left** | Your free trial is running. "Your trial ends on [date]." |
| **Trial ended** | The trial is over and there is no subscription. Choose a plan to continue. |
| **Active** | Paid and working. "Renews on [date]." or "Next payment on [date]." A cancellation you have scheduled shows "Ends on [date], cancellation scheduled." |
| **Past due** | The last payment failed. See [Managing your subscription](/billing/managing-subscription/). |
| **Cancelled** | The subscription has ended. "Ended on [date]." |
| **Incomplete** | "Your first payment has not gone through." |
| **No subscription** | No trial or plan on record. |

With **Trial ended**, **Cancelled**, **Incomplete** or **No
subscription**, the **Plan** row offers **Choose** so you can subscribe
again.

## Receipts and tax invoices

Once you have paid, the **Subscription** section shows a **Receipts** row
with a count. Click it to open **Receipts**, "Tax invoices and receipts
for your subscription." Each paid one is a **Tax invoice / receipt**
showing the date paid, the amount and what it covered, with **View** (the
StemCounts-branded PDF opens in the app) and **Download**. An unpaid
renewal shows as **Awaiting payment**.

A receipt is also emailed automatically after each successful payment.

<Aside type="note">
Today's receipts do not state a GST amount. For a GST question about
your subscription, email **support@stemcounts.com**.
</Aside>

## Already subscribed?

Each organisation can have only one live subscription. If someone in
your business already subscribed, the button says "Your organisation
already has a subscription. Manage it instead of starting a new one."
Use **Manage subscription** rather than subscribing again, so you are
not charged twice.
