Florist access to StemCounts is **subscription-gated**. Wholesalers
don't need a subscription.

<HelpMedia id="account-subscription-card" caption="The Subscription card on the Account page" />

## What the subscription includes

Active subscription unlocks:

- Building and sending unlimited orders
- Multi-event weekly orders
- The Flower Library and Flower Picker
- Printable event sheets
- Email notifications and reports

Without an active subscription, the app shows a hard block. See [Trial
and blocked access](/billing/trial-and-blocked-access/).

## The free trial

New florists get a **30-day free trial** automatically. During the trial:

- You have full access to every feature
- A persistent banner at the top of the app shows **"X days left in
  your trial"** with a **Subscribe to StemCounts** button
- The countdown updates daily

## Subscribing

<Steps>

1. Click **Account** in the user menu (top-right avatar) — or click
   **Subscribe to StemCounts** on the trial banner.

2. The Account page loads with the **Subscription** card visible.

3. Click **Subscribe**.

   <HelpMedia id="subscribe-button" />

4. You're redirected to **Stripe Checkout** to enter payment details.

5. Complete the Stripe checkout. On success Stripe sends you back to
   the StemCounts Account page with a green confirmation banner:
   **"Subscription successful. Your subscription is active."**

</Steps>

If you abandon the Stripe checkout, you're returned to the **Subscription
Cancel** page with a banner saying **"Checkout canceled"**. Your account
is unchanged — you can try again.

## Subscription statuses

The Subscription card on the Account page shows your current status:

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Trial** / **Trialing** | Active free trial; access full |
| **Active** | Paid; access full |
| **Past Due** | Payment failed; access still full but at risk |
| **Cancelled** | Cancelled by you; access ends at period end |
| **Incomplete** | Stripe needs another action; access may be blocked |

If you see **No subscription found for your organisation yet** on the
Account page and you've already paid, contact support — your Stripe
record may not have synced.

## Pricing and plans

Tiers and prices are managed in Stripe — see the Account page for what
applies to you. The Stripe portal (see [Managing your
subscription](/billing/managing-subscription/)) shows the current price
and renewal date.

<Aside type="note">
Plan switching is also done through the Stripe portal — not yet a
direct in-app action.
</Aside>

## Receipts and tax invoices

Stripe sends an email receipt for every successful charge. To get a
tax invoice (with GST), open the [Stripe customer
portal](/billing/managing-subscription/) and click **Download
invoice** next to the most recent charge.

For invoices that need to be customised (P.O. number, special billing
contact), email **support@stemcounts.com**.
