Everything about your account lives in **Settings**, under **You**:
**Your Profile** (your own details and notifications) and **Your
Organisation** (your business details and, for florists, the
**Subscription** section). There is no separate Account page. Florists
need an active trial or subscription to use StemCounts; wholesalers do
not.
