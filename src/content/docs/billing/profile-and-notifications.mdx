Your own details live in **Settings** under **Your Profile**. Open the
menu under your name at the top right, click **Settings**, then **Your
Profile**. This is where you keep your name and contact details, and
how StemCounts gets in touch with you.

## How the screen works

Everything reads as a list of rows. Click a row to open a small sheet
for that one thing, change it and save. Switches save the moment you
flip them. On success you see **"Saved"**; if something goes wrong you
see **"Could not save. Please try again."**

## What is on the page

| Group | Rows |
|---|---|
| **Account** | **Name**, **Email**, **Mobile** |
| **Contact and social** | **Website**, **Best way to reach you**, and your Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and LinkedIn handles. All optional. |
| **Notifications** | **Order emails**, **Tips when you sign in** |
| **Security** | **Password** |

## Your email cannot be changed here

Your **Email** is shown but not editable. The screen says: "Your email
is how you sign in. To change it, ask the StemCounts team." Email
**support@stemcounts.com** from your current address with the new one
you want to use. Your **Name** and **Mobile** you can change yourself.

## Change your password

<Steps>

1. In **Your Profile**, click **Password** under **Security**.

2. The **Change password** sheet asks for your **Current password**,
   your **New password** and **Confirm new password**. The new one needs
   at least 8 characters, with upper and lower case letters, a number
   and a symbol.

   <Tabs syncKey="device">
     <TabItem label="Computer">
       <HelpMedia id="settings-profile-desktop-03-change-password-sheet" caption="Change password asks for your Current password, a New password and to Confirm new password, then Update password." />
     </TabItem>
     <TabItem label="Phone">
       <HelpMedia id="settings-profile-phone-03-change-password-sheet" caption="Change password asks for your Current password, a New password and to Confirm new password, then Update password." />
     </TabItem>
   </Tabs>

3. Click **Update password**.

</Steps>

If the current password is wrong, the change is refused and you are
told so. If you cannot remember it, sign out and use **Forgot
password?** on the sign-in screen instead. See [Forgot your
password](/getting-started/forgot-password/).

## Order emails

The **Notifications** group has an **Order emails** switch, described as
"New orders, changes and updates from your wholesalers." Turn it on or
off and it saves straight away. It is a switch for your own account:
each person in your organisation sets their own.

Subscription receipts are emailed regardless of this switch.
</Aside>

## Tips when you sign in

Now and then, after you sign in, a small **Did you know?** card offers
one tip about StemCounts. The **Tips when you sign in** switch turns
those cards on and off. See [What's new and
updates](/getting-started/whats-new-and-updates/) for what the card does.

## What is not on this page

- **Business details** (name, ABN, address, logo): **Your Organisation**.
  See [Your organisation](/billing/your-organisation/).
- **Subscription**: the **Subscription** section of **Your
  Organisation**. See [Your StemCounts subscription](/billing/subscription/).
- **Team members**: there is no invite button. Each person creates
  their own account and picks your organisation at sign-up. See
