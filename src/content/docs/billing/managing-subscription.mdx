Once you are subscribed, the **Subscription** section of **Your
Organisation** shows a **Manage subscription** row, described as "Update
your card or cancel". It opens Stripe's secure billing page for your
organisation, in the same tab. When you are done, the page's return link
brings you back to StemCounts.

## Open Manage subscription

<Steps>

1. Go to **Settings**, then **Your Organisation**, and scroll to
   **Subscription**.

2. Click **Manage subscription**.

3. Stripe's billing page opens. It already knows your organisation.

</Steps>

**Manage subscription** only appears once you have a subscription. If you
have not subscribed yet, the **Plan** row with **Choose** is there
instead. See [Your StemCounts subscription](/billing/subscription/).

## Update your card

On Stripe's billing page, change your payment card. The new card is
used for the next charge.

## Cancel

Cancel on Stripe's billing page. A cancellation set for the end of your
paid period shows in StemCounts as **Active** with "Ends on [date],
cancellation scheduled.", and you keep access until then. Once it has
ended the status reads **Cancelled** with "Ended on [date]."

Your data stays in your account. You can subscribe again at any time
from **Plan** and **Choose**. If you sign in after the end date without
subscribing, you see the paused screen described in [Trial and paused
access](/billing/trial-and-blocked-access/).

## Switching between the 6 and 12 month plans

Plan changes are not offered on Stripe's billing page, and the app will
not start a second subscription while one is active. To move to a
different plan, email **support@stemcounts.com** and the team will change
it for you.

## Failed payments

If a renewal payment fails, your status becomes **Past due** and you
keep access for **7 days** from the renewal date. A banner under the
header says **"Your last subscription payment did not go through. X days
to fix it."** (or "Access ends today.") and "Update your payment details
under Manage subscription by [date] to keep ordering."

<Steps>

1. Click **Update payment details** on the banner. It takes you to the
   **Subscription** section.

2. Click **Manage subscription** and update your card on Stripe's
   billing page.

</Steps>

If it is not fixed within the 7 days, florist access is paused until
payment goes through. If you try to subscribe again instead, the app
says "Your subscription has a payment outstanding. Update your payment
details instead of starting a new subscription."

<Aside type="note">
For anything Stripe's billing page cannot do, email
**support@stemcounts.com**.
</Aside>
