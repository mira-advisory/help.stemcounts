Once you have an active subscription, day-to-day changes happen in the
**Stripe customer portal** — not directly inside StemCounts. The
portal is hosted by Stripe and opens in a new tab.

<HelpMedia id="manage-subscription-button" caption="The Manage subscription button opens the Stripe portal" />

## Open the portal

<Steps>

1. Go to **Account** (user menu → Account).

2. Click **Manage subscription**.

3. You're sent to the Stripe-hosted portal. The portal recognises your
   StemCounts account automatically.

</Steps>

## What you can do in the portal

| Action | Result |
|---|---|
| **Update payment method** | Swap your card. The new card is used for the next charge. |
| **Change plan** | Upgrade or downgrade. Pro-rated immediately. |
| **Cancel subscription** | Cancels at end of current billing period. Access continues until then. |
| **Download invoices** | Tax invoices for every past charge. |
| **Update billing details** | Name, address on invoice, etc. |
| **Pause / resume** | (If enabled by StemCounts admin — not always available.) |

## Cancelling

Cancelling does **not** instantly terminate access. Your subscription
stays active until the end of the current billing period — typically
a month from your last charge.

<Steps>

1. Open the Stripe portal (see above).

2. Click **Cancel subscription**.

3. Stripe asks for a reason (optional, helpful feedback).

4. Confirm. You see a cancellation date — that's when access ends.

</Steps>

After the cancellation date, you'll hit the [Trial and blocked
access](/billing/trial-and-blocked-access/) screen on next login.

## Restarting a cancelled subscription

Before the cancellation date, you can **reactivate** in the same Stripe
portal — one click and you're back on the same plan.

After the cancellation date, you start fresh:

1. Log back in to StemCounts (you'll see the Subscription Access
   Blocked screen)
2. Click **Subscribe** to go through Stripe Checkout again

Your historical data (orders, events, settings) is preserved.

## Failed payments

Stripe attempts each charge multiple times if your card fails. You'll
receive emails after the first failure. While in **Past Due** status:

- Access continues for a short grace period (set by Stripe's smart
  retry logic, typically a few days)
- A banner in the app prompts you to update your card

Update your card in the portal as soon as you can — if Stripe gives up
retrying, the subscription cancels and you're blocked.

## Changing plans mid-cycle

In the portal, click **Change plan** and pick another tier. Stripe
pro-rates:

- Upgrade → you pay the difference for the rest of the period
- Downgrade → unused credit applies to the next invoice

<Aside type="note">
For custom plans (enterprise, special pricing), Stripe portal options
may not match. Email **support@stemcounts.com** to discuss.
</Aside>
