Your business details live in **Settings** under **Your Organisation**.
This is the record your whole business shares: everyone in the
organisation sees the same details, and a change by one person applies
to all.

<HelpMedia id="settings-organization-tab" caption="Your Organisation in Settings: the Business group with Name, Logo, ABN, Order prefix and Retail markup, then Where you are" />

Like **Your Profile**, it is a list of rows. Click a row to open a
sheet for that one thing and save it. A row that is empty shows **Add**,
**Upload** or **Choose**.

## What is on the page

| Group | Rows |
|---|---|
| **Business** | **Name**, **Logo**, **ABN**, **Order prefix**, **Retail markup** |
| **Where you are** | **Address**, **Based in** |
| **Business type** | **Florist** and **Wholesaler** switches |
| **Areas you trade in** (wholesalers) | **Trading areas** |
| **Contact and social** | **Website**, **Phone**, **Contact email**, **Best way to reach you**, and your social handles. All optional. |
| **Subscription** (florists) | See [Your StemCounts subscription](/billing/subscription/) |

## Logo

Click **Logo** (**Upload** when empty, **Change** once set) and choose an
image file. To take it off, click **Remove logo** and confirm **Remove
your logo?**. Your organisation shows without a logo until you upload a
new one.

## ABN

Click **ABN** (**Add** when empty). The sheet says "Find your business on
the ABN register and pick it." Search by business name or number, pick
the right entry and save. A mistyped number is refused. Your ABN is
shown to people choosing your organisation at sign-up, so they pick the
right business.

## Order prefix

The order prefix is "The letters at the start of your order numbers,
e.g. BLOOM-001." It is set automatically when the organisation is
created, from the first three letters of the business name, with a
number added if those letters are already taken.

Most people see it as a plain row with the note "Your reference prefix
is set by your organisation admin." To change yours, email
**support@stemcounts.com**.

## Retail markup

Florists only. **Retail markup** is the multiplier applied to wholesale
costs when pricing for your customers, shown as, for example, **3 x
cost**. The sheet explains: "Example: 3 x $10 = $30." Type a
**Multiplier** of 1 or more. Values snap to the nearest 0.25 (3, 3.25,
3.5) and the hint shows exactly what will be saved. Anyone in the
organisation can change it.

See [Pricing and budgets](/florists/pricing-and-budgets/) for how the
markup flows into customer prices and **Revenue This Week**.

## Address and where you are based

Click **Address** to open **Business address**, type and pick your
address. **Based in** is "Filled in from your address postcode. Change
it if that is wrong." It is the area your business operates from.
Florists see the wholesalers that trade in the area they are based in.

## Areas you trade in (wholesalers)

Wholesalers also set **Trading areas**. "Florists only see wholesalers
that trade where they are based. Leave it empty to be visible
everywhere." You can tick a whole state or country, or pick individual
regions and cities.

## Business type

"What this company does on StemCounts. Turn on both if you buy as a
florist and sell as a wholesaler." **Florist** means the company "Buys
flowers and sends orders to wholesalers." **Wholesaler** means it "Sells
flowers and receives orders from florists." Each switch saves when you
flip it.

<Aside type="note">
Business type describes the company. Your own login role (florist or
wholesaler) was set when you signed up and is changed only by the
StemCounts team.
</Aside>

## Team members and roles

There is no invite button. Each person creates their own account and,
in the **Your Organisation** step of sign-up, searches for your business
and picks it. Make sure they pick the existing organisation rather than
creating a new one with a similar name. If someone has joined the wrong
organisation, email **support@stemcounts.com**.

Each person has one role, **Florist** or **Wholesaler**, shown under
their name in the top-right menu. There is no screen for your own
organisation to change people's roles; ask **support@stemcounts.com**.
