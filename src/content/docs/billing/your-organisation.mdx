Organisation settings live in **Settings → Organization**. These are
the details about your business — separate from your personal profile.

<HelpMedia id="settings-organization-tab" caption="Organisation settings" />

## Fields

| Field | What it's for | Editable by |
|---|---|---|
| **Company name** | Display on invoices, pick-lists, communications | Anyone in the org |
| **ABN** | Tax identifier (Australia) | Anyone in the org |
| **Address** | Billing and delivery; uses Google Places lookup | Anyone in the org |
| **Reference prefix** | Customises your order reference numbers (e.g. `SMITH-1234` instead of `F-1234`) | Admin only |
| **Retail markup (%)** | The % you mark up wholesale → customer price | Admin only |

## Reference prefix

By default, orders get a generic prefix. Setting your own makes references
recognisable in correspondence and on printed sheets.

Example:
- Default: `F-1234`
- With prefix `SMITH`: `SMITH-1234`

The prefix only applies to **new** orders. Existing orders keep whatever
reference they were created with.

## Retail markup

Florists only. Used to calculate customer-side pricing from wholesale
cost.

- A markup of **200%** means wholesale × 2 = customer price
- A markup of **250%** means wholesale × 2.5 = customer price
- Australian florists often use 240–300% depending on segment

See [Pricing and budgets](/florists/pricing-and-budgets/) for how the
math flows into arrangement budgets and the **Revenue This Week** metric.

<Aside type="caution">
Changing retail markup affects **all open quotes and budgets** in real
time. Sent orders are not retroactively recalculated — they keep the
markup that was in force when they were sent.
</Aside>

## Google Places address lookup

The Address field uses Google Places to autocomplete. Start typing
your street address and pick the right match — this ensures
postcodes, suburbs, and coordinates are accurate.

If Google Places isn't finding your address, manually type the full
address. You can edit it freely after the auto-complete fills the field.

## Roles and edit permissions

Only **admin** and **organisationadmin** roles can edit reference prefix
and retail markup. Other users see those fields as read-only.

To promote a user to admin: **Settings → Admin** (requires existing
admin access). The toast you'll see: **"User role updated to [role]
successfully!"**

## Multi-user organisations

If multiple staff use the same StemCounts organisation, they all share
this organisation record. Changes propagate immediately for all signed-in
users.

To add a team member, they sign up with **the same organisation name**
during registration. Currently there's no admin invite flow — new
users self-register and get matched to your org by name.
