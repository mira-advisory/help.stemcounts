When a florist's trial ends without an active subscription, StemCounts
shows a **hard-block screen** until access is restored. This article
explains what you see and how to fix it.

<HelpMedia id="subscription-blocked-screen" caption="The Subscription Access Blocked screen" />

## What you see

After your trial ends (or if your subscription is cancelled), logging in
takes you straight to:

> **Your florist access is currently paused.**
>
> Your organisation does not currently have an active StemCounts
> subscription. Go to your account page to review your subscription
> status and restart access.

You can navigate to the **Account** page from this screen, but no other
pages are accessible. The full app is hidden until subscription is
restored.

## The trial countdown

While the trial is still active, you instead see a **sticky banner**
at the top of every page:

> **You have X days left in your trial.** [Subscribe to StemCounts →]

The number ticks down daily. When it reaches zero, the banner is
replaced by the blocked screen above on your next page load.

## Restoring access

<Steps>

1. Click through to **Account** from the blocked screen.

2. The Account page loads with the **Subscription** card.

3. Click **Subscribe** to start Stripe checkout (or **Reactivate** if
   you're returning from a cancelled state).

4. Complete payment in Stripe. You're redirected back to StemCounts.

5. Refresh once — the blocked screen is replaced by the normal florist
   dashboard.

</Steps>

See [Your StemCounts subscription](/billing/subscription/) for the full
flow.

## What's still accessible while blocked

| Page | Accessible during block? |
|---|---|
| Sign in / sign out | Yes |
| Account page | Yes |
| Reset password | Yes |
| Orders, events, catalog | **No** |
| Printable event sheets (existing) | No |

Your **data is preserved** — orders, events, history, settings all remain
intact. You see them again as soon as access is restored.

## Why we gate access at the trial end (not earlier)

The trial is full-featured and unrestricted. We don't downgrade
gradually — full access for 14 days, then full block. This makes it
easy to evaluate during the trial without surprise restrictions, and
clear when payment is required.

<Aside type="tip">
Add a calendar reminder for around day 12 of the trial. Subscribing
before the trial ends is seamless — Stripe charges you on the trial
end date so you don't lose any trial time.
</Aside>

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| You subscribed but still see the blocked screen | Hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R / Cmd+Shift+R). If still blocked after 1 minute, email support. |
| "No subscription found for your organisation yet" | Stripe didn't sync; email support@stemcounts.com with your email + Stripe receipt. |
| You believe you're still on trial but see the block | Email support — the trial calculation may be off. |
| You want a trial extension | Contact your StemCounts account contact directly. Trial extensions are case-by-case. |
