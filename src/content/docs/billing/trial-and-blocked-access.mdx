When a florist organisation's trial ends without a subscription,
StemCounts replaces the florist pages with a **paused** screen until
access is restored. This page explains what you see and how to fix it.

## The trial countdown

While the trial is running, a banner sits under the header on every
page:

> **X days left in your trial**
> Subscribe now to keep full access to StemCounts after your trial ends.

with a **Subscribe to StemCounts** button. The number counts down daily.
The trial is 7 days, so if you want to keep going without a pause, a
good moment to subscribe is around day 5.

## What you see when it ends

Florist pages are replaced by a screen reading:

> **Subscription required**
>
> **Your florist access is currently paused**
>
> Your organisation does not currently have an active StemCounts
> subscription. Go to your subscription settings to review your status
> and restart access.

with a **Go to Subscription** button. Florist ordering features are
refused until a subscription is active. Wholesalers are never affected.

Your **data is kept**. Orders, events, history and settings all remain
exactly as they were, and you see them again as soon as access is
restored.

## Restoring access

<Steps>

1. Click **Go to Subscription** on the paused screen. It opens the
   **Subscription** section of **Your Organisation**, which stays
   available while you are paused.

2. Click **Choose** on the **Plan** row and pick **6 months** or **12
   months**.

3. Click the subscribe button and complete payment on the Stripe
   checkout page.

4. You return to **Your Organisation** with "Subscription successful.
   Your subscription is active." and the app is open again.

</Steps>

See [Your StemCounts subscription](/billing/subscription/) for the full
flow.

## Why access pauses at the end of the trial, not earlier

The trial is full-featured and unrestricted. There is no gradual
downgrade: full access for 7 days, then a pause. That makes it easy to
evaluate during the trial, and clear when payment is required.

## Trial extensions

Only the StemCounts team can extend a trial. To ask, email
**support@stemcounts.com** with your organisation name.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| You subscribed but still see the paused screen | Reload the page. If it is still there after a minute, email support@stemcounts.com. |
| The status reads **No subscription** and you have already paid | Email support@stemcounts.com with your email address and your receipt. |
| You believe you are still on trial but see the pause | The trial belongs to the organisation and started when it was created, not when you joined. If that does not explain it, email support@stemcounts.com. |
| A renewal payment failed | You have 7 days to update your card before access pauses. See [Managing your subscription](/billing/managing-subscription/). |
