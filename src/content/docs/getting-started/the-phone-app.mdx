StemCounts works on your phone as well as on a computer. There is
nothing to download from an app store. Open it in your phone's browser,
or install it on your home screen and it opens full screen like an
app, with its own icon and no browser bar.

## Install it on your home screen

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="iPhone">
    <Steps>

    1. Open **app.stemcounts.com** in Safari.

    2. Tap **Share**.

    3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**.

    </Steps>
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Android">
    <Steps>

    1. Open **app.stemcounts.com** in Chrome.

    2. Tap the three dots menu.

    3. Tap **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).

    </Steps>
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

Sign in once and it remembers you. It needs an internet connection, as
it does not work offline. It updates itself: when a new version is
ready you see a **Reload now** bar, see [What's new and
updates](/getting-started/whats-new-and-updates/).

## The bar along the bottom

On a phone there is no menu button. A bar along the bottom of the
screen takes you anywhere in one tap.

| Florists | Wholesalers |
|---|---|
| **Home** | **Home** |
| **Orders** | **Orders** |
| **New order** (the raised **+** button) | **Library** |
| **Search** | **Search** |
| **Chat** | **Chat** |

<HelpMedia id="phone-shell-phone-01-home" caption="Home on a phone: the tab bar along the bottom has Home, Orders, the raised New order, Search and Chat." />

- Inside an order, an event or the Flower Picker, **Home** becomes
  **Back** and says where it goes, for example "Back to STM-0189".
- On some screens the raised button does that screen's main job
  instead, for example **Add event** on an order.
- **Search** opens a sheet headed **Search**. Florists can find an
  order or an event by name, order number or week; wholesalers can find
  an order by event, florist, order number or week. Type "week 40" to
  jump to a week.

- **Chat** opens the same StemCounts chat as the round button on a

## Your initials at the top right

Tap your initials at the top right of the screen to open your account
sheet. It is headed with your name and role and holds **Settings**,
**What's new** with your version beside it, and **Sign out**.

## The home page on a phone

Florists see **Welcome** with your name and business, this week's three
figures (**Open Drafts**, **Events This Week**, **Revenue This Week**) as
tiles, a large **New order** button, then a list of rows: **Orders**,
**Invoices**, **Explore Flowers**, **Settings** and **Help guides**.
Wholesalers see their figures, then rows for **Order Board**,
**Library**, **Invoices**, **Settings** and **Help guides**.

## How screens work on a phone

- The soft painting of tulips behind the top of each page is the same
  one you see on the sign-in screen. It fades out before the content
  starts.
- Saved values read as rows. Tap a row and a sheet slides up from the
  bottom to change that one thing. It saves when you are done.
- On and off settings are switches in the row.
- Choosing from a list opens a sheet of options with a tick beside the
  one you have picked. Long lists have a search box at the top.
- On a phone, sections are swipeable pill tabs rather than a sidebar.
