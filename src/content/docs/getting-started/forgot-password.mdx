Locked out? It takes about a minute to get back in.

## Reset your password

<Steps>

1. On the **Sign in** screen, click **Forgot password?** under the
   password field.

   <HelpMedia id="login-forgot-password-link" />

2. Enter the email address linked to your account and click **Send Reset Link**.

   You'll see this message either way:

   > If an account with that email exists, you will receive password
   > reset instructions.

   <Aside type="tip">
   This message looks the same whether the email is on file or not — that's
   intentional, to avoid leaking which emails have accounts.
   </Aside>

3. Check your email for the reset link. Click it.

4. On the **Reset password** page, choose a new password and confirm it.

   <HelpMedia id="reset-password-form" />

   - Minimum 8 characters
   - Both fields must match — you'll see **"Passwords do not match."** if
     they don't

5. Click **Reset Password**. You'll see **"Password reset successfully!
   Redirecting to login..."** and be sent to the sign-in screen after 2
   seconds.

</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| "Invalid or missing reset token" | The link in the email is broken or you copy-pasted only part of it. Request a fresh reset link. |
| "The token may be invalid or expired" | Reset links expire after a short window. Request a new one. |
| Email never arrives | Check spam. If still missing, the email may not be on the account — try the address you registered with. |
| You don't remember which email you used | Email **support@stemcounts.com** with your phone number and organisation name. |
