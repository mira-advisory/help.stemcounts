Locked out? It takes about a minute to get back in.

## Reset your password

<Steps>

1. On the **Sign In** screen, click **Forgot password?** under the
   password field.

   <HelpMedia id="login-forgot-password-link" caption="The Sign In screen, with Forgot password? under the password field" />

2. Enter the email address on your account and click **Send Reset Link**.

   You see this message either way:

   > If an account with that email exists, you will receive password
   > reset instructions.

   <Aside type="tip">
   The message looks the same whether or not the email is on file. That
   is deliberate, so nobody can use it to find out which emails have
   accounts.
   </Aside>

3. Check your email for the reset link and click it. The link works for
   one hour and only once.

4. On the **Reset Password** page, choose a new password and confirm it.

   <HelpMedia id="reset-password-form" caption="The Reset Password page, with the new password entered twice" />

   - At least 8 characters
   - Both fields must match, or you see **"Passwords do not match."**

5. Click **Reset Password**. You see **"Password reset successfully!
   Redirecting to login..."** and land on the sign-in screen.

</Steps>

## Already signed in?

You can change your password without a reset link. Open **Settings**,
then **Your Profile**, and click **Password** under **Security**. See
[Profile and notifications](/billing/profile-and-notifications/).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| "Invalid or missing reset token. Please request a new password reset link." | The link is broken or you copied only part of it. Request a fresh one. |
| "This reset link is invalid or has expired. Please request a new one." | Links last one hour and work once. Go back to **Forgot password?** and use the newest email. |
| Email never arrives | Check spam. If it is still missing, the address may not be the one on your account. Try the one you signed up with. |
| You cannot remember which email you used | Email **support@stemcounts.com** with your phone number and organisation name. The team can also send you a reset link. |
