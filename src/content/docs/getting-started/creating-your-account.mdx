Signing up takes a few minutes: **pick your role**, fill in **Your
details**, choose **Your Organisation**, then **verify your email**.

## Step by step

<Steps>

1. Go to **[app.stemcounts.com](https://app.stemcounts.com)** and click
   **Create an account** under the sign-in form.

2. Choose your role:

   - **Florist**: "Create and manage flower orders."
   - **Wholesaler**: "Review and fulfil florist orders."

   <Tabs syncKey="device">
     <TabItem label="Computer">
       <HelpMedia id="signup-desktop-01-role-picker" caption="Choose Florist or Wholesaler to start creating your account." />
     </TabItem>
     <TabItem label="Phone">
       <HelpMedia id="signup-phone-01-role-picker" caption="Choose Florist or Wholesaler to start creating your account." />
     </TabItem>
   </Tabs>

   <Aside type="caution">
   Only the StemCounts team can change your role later. Pick the one
   that matches your business. Already have an account? Click **Oops I
   have an account** to go back to sign in.
   </Aside>

3. Fill in **Your details**:

   - **First Name** and **Last Name**
   - **Email**: this is how you sign in
   - **Phone Number**: the country picker defaults to Australia (+61),
     and a leading 0 is dropped for you
   - **Password** and **Confirm Password**

   Click **Continue →**. If you picked the wrong role, **Back to Role
   Selection** takes you back a step.

   <Tabs syncKey="device">
     <TabItem label="Computer">
       <HelpMedia id="signup-desktop-02-your-details" caption="Step 1, Your details: your name, email, mobile number and a password, then Continue." />
     </TabItem>
     <TabItem label="Phone">
       <HelpMedia id="signup-phone-02-your-details" caption="Step 1, Your details: your name, email, mobile number and a password, then Continue." />
     </TabItem>
   </Tabs>

4. Choose **Your Organisation**. Type at least two letters of your
   business name into **Search for your organisation…**.

   - If your business is already on StemCounts, pick it from the list.
     You will see "Joining existing organisation" with the current
     member count. Everyone in the same organisation shares its business
     details and its subscription.
   - If it is not there, choose **Create "[name]" as a new
     organisation**. A new organisation also asks for a **PayID Email**,
     the business email payers will use to send money to your
     organisation. Click **Use my email** to reuse your sign-up email,
     or **Use a different email**.

   Click **Create Account**.

   <Tabs syncKey="device">
     <TabItem label="Computer">
       <HelpMedia id="signup-desktop-03-your-organisation-search" caption="Step 2, Your Organisation: type your business name under Organisation Name and pick it from the list if it is already on StemCounts." />
       <HelpMedia id="signup-desktop-04-your-organisation-create-new" caption="Not listed? Choose Create ... as a new organisation, then set the PayID Email payers will use, and press Create Account." />
     </TabItem>
     <TabItem label="Phone">
       <HelpMedia id="signup-phone-03-your-organisation-search" caption="Step 2, Your Organisation: type your business name under Organisation Name and pick it from the list if it is already on StemCounts." />
       <HelpMedia id="signup-phone-04-your-organisation-create-new" caption="Not listed? Choose Create ... as a new organisation, then set the PayID Email payers will use, and press Create Account." />
     </TabItem>
   </Tabs>

5. Verify your email. The **Verify Your Account** screen says "Enter
   the 6-digit code we emailed to" your address. Type the code into
   **Verification code** and click **Verify**.

   <Tabs syncKey="device">
     <TabItem label="Computer">
       <HelpMedia id="signup-desktop-05-verify-code" caption="Verify Your Account: enter the 6 digit code we emailed you and press Verify. Resend code sends another." />
     </TabItem>
     <TabItem label="Phone">
       <HelpMedia id="signup-phone-05-verify-code" caption="Verify Your Account: enter the 6 digit code we emailed you and press Verify. Resend code sends another." />
     </TabItem>
   </Tabs>

   Nothing arrived? Check your spam folder, then click **Resend code**.

6. On success you see **"Registration successful! Logging you in..."**
   and you are signed in.

</Steps>

## Password rules

Use at least 8 characters, with an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter
and a number. Adding a symbol is a good idea too. If something is
missing, the form says "Password must contain:" followed by what is
missing, and "Passwords do not match" if the two entries differ.

## What happens next

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Florist">
    You land on the florist home page with your **7-day free trial**
    running. A banner across the top shows how many days are left until
    you subscribe. See [Your StemCounts subscription](/billing/subscription/)
    and [The florist landing page](/florists/florist-landing/).
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Wholesaler">
    You land on the wholesaler home page with tiles for **Order Summary**,
    **Order Board**, **Invoices**, **Inventory Management**, **Settings**
    and **Help guides**. The board starts empty and fills as florists
    send you orders. See [The wholesaler landing page](/wholesalers/wholesaler-landing/).
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Signing in

Enter your **Email** and **Password** on the **Sign In** screen and
click **Sign In**. To sign out, open the menu under your name at the
top right and click **Sign Out**.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| "Invalid email or password" | Check both. This message also appears if the account was never verified with the emailed code. Try **Forgot password?**, and if you never finished verification, email support@stemcounts.com. |
| The code never arrives | Check spam, then click **Resend code**. Make sure the email shown on the screen is yours. |
| "That code is not correct. Please check and try again." | Type the newest code you were sent. |
| "That code has expired. Please request a new one." | Click **Resend code** and use the new one. |
| "Too many attempts. Please wait a few minutes and try again." | Wait a few minutes, then try once more. |
| Your teammate created a second organisation with your business name | Email support@stemcounts.com and the team will move them across. |
