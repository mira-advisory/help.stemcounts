Signing up is a three-step process: **pick your role**, **fill in your
details**, **verify your phone**.

<HelpMedia id="signup-flow-overview" />

## Step-by-step

<Steps>

1. Go to **[app.stemcounts.com](https://app.stemcounts.com)** and click
   **Sign up**.

2. Choose your role:

   - **Florist** — "Create and manage flower orders"
   - **Wholesaler** — "Review and fulfil florist orders"

   <HelpMedia id="signup-role-selection" caption="Florist vs Wholesaler role picker" />

   <Aside type="caution">
   You can't change this later without contacting support. Pick the role
   that matches your business.
   </Aside>

3. Fill in the form:

   - First name, last name
   - **Organisation name** — your business or shop name
   - Email and phone number (Australian numbers default to +61)
   - Password — minimum 8 characters, must include an uppercase letter,
     a lowercase letter, and a number

4. Click **Create Account**. You'll see "Creating Account..." briefly,
   then be redirected to the OTP verification screen.

5. Check your phone for a **6-digit code** and enter it.

   <HelpMedia id="signup-otp-screen" caption="OTP entry — the code arrives by SMS to the phone you registered with" />

   - Didn't receive it? Click **Resend OTP**.
   - The code is single-use and expires after a few minutes.

6. On success you'll see **"Registration successful! Logging you in..."**
   and be redirected to your landing page after about 2 seconds.

</Steps>

## What happens next

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Florist">
    You land on the **florist dashboard** with your **14-day free trial**
    activated. You'll see a trial countdown banner at the top of the app
    until you subscribe. See [Your StemCounts subscription](/billing/subscription/)
    for next steps.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Wholesaler">
    You land on the **wholesaler dashboard** showing the Order Board and
    Order Summary. The Order Board starts empty — orders arrive as
    florists send them. See [The wholesaler landing page](/wholesalers/wholesaler-landing/).
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| "Account not verified. Please check your phone for OTP." | Go to OTP verify directly via the link in the error, or request a new code. |
| OTP never arrives | Wait 60 seconds, then click **Resend OTP**. If still nothing, check your number is correct on the previous screen. |
| "First name is required" / "Passwords do not match" | Inline form validation. The red message points at the field with the problem. |
| Password rejected | Must be 8+ chars, with uppercase, lowercase, and a number. |
