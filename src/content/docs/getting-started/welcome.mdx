**StemCounts is a flower-ordering platform that connects florists with
their wholesalers.** It's two-sided — one product, two audiences.

<HelpMedia id="welcome-platform-overview" />

## The two-sided picture

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Florists">
    Build event-based orders. Browse a live catalog, design arrangements,
    and send a single weekly order to your wholesaler. Track each flower
    line through fulfilment.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Wholesalers">
    Receive florist orders, aggregate the stems you need across all your
    customers, assign suppliers, and mark items ready for pickup or
    delivery — all on a Kanban board.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Event-based, not cart-based

StemCounts isn't a typical online shop. Florists don't add items to a
cart; they create **events** — a wedding, a corporate gig, a funeral —
on a specific date. Each event holds **arrangements** (designs), and
each arrangement holds **stems**. Orders are grouped by week, so a
single weekly order can contain multiple events.

That structure flows through to the wholesaler side: the Order Board
sorts incoming orders by delivery week, and stems are aggregated across
florist orders before being assigned to suppliers.

## What you need to get started

<Aside type="note">
StemCounts is a paid service for florists, with a free trial. Wholesalers
on the platform do not need a subscription. See [Your StemCounts
subscription](/billing/subscription/) for details.
</Aside>

- A modern browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox — latest versions)
- A phone number for OTP verification
- For florists: a debit/credit card for subscription after the trial
- For wholesalers: an invite or self-registration as your wholesaler role
