**StemCounts is a flower-ordering platform that connects florists with
their wholesalers.** Two sides, one product — built so each can
quietly do their job without stepping on the other's toes.

## Events, not shopping carts

StemCounts isn't a typical online shop. Florists don't drop items in
a cart; they create **events** — a wedding, a corporate gig, a funeral
service — on a specific date. Each event holds **arrangements** (your
designs), and each arrangement holds **stems**. Orders are grouped by
week, so one weekly order can contain several events.

That same structure carries through to the wholesaler side. The Order
Board sorts incoming orders by delivery week, then groups stems across
florists so you can source efficiently from your suppliers.

## What you'll need

<Aside type="note">
StemCounts is a paid service for florists, with a free trial to start.
Wholesalers don't pay a subscription. Full details are in
[Your StemCounts subscription](/billing/subscription/).
</Aside>

- A modern browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, or Firefox — latest version)
- A phone number for the verification code we'll send when you sign up
- For florists: a debit or credit card for the subscription after your trial ends
- For wholesalers: an invite from your StemCounts contact, or sign up directly as a wholesaler
