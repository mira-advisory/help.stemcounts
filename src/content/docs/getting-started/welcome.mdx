**StemCounts is a flower ordering platform that connects florists with
their wholesalers.** Two sides, one product. Each side does its own job
without getting in the other's way.

## Florists and wholesalers, side by side

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Florists">
    Build orders around your real events. Choose flowers from the
    catalogue, design your arrangements, then send one weekly order to
    your wholesaler. Follow each flower as the week unfolds.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Wholesalers">
    Receive florist orders, accept them, confirm what you can supply,
    and mark them ready and delivered. Everything sits on one order
    board.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Events, not shopping carts

StemCounts is not a typical online shop. Florists do not drop items in
a cart. They create **events** (a wedding, a corporate job, a funeral
service) on a date. Each event holds **arrangements** (your designs),
and each arrangement holds **stems**. Orders are grouped by week, so
one weekly order can hold several events. If you only need flowers by
the bunch, a **Bunch Order** does that without an event.

The same structure carries through to the wholesaler side. Orders
arrive by delivery week, so stems can be sourced efficiently.

## What you will need

<Aside type="note">
StemCounts is a paid service for florists, with a free 7-day trial to
start. Wholesalers do not pay a subscription. Full details are in
[Your StemCounts subscription](/billing/subscription/).
</Aside>

- An up-to-date browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox). There is
  nothing to download. On a phone you can add StemCounts to your home
  screen, see [StemCounts on your phone](/getting-started/the-phone-app/).
- An email address you can check. The verification code at sign-up is
  emailed to you, and your email is how you sign in.
- For florists: a debit or credit card for the subscription after the
  trial ends.
- For wholesalers: nothing more. Sign up as a wholesaler and your
  florists' orders come to you. Some wholesalers receive orders by
  email and a link, and do not need to sign in at all.
