Brand new to StemCounts? Here's the shortest path from logging in to a
sent order. About 10 minutes start to finish.

<Aside type="tip">
This walkthrough is florist-focused. If you signed up as a wholesaler,
read [The wholesaler landing page](/wholesalers/wholesaler-landing/) instead.
</Aside>

## The shortest happy path

<Steps>

1. **Log in** at [app.stemcounts.com](https://app.stemcounts.com).

   You'll land on the florist dashboard. Three metrics at the top:
   *Open Drafts*, *Events This Week*, *Revenue This Week*.

2. Click **New Order**.

   <HelpMedia id="florist-landing-new-order-cta" />

   This opens the Add Event modal.

3. Fill in:

   - **Event Name** — e.g. "Smith Wedding"
   - **Event Date** — the day of the actual event (not the delivery date)
   - **Event Location** — venue or address

   Click **Save**. StemCounts will either add this event to an existing
   weekly order (if you have a draft for that week) or create a new
   draft.

4. Click **Add Arrangement** on the event detail page.

   <HelpMedia id="event-add-arrangement" />

   Give the arrangement a **name** (e.g. "Bridal bouquet") and a
   **budget** in customer price excluding GST.

5. Click **Pick Flowers** inside your new arrangement.

   The Flower Picker opens. Use the **Category**, **Colour**, **Season**
   filters or search. For each flower you want, choose a colour variant
   and set the quantity in **stems**.

   <HelpMedia id="flower-picker-quantity" />

   Click **Done** when you've finished.

6. (Optional) Repeat steps 4–5 for any additional arrangements in the
   same event — ceremony, reception, cake florals, etc.

7. (Optional) Add another event to the same weekly order with **Add
   another event** on the order details page.

8. Send the order.

   When everything looks right, send the weekly order to your wholesaler.
   The order transitions from **Draft** to **Sent** and lands on their
   Receive & Accept board.

   <HelpMedia id="order-send-confirmation" />

</Steps>

## What happens next

Once sent, your order moves through fulfilment. You'll see the status
change in the Orders list:

| Status | Means |
|---|---|
| **Sent** | Your wholesaler has the order; not yet accepted |
| **Accepted** | They've accepted; they're sourcing the stems |
| **Supply Confirmed** | All flowers confirmed by suppliers |
| **Ready** | Flowers are ready for pickup or delivery |
| **Complete** | Delivered. Payment captured. |

Full detail on tracking is in [Tracking your order](/florists/tracking-your-order/).
