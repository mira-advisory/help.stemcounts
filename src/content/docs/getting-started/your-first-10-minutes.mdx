Brand new to StemCounts? Here is the shortest path from signing in to a
sent order. About 10 minutes start to finish.

<Aside type="tip">
This walkthrough is for florists. If you signed up as a wholesaler,
read [The wholesaler landing page](/wholesalers/wholesaler-landing/) instead.
</Aside>

## The shortest path

<Steps>

1. **Sign in** at [app.stemcounts.com](https://app.stemcounts.com).

   You land on the florist home page. Three figures sit at the top:
   **Open Drafts**, **Events This Week** and **Revenue This Week**.
   The first time, StemCounts may ask a few questions about your
   business before you get there: see [The first time you sign
   in](#the-first-time-you-sign-in).

2. Click **New Order**.

   <HelpMedia id="florist-landing-new-order-cta" caption="The New Order card on the florist home page" />

   The **Add Event to Order** window opens with two pages: **Event
   Order** (a named event, on a date, at a venue) and **Bunch Order**
   (flowers by the bunch, no event). Stay on **Event Order**.

3. Fill in the event:

   - **Name**, for example "Smith Wedding"
   - **Event Date**: the day of the event itself
   - **Location**: the venue or address
   - **Delivery / Pickup Date**: when you want the flowers
   - **Preferred wholesaler** (optional)

   Click **Add and Edit** to open the event straight away. StemCounts
   puts it into your draft weekly order for that week, or starts a new
   draft if there is none.

4. Click **Add Arrangement** on the event page.

   In **Add Arrangements**, give it a **Name** (for example "Bridal
   bouquet"), a **Qty** and a **Budget** each. "All budget values are Ex
   GST". Click **Add Arrangement**.

5. Click **Add Flowers** at the top of the event page. In the Flower
   Picker, search or filter by **Category**, **Colour** and **Season**,
   and turn on **Similar shades** to widen a colour. Pick the colours
   you want and click **Add Selections**. Back on the event page, set
   the stems for each flower on the arrangement card.

6. (Optional) Repeat steps 4 and 5 for the other arrangements in the
   event: ceremony, reception, cake flowers and so on.

7. (Optional) Add another event to the same week with **Add Event** on
   the order page.

8. Click **Add to Weekly Order** at the top of the event page. If a
   flower does not fill whole bunches, the page asks you to round up and
   place the spare stems first.

9. Send the order. In the order summary, click **Send Order**. The
   **Send Assigned Orders** window lists each wholesaler with a delivery
   date and how the flowers will get to you. Click **Send Order** to
   confirm. The order moves from **Draft** to **Sent**, and each
   wholesaler is told.

</Steps>

## The first time you sign in

After you sign in, StemCounts may show a short set of steps about your
business before you start, for example **Step 1 of 3: Your ABN**.

- **Your ABN** appears if your business has no ABN saved. Search the
  Australian Business Register by **Business name, ABN or ACN**, select
  your business and click **Save and continue**. You can click **Skip
  for now**, and you will be asked again in a later session.
- **Your details** asks for anything missing, such as **Business
  address**, **Phone**, **Contact email** (where order emails from
  wholesalers go), **Website** and **Best way to reach you**. Fill in
  what you can. Anything you skip is asked about again in ten days.

## What happens next

Your order moves through fulfilment. You see the status change on the
**Orders** page:

| Status | Means |
|---|---|
| **Sent** | Your wholesaler has the order and has not accepted it yet |
| **Accepted** | They have accepted it and are sourcing the stems |
| **Supply Confirmed** | They have confirmed what they can supply |
| **Ready** | Your wholesaler has packed the order |
| **Complete** | Delivered |

If a wholesaler cannot supply a flower, or offers a substitute or fewer
stems, the order asks you to respond. Full detail is in [Tracking your
order](/florists/tracking-your-order/).
