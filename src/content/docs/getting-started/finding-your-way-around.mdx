The same chrome wraps every page in StemCounts. Once you know what each
piece does, the app stops feeling new.

<HelpMedia id="app-shell-overview" kind="video" caption="A quick tour of the StemCounts header and navigation" />

## The header

The sticky header at the top of every page has three parts:

- **Left** — the StemCounts logo (clicking it sends you to your
  landing page) and the **Orders** dropdown
- **Middle** — search (planned)
- **Right** — your user avatar and menu

## The Orders dropdown

Both roles see an **Orders** dropdown in the header, but the items
differ a little. Both have three workflow stages:

- **Receive and Accept** — florists see incoming confirmations, wholesalers see new orders
- **Assign and Send** — florists see orders being processed, wholesalers see supplier assignment
- **Confirmed and Ready** — items ready for the next step

For full detail on these stages, see [Tracking your order](/florists/tracking-your-order/)
(florist view) or [The Order Board](/wholesalers/the-order-board/)
(wholesaler view).

## Your user menu

Click your **avatar circle** in the top-right to open the user menu.

<HelpMedia id="user-menu-open" />

Every user sees:

- **Settings** — your profile, organisation, notifications
- **Sign out**

Florists also see:

- **Account** — your subscription and billing

## Trial banner (florists only)

While you're on the free trial, a banner stays pinned at the top of the
page showing **"X days left in your trial"** with a **Subscribe to
StemCounts** button. The countdown updates daily. After the trial ends,
the banner is replaced by a hard block until you subscribe — see
[Trial and blocked access](/billing/trial-and-blocked-access/).
