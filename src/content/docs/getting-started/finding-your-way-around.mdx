The same frame wraps every page in StemCounts. Once you know what each
piece does, the app stops feeling new. This guide is the computer view.
On a phone the layout is different, see [StemCounts on your
phone](/getting-started/the-phone-app/).

<HelpMedia id="phone-shell-phone-01-home" caption="Home on a phone: the tab bar along the bottom has Home, Orders, the raised New order, Search and Chat." />

## The header

The header stays at the top of every page.

- **Left**: the StemCounts logo. Click it to go back to your home page.
- **Right**: any actions for the page you are on, the **Notifications**
  bell, and your name with your role under it. Click your name to open
  your menu.

## Your name menu

<HelpMedia id="user-menu-open" caption="The menu under your name at the top right: Settings, Sign Out, What's new and the version line, with the Notifications bell beside it" />

The menu has three items and a version line:

- **Settings**: your profile, your organisation, and the ordering
  screens for your role.
- **Sign Out**.
- **What's new**: the list of recent changes to StemCounts. The small
  grey line under it is the version you are running. If you ever need
  help, the team may ask for that number.

There is no separate Account page. Your subscription and billing live in
**Settings** under **Your Organisation**.

## Settings

Settings has a sidebar grouped by job. Everyone sees **You**, holding
**Your Profile** (your name, contact details and password) and **Your
Organisation** (your business details, trading areas, payment setup and,
for florists, the subscription). Under **Ordering**, florists see
**Favourite Arrangements** and **Explore Flowers**, and wholesalers see
**Suppliers**.

- [Profile and notifications](/billing/profile-and-notifications/)
- [Your organisation](/billing/your-organisation/)
- [Your StemCounts subscription](/billing/subscription/)

## Your home page

Florists land on the florist home page: three figures for the week and
tiles for **Orders**, **New Order**, **Invoices**, **Explore Flowers**,
**Settings** and **Help guides**. Wholesalers land on the wholesaler home
page with **Order Summary**, **Order Board**, **Invoices**, **Inventory
Management**, **Settings** and **Help guides**. **Help guides** opens this
site in a new tab.

Florists' **Orders** page has two views, **Calendar** and **Order
Board**, switched at the top of the page.

## The trial banner (florists only)

While your organisation is on its free trial, a banner sits under the
header on every page. It reads **"X days left in your trial"** with
"Subscribe now to keep full access to StemCounts after your trial
ends." and a **Subscribe to StemCounts** button, which takes you to the
**Subscription** section of **Your Organisation**. Once you subscribe,
the banner goes away. When the trial ends without a subscription, see
[Trial and paused access](/billing/trial-and-blocked-access/).

## The chat button

The round chat button at the bottom right of every page opens the
StemCounts chat. Its header reads **Chatting with Stems Bot**. Type your
question in your own words, for example "how do I round up bunches",
and it answers from these help guides. If it cannot help, it offers to
pass you to the team, and you can email **support@stemcounts.com** at any
time.

<Aside type="note">
Now and then, after you sign in, a small **Did you know?** card offers
one tip. **Show me** opens the matching guide, **Ask more** puts the
question to the chat, and **Stop tips at sign-in** turns the cards off.
See [What's new and updates](/getting-started/whats-new-and-updates/).
</Aside>
