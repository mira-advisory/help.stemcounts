StemCounts changes often. This page covers the three ways you hear
about it: the **What's new** page, the update bar, and the occasional
tip after you sign in.

## What's new

Open the menu under your name at the top right (on a phone, tap your
initials) and click **What's new**. The page is headed **Recent changes
to Stem Counts** and says which version you are running.

Each release is listed by date with one line per change, tagged
**New**, **Improved** or **Fixed**. Use the filters at the top,
**Everything**, **New**, **Improved** and **Fixed**, to narrow the list.

Your version also appears in small grey text under **What's new** in
the menu, as "Version" and a number. If you contact the team about a
problem, that number tells them exactly what you are running.

## The "Version ... is ready" bar

When a new version of StemCounts has been released while you have the
app open, a bar appears along the bottom of the screen reading, for
example, **"Version 580 is ready"** with a **Reload now** button.

- Click **Reload now** when it suits you, ideally after saving what you
  are working on. You get the latest version, and **What's new** lists
  the changes.
- Close the bar with the **X** if now is a bad time. The app never
  reloads by itself.
- Small bug-fix releases do not show the bar at all.

<Aside type="note">
Rarely, a major release shows a full-screen **Major release** notice
reading "Reload the page to carry on with the latest version of Stem
Counts." Its only option is **Reload now**.
</Aside>

## The "Did you know?" tip after sign-in

Now and then, after you sign in, a small **Did you know?** card appears
at the bottom right of the screen (on a phone, as a sheet above the
bottom bar). It answers one question about StemCounts, drawn from these
help guides. It appears on your first sign-in and then every third one,
never twice with the same tip, and it never covers the page or blocks
anything.

- **Show me** opens the matching help guide in a new tab.
- **Ask more** puts the question to the chat so you can follow up.
- The **X** closes it.

To stop the cards, click **Stop tips at sign-in** on the card, or open
**Settings**, **Your Profile**, and turn off the **Tips when you sign
in** switch under **Notifications**. Turning it back on brings them
back.
