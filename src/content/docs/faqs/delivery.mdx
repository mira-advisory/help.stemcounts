## How do I set delivery dates for my orders?

When creating an order, you'll specify the delivery date (If imported product is required this would require approximately 4 weeks lead time. Also, if large quantities are required 4 weeks prior to delivery date would most likely be required(Check with your wholesaler)

**Where:** `/order-details` in the app · **UI element:** Delivery Date Picker

## Can I track my order status?

Yes, you can track your order easily.
Just sign in to Stem Counts, open your sent order, and you'll see the status of every flower � from Accepted, to Confirmed, to Ready.
This gives you peace of mind and confidence that your flowers are on the way.

**Where:** `/orders` in the app · **UI element:** Order Status Tracker

## What if I need to change my delivery date?

If your order hasn't been confirmed yet, you can modify the delivery date in the order details. For confirmed orders, contact your wholesaler directly through the messaging feature to request changes. Changes may not be possible for orders already in preparation.

**Where:** `/order-details` in the app · **UI element:** Delivery Date Field

## How do I report delivery issues?

If there are quality issues, missing items, or other delivery problems, use the "Report Issue" button on the order page. Document the issue with photos and descriptions. Your wholesaler will be notified immediately and can arrange replacements or credits. | Check with your wholesaler the time frames required for reporting

**Where:** `/orders` in the app · **UI element:** Report Issue Button

## Can I consolidate multiple orders into one delivery?

Yes! When creating orders for the same week with the same delivery date, the system automatically groups them.

**Where:** `/orders` in the app · **UI element:** Order Grouping

## What are the delivery lead times?

Standard delivery lead time will depend on each individual wholesaler. Some specialty or seasonal flowers may require longer lead times (Usually 4 weeks before the date of delivery.This would include imported or interstate product) Rush delivery may be available - check with your wholesaler for availability and fees.

**Where:** `/orders` in the app · **UI element:** Lead Time Info
