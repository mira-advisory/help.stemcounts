These are the same finances-focused FAQ entries shown inside the StemCounts app under **FAQ** in the user menu. Each entry links to a step-by-step Scribehow walkthrough where one exists.

## How is flower pricing calculated?

Flower pricing is based on your wholesaler's rates. The prices in the Flower Library are an average indicator price for budgeting. | You can set markup percentages to automatically calculate customer pricing. The system tracks cost vs. selling price to show profit margins. Stem Counts has a small agent fee. This fee is already included in the Flower Picker which means the fee is passed on to the customer

**Where:** `/flower-library` in the app · **UI element:** Pricing System

## Where can I see my total spending for a week?

On the "Orders" page, each week displays total spending across all orders. | The Page "Weekly Order Details" shows you the total spend for that week | You can view spending by Customer vs. Wholesale and by individual order parts within the weekly summary by clicking on the Event name

**Where:** `/orders` in the app · **UI element:** Weekly Summary

## How do I track event budgets vs actual costs?

Each event order part has a budget field where you set the client's budget. As you add flowers and arrangements, the system shows spent amount, remaining budget, and profit margin. You'll see warnings if you're approaching or exceeding the budget.

**Where:** `/order-part-details` in the app · **UI element:** Budget Tracker

## Can I export financial reports?

Currently in development - however we will be able to export weekly summaries, order details, and spending reports. These reports include itemized flower costs, markup calculations, and profit margins for accounting and client invoicing purposes.

**Where:** `/orders` in the app · **UI element:** Export Feature

## What markup should I use for retail sales?

Industry standard retail markup for flowers is typically 2.5x to 3.5x wholesale cost (150%-250% markup). The system allows you to set custom markup percentages per order. The program has it automatically set at 200%

**Where:** `/settings` in the app · **UI element:** Markup Settings

## How do wholesale prices get updated?

Wholesalers update their pricing in the system, which automatically reflects in the flower library. Florists see current wholesale prices when building orders. Historical orders retain the pricing from when they were created.

**Where:** `/flower-library` in the app · **UI element:** Price Updates
