## What's the difference between Retail and Event orders?

Retail orders are for regular inventory for walk-in customers and daily sales. Event orders are for specific arrangements for weddings, corporate events, and special occasions. Each type has different workflows and pricing structures.

**Where:** `/orders` in the app · **UI element:** Order Type Selection

## What is the Spares Bucket?

The Spares Bucket tracks leftover stems, which are not rounded to a full bunch. Asflowers are sold in bunches (for example, 10 stems per bunch), but your designs might need odd numbers like 23 roses. | Since you cannot buy 2.3 bunches, you purchase 3 bunches (30 stems total). Your design uses 23 stems, and 7 stems automatically go into the Spares Bucket. Our Magic Calculator shows you these leftovers and suggests smart adjustments. | Example: You have 26 stems of Andromeda, and they come in bunches of 5. You can make 5 full bunches (5 × 5 = 25) with 1 stem left over, which goes into the Spares Bucket. | If you want to make 6 bunches, you would need 30 stems, so you are 4 stems short. | In simple terms: You can make 5 bunches with 1 leftover, or you are 4 stems short of making 6 bunches.

**Where:** `/order-part-details` in the app · **UI element:** Spares Bucket Section

## What happens after I send an order?

Once sent, your order goes to the wholesaler for review. You can track its status in the Orders page through these stages: Received, Accepted, Confirmed, Substitute and Ready for dispatch, Out for Delivery, Delivered. You'll receive notifications at each stage. Blue tiles and features indicate a SENT order.

**Where:** `/orders` in the app · **UI element:** Order Status Tracking

## Do I put the customer price or Wholesale price into the budget for an arrangement?

Always use the Customer price.

**Where:** `/order-part-details` in the app · **UI element:** Budget Field

## Should I include GST into my budget ?

No, exclude the GST amount - By keeping GST out of your arrangement costings, you make sure you don't accidentally spend the GST your customer pays you.
If you include GST in your budget, you'll end up using that money on flowers � but that GST actually needs to be put aside to pay the ATO.
Separating it keeps you safe and avoids surprises later.

**Where:** `/order-part-details` in the app · **UI element:** Budget Field

## Does the Budget Price include the GST?

No, exclude the GST amount. as above
