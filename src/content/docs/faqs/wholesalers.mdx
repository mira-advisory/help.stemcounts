## What does a wholesaler see after logging in?

The wholesaler home page opens with a welcome banner showing three counts: **New Orders** (orders waiting for you to act), **In Progress** and **Due This Week**. Below that are cards for **Order Summary**, **Order Board** and **Invoices**, then **Inventory Management**, **Settings** and **Help guides**, which opens the help site in a new tab. **Order Summary** and **Order Board** open the same orders page; **Order Board** simply starts it in the Board view. The **Inventory Management** card shows a small counter of how many flowers you have selected in your library against how many you have not. In the top menu, **Orders** takes you straight to the Order Summary.

**Where:** Home (wholesaler landing page)

[Read more](/wholesalers/wholesaler-landing/)

## What views does the Order Summary have?

The **Order Summary** page has three views you switch between at the top: **Calendar** (orders in a weekly planner), **Board** (orders grouped by workflow stage) and **List** (a searchable table with **Order Reference**, **Florist**, **Status**, **Amount** and **Delivery Date**). It opens in **Calendar** unless you arrived from the **Order Board** card. Every view has a search box for order reference plus **Florist** and **Status** filters; Board and List can also sort by **Delivery Date** or **Received Date**. Clicking an order in any view opens its order detail window.

**Where:** Orders > Order Summary

[Read more](/wholesalers/wholesaler-landing/)

## What is the calendar view for?

**Calendar** is the default view of the Order Summary. It shows your orders in the **Orders and Weekly Planner**, laid out by delivery date across a run of weeks (twelve by default), so you can see busy weeks and days coming. You can narrow it with the search box and the **Florist** and **Status** filters. Click any order in the planner to open its full detail, where you accept, confirm and complete it.

**Where:** Orders > Order Summary > Calendar

[Read more](/wholesalers/the-order-board/)

## What columns are on the Order Board?

The Board groups orders into columns, left to right in the order work happens. The full set is **New Orders** (awaiting your review), **Accepted** (accepted and moving into procurement), **Supply Confirmed** (supply is confirmed), **Supplier Fulfilled** (prepared for florist delivery or pickup) and **Completed**. Which columns you see depends on how your account is set up: a wholesaler that sources through suppliers in StemCounts sees all five, while a simpler set-up skips **Supplier Fulfilled**. Within each column, orders are grouped by delivery week, and each column shows a count.

**Where:** Orders > Order Summary > Board

[Read more](/wholesalers/the-order-board/)

## Can I drag orders between columns on the board?

Yes, but only one step forward at a time. Dragging to any other column shows **Drag to the next workflow stage to continue.** Dragging a single order to the next column opens that order's detail window, where you finish the step (for example **Accept Order**). You can also drag a whole week's group of orders at once. From **New Orders** to **Accepted** it walks you through each order to accept; from **Accepted** to **Supply Confirmed** it opens the week's **Accepted Workflow**; from **Supply Confirmed** to **Supplier Fulfilled** it opens **Mark Ready for Dispatch**; and from **Supplier Fulfilled** to **Completed** it completes every order in that week.

**Where:** Orders > Order Summary > Board

[Read more](/wholesalers/the-order-board/)

## How do I accept or reject a new order?

Open the order from the Calendar, Board or List. A new order shows **Review order details before accepting or rejecting.** with the pickup date, event name, florist, total, any florist note, and an **Inventory List** of every flower with stems, bunches and line cost. Click **Accept Order** to take it on, or **Reject Order** to decline it. You accept or reject the whole order here; if a single flower is a problem, accept and then use the flower's **Actions** or **Can't Supply** option. When you accept, the florist is notified and receives an email, and the order moves to **Accepted**.

**Where:** Orders > Order Summary > open an order in New Orders

[Read more](/wholesalers/accepting-an-order/)

## Can I accept an order straight from the email without logging in?

Yes. The order email includes a link that opens the order itself with no login. The page shows **New order from** the florist, with an **Accept this order** button and a **Log in to manage** button. Accepting there tells the florist you have taken the order on; once accepted, the page confirms **Accepted.** and that the florist has been told. The link can only be used to accept once. Quantities, substitutions and delivery are still managed in the app after you log in.

**Where:** New order email or text > order link

## What emails and notifications does a wholesaler get when an order arrives?

When a florist sends you an order, StemCounts emails it to your organisation's **Contact email** (set in **Settings > Your Organisation**), or to every active user in your organisation if no contact email is set. The florist who sent it is copied in, and replies go to that florist. Logged-in wholesalers also get an in-app notification, **New order received**. The email carries a link to view and accept the order, and a link to the platform authorisation letter. Text messages to the wholesaler about new orders are being rolled out separately and may not be switched on yet.

**Where:** Email, notification bell, text message

## How do I confirm supply as an integrated wholesaler?

After accepting, the order detail says **Confirm supply for each flower below. Flag anything you can't supply.** Each flower line in the **Inventory List** has a supplier dropdown (**Select supplier…**), listing the suppliers you have added in Settings, or **Not applicable**. Once a supplier is chosen, the line shows **Confirm** and **Can't Supply**. Use **Set supplier for all…** in the header to apply one supplier to every open line, and **Confirm All** to confirm them together. When every flower is confirmed, the order moves to **Supply Confirmed** automatically and the florist is notified.

**Where:** Order detail window > Inventory List (Accepted stage)

[Read more](/wholesalers/confirming-supply/)

## How does a non-integrated wholesaler confirm an order?

For a non-integrated wholesaler every flower is accepted with the order, so there is no per-flower confirm. The order detail says **Confirm this order when supply is locked in. Use Actions on a flower only if something changes.** Click **Confirm Order** to move it to **Supply Confirmed**. Then, once the florist has picked up or received the flowers, click **Mark Delivered**. If a flower is waiting on the florist's reply to an offer you made, the button reads **Awaiting Florist** until they respond.

**Where:** Order detail window (Accepted stage)

[Read more](/wholesalers/the-order-board/)

## What can I do if I cannot supply a flower?

Open **Can't Supply** (integrated) or **Actions** (non-integrated) on the flower line. You are asked **What would you like to offer for** that flower, with three choices: **Offer Substitutes** (pick up to five alternatives from flowers you have in stock, with quantities), **Partial Quantity** (enter how many stems you can supply, in whole bunches) or **Unavailable** (the flower is removed and the order total is reduced). You can add a **Note to florist (optional)**, then click **Send to Florist** or **Confirm Cancellation**. The florist gets an email about the change, and the line waits in **Awaiting Florist Response** until they reply.

**Where:** Order detail window > Inventory List > Can't Supply (integrated) or Actions (non-integrated)

## What happens after I offer a substitute or partial quantity?

The flower shows **Awaiting Florist** and sits under **Awaiting Florist Response**, with a note that there is nothing to do while the florist reviews your offer. On the florist's side the flower shows **Action Required** until they accept a substitute, accept the reduced quantity, or remove it. While any flower is waiting, you cannot move the order on: the confirm or delivered buttons read **Awaiting Florist**. Florists who accept fewer stems or a substitute pay for what is supplied, and a flower you cannot supply is not charged on the order.

**Where:** Order detail window > Inventory List > Awaiting Florist Response

## How do I assign suppliers for a whole week of orders?

Drag a week's group of orders from **Accepted** to **Supply Confirmed**. This opens the week's **Accepted Workflow**: **Select flower lines, assign suppliers, and confirm availability in one flow.** The **Flower Requirements** table combines each flower and colour across the week's orders, with **Flower**, **Colour**, **Stem / Bunches**, **Supplier** and **Status**. Tick lines (or **Select All**) and use **Set supplier for selected**, then set each line's status to confirmed. **Save and Close** keeps your progress; **Supply Confirmed** appears once every line has a supplier and is confirmed. The substitute status in this table is marked **Coming soon**.

**Where:** Orders > Order Summary > Board > drag a week from Accepted to Supply Confirmed

[Read more](/wholesalers/confirming-supply/)

## Does StemCounts send orders to my suppliers?

No. When you assign a supplier to a flower, StemCounts records which supplier that flower is coming from and groups the lines into a supplier order inside your account, but it does not email, text or otherwise send anything to the supplier. You place the order with your supplier the way you normally do, then come back and confirm the flower in StemCounts. The supplier name then shows against that flower line for the rest of the order.

**Where:** Settings > Suppliers; order detail window

[Read more](/wholesalers/confirming-supply/)

## How do I add or manage my suppliers?

Go to **Settings > Suppliers** ("The growers and suppliers your organisation buys from"). Click **Add supplier** and fill in **Supplier name**, **Contact first name**, **Contact last name**, **Contact email** and optionally **Contact phone**. The list can be searched and shows **Name**, **Contact Name** and **Contact Email**. Each supplier can be edited or deleted, and **Orders and details** shows what you have ordered from that supplier. Suppliers you add here are the ones offered in the supplier dropdowns when confirming supply.

**Where:** Settings > Suppliers

## What does a supplier see or do in StemCounts?

In the current app a supplier is a contact record in a wholesaler's **Suppliers** list, used to track where each flower is coming from. There are no supplier screens: suppliers do not get an order board, and StemCounts does not send them orders or emails. A "Supplier" role exists in the account settings StemCounts staff manage, but the app has no pages built for it. Your supplier relationship, pricing and ordering with them stay between you and the supplier.

**Where:** none (suppliers have no screens)

## How do I handle one florist order that uses several suppliers?

Suppliers are chosen per flower line, not per order, so one order can use as many suppliers as it needs. In the **Inventory List**, pick a different supplier on each line, or use **Set supplier for all…** and then change the exceptions. StemCounts groups the lines by supplier behind the scenes. The order only moves to **Supply Confirmed** when every flower line is confirmed (or removed), so the order waits for its last flower.

**Where:** Order detail window > Inventory List

[Read more](/wholesalers/multi-supplier-orders/)

## How do I mark flowers packed and the order supplier fulfilled?

At **Supply Confirmed**, each confirmed flower line has a **Mark Packed** button; packed lines move under **Packed**. When the whole order is ready, click **Mark Supplier Fulfilled** at the bottom of the order. If any flower is not yet confirmed or removed, the button reads **Confirm flowers first**. The order then moves to the **Supplier Fulfilled** column, and on the florist's side it shows as **Ready**.

**Where:** Order detail window (Supply Confirmed stage)

[Read more](/wholesalers/packing-and-delivery/)

## How do I mark an order delivered or completed?

Click **Mark Delivered** in the order detail window once the flowers have been picked up or delivered. Integrated wholesalers see it after **Mark Supplier Fulfilled**; non-integrated wholesalers see it after **Confirm Order**. The order moves to the **Completed** column and shows **Complete** to the florist. You can also complete a whole week by dragging its group from **Supplier Fulfilled** to **Completed** on the Board. Mark Delivered is disabled while a flower is awaiting the florist's reply.

**Where:** Order detail window

[Read more](/wholesalers/packing-and-delivery/)

## What is Mark Ready for Dispatch and Prep Day?

Dragging a week's orders from **Supply Confirmed** to **Supplier Fulfilled** opens **Mark Ready for Dispatch** for that week. Its **Confirmed Flower Preparation** table lists each flower with **Bunches / Stems**, **Supplier**, **Prep Day** and **Status**. **Prep Day** is the weekday (Monday to Friday) you will prepare that flower for dispatch. Select lines to set a prep day or status for several at once. When every line is set to fulfilled with a prep day, **Mark Ready for Dispatch** moves the week's orders on.

**Where:** Orders > Order Summary > Board > drag a week from Supply Confirmed to Supplier Fulfilled

## Can I print or download an order as a pick-list?

Yes, one order at a time. In the order detail window, the printer icon beside the order number (**Download order PDF**) downloads the order PDF, the same document sent with the order email. From the order review window there is also a **Download Order** button that opens an A4 printable page showing the florist's details, event date, any order note and a table of **Name**, **Colour**, **Quantity**, **Bunches** and **Price** with the total value. On that page, **Download Order** opens your browser's print dialog (print or save as PDF) and **Close & Back** returns you.

**Where:** Order detail window > printer icon; order review window > Download Order

[Read more](/wholesalers/daily-pick-lists/)

## What is the platform authorisation letter?

Each order carries a platform authorisation letter from the florist to you, downloadable with the **Authorisation letter** button in the order detail window and linked from the order email. It states that orders you receive from StemCounts on that florist's account are placed with the florist's authority, that order documents issued through the platform are covered by it, and that it does not transfer liability for goods or let StemCounts vary pricing, credit or trading terms agreed between you and the florist. It lasts until the florist withdraws it in writing.

**Where:** Order detail window > Authorisation letter

## Can I message the florist about an order?

Yes. In the order detail window, the **Chat** button opens a conversation about that order with the florist. On a wide screen it opens beside the order so you can keep the order in view; on a narrower screen it opens over it. The button shows a count of unread messages. Chat is not available for shadow wholesalers, who do not have a StemCounts login.

**Where:** Order detail window > Chat

## How do my flowers become available to florists?

Your inventory is managed on the **Inventory Management** page, opened from the home page card. It is built on the StemCounts flower library. Filter with the buttons **My Inventory** (flowers you carry), **Available to Add** (flowers or colours you could add), **Differs from Library** (flowers where your saved details differ from the library) and **All**. Select a flower with the round tick, choose its colour variants, set your price and stems per bunch, then click **Save Inventory**. Florists are offered your in-stock flowers in the colours you have made available.

**Where:** Home > Inventory Management

[Read more](/wholesalers/managing-inventory/)

## How do I set my prices and stems per bunch?

Each row has **Name** (your internal name for the flower), **Flower**, **Variants**, **Stems per bunch** and **Price (Bunch)**. Type your price per bunch in dollars and your stems per bunch; stems per bunch can also be set per colour variant, and a colour with its own stems per bunch or its own price (from your price list) is ordered and billed at those, ahead of the flower's. Changes are not saved as you type: a **Save Inventory** button appears with a count of changed flowers. A new flower needs both a price and stems per bunch before it will save. If your stems per bunch differ from the library, you are asked to **Confirm Stems Per Bunch Change** and can **Continue with Save**.

**Where:** Home > Inventory Management

[Read more](/wholesalers/managing-inventory/)

## Can I choose which colours of a flower I sell?

Yes. Each flower row has a **Variants** column listing its colour variants, and you tick the ones you carry. Only the colours you have ticked are offered to florists for that flower. When a flower you already stock has new colours you have not added, they show as addable, and the **Available to Add** filter helps you find them. Remember to click **Save Inventory** after changing variants.

**Where:** Home > Inventory Management > flower row > Variants

[Read more](/wholesalers/managing-inventory/)

## How do I bulk upload prices with a CSV?

Click **Bulk Upload** (desktop and tablet), then **Download Template**. The template columns are scCode, colour, price, stemsPerBunch, wholesalerCode and wholesalerName. scCode (the StemCounts flower code) and price (dollars per bunch) are required. Colour can list several colour names separated by commas to identify one colour variant; blank optional cells leave existing values unchanged. Drop your CSV in, click **Validate** to check each row, and fix any invalid rows (a missing colour can be added with **Add colour**). When all rows are valid, click **Upload**. If any row is invalid, nothing is saved. New flowers from the file are added to your inventory as selected.

**Where:** Home > Inventory Management > Bulk Upload

[Read more](/wholesalers/managing-inventory/)

## How do stem lengths work?

When a wholesaler lists one flower several ways, for example at different stem lengths in different pack sizes, each way is an offering with its own length, pack and dated price. These come from the wholesaler's price list loaded by StemCounts. A florist can choose a stem length when a wholesaler offers more than one, and the line is priced from that offering. The requested length reaches the wholesaler on the order and rounds to its own bunch size. A florist can also pick a length that only another wholesaler lists; the line then says who lists it.

**Where:** Florist flower picker; wholesaler order lines

## How do my prices turn into the prices florists see?

Your price per bunch is your wholesale price. Florists see prices with a StemCounts platform fee added on top. The fee is a percentage StemCounts sets per florist, and the rate is locked onto an order when it is created. Your own order totals and invoices show your price without the fee (the Invoices table column is **Amount (exc. fee)**). When a florist chooses you as the wholesaler for an order, every line is recounted to your bunch sizes.

**Where:** Inventory Management (wholesaler); flower picker and order totals (florist)

## Do wholesalers pay a subscription or fees?

Wholesalers do not pay a subscription. The subscription check only applies to florist accounts, and the subscription price is what a florist pays StemCounts for platform access. Platform fees are already managed within the online pricing: the fee is added to the price the florist sees, so it is not charged to you as a subscription. To join as a wholesaler, sign up at [https://app.stemcounts.com/register](https://app.stemcounts.com/register), or email support@stemcounts.com with questions about your business.

**Where:** none

## Where are my invoices?

The **Invoices** card on the home page lists your florist orders (excluding drafts) with **Reference**, **Florist**, **Delivery Date**, **Amount (exc. fee)** and **Status**, sorted with active orders first and then by delivery date. **View Invoice** opens the invoice with each flower, variant, total stems, stems per bunch, bunches, price per bunch and line total. Paying orders online through StemCounts, and the refund actions that depend on it, are not available yet, so settle payment with your florists the way you normally do.

**Where:** Home > Invoices

## How do florists find me and send me orders?

Florists choose which wholesaler to send each order to from the wholesalers they can see. You can register your own account by choosing **Wholesaler** ("Review and fulfil florist orders.") at sign-up. In **Settings > Your Organisation**, **Areas you trade in** controls where you appear: florists only see wholesalers that trade where they are based, and leaving it empty makes you visible everywhere. Set **Based in**, your **Contact email** (where order emails go) and your phone so florists can reach you. Keep your inventory selected and priced so florists can order your flowers.

**Where:** Settings > Your Organisation (Based in, Areas you trade in, Contact email)
