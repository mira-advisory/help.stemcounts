These are the same wholesalers-focused FAQ entries shown inside the StemCounts app under **FAQ** in the user menu. Each entry links to a step-by-step Scribehow walkthrough where one exists.

## How do I view incoming florist orders?

Go to the "Receive & Accept" page to see all incoming orders from florists. Orders are organized by week and delivery date. You can review order details, flower quantities, and special requests before accepting.

**Where:** `/receive-accept` in the app · **UI element:** Orders List

## What is the 'Assign and Send' workflow?

After accepting florist orders, use "Assign and Send" to select suppliers for each flower type, group orders by supplier, and send them out. The system shows combined flower quantities across all accepted orders, letting you assign each flower to your preferred supplier and track order status.

**Where:** `/assign-send` in the app · **UI element:** Assign Workflow

## How do I handle out-of-stock flowers?

When reviewing orders, you can mark specific flowers as unavailable or suggest substitutions. The system will notify the florist, who can then approve the substitution or modify their order accordingly.

**Where:** `/receive-accept` in the app · **UI element:** Substitution Controls

## Can I see aggregated flower quantities across all orders?

Yes! The system automatically aggregates all flower quantities across accepted orders for each delivery date you can select your own date for arrival

**Where:** `/assign-send` in the app · **UI element:** Aggregate View

## How do I mark an order as ready for delivery?

In the "Confirmed and Ready" section, you can mark orders as prepared and ready. This sends a notification to the florist and updates the delivery status.

**Where:** `/confirmed-ready` in the app · **UI element:** Mark Ready Button

## What does the Accept Order button do?

Accepts a florist's order and moves it to your workflow for fulfillment

**Where:** `/receive-accept` in the app · **UI element:** Accept Button

## What does the Reject button do?

Declines a florist's order - they'll be notified that it was rejected

**Where:** `/receive-accept` in the app · **UI element:** Reject Button

## What does the Next: Assign & Confirm button do?

Takes you to the next step where you assign orders to suppliers

**Where:** `/receive-accept` in the app · **UI element:** Next Button

## How do I assign flowers to a supplier?

Select a supplier from the dropdown next to each flower group, then click Send to create the supplier order

**Where:** `/assign-send` in the app · **UI element:** Supplier Dropdown

## What does the Send button do?

Creates and sends an order to the selected supplier for those flowers

**Where:** `/assign-send` in the app · **UI element:** Send Button

## What does the Confirm button do?

Confirms that your supplier has accepted and will fulfill the flower order

**Where:** `/assign-send` in the app · **UI element:** Confirm Button

## What does Mark as Fulfilled do?

Marks an order as delivered and complete - florists will be notified

**Where:** `/confirmed-ready` in the app · **UI element:** Mark Fulfilled Button

## What do the checkboxes do?

Track individual items as you prepare or deliver them - check them off as you complete each item

**Where:** `/confirmed-ready` in the app · **UI element:** Item Checkboxes

## What do the date filters do?

Filter orders by delivery date range - select From and To dates to narrow down the list

**Where:** `/assigned-orders` in the app · **UI element:** Date Filters

## What does Clear Filters do?

Removes all date filters and shows all assigned orders

**Where:** `/assigned-orders` in the app · **UI element:** Clear Filters Button

## What do the view tabs do?

Switch between Florist View (by customer), Aggregate View (combined totals), and Fulfill View (for delivery)

**Where:** `/assigned-orders` in the app · **UI element:** View Tabs
