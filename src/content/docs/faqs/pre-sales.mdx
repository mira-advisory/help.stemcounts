## What is StemCounts?

StemCounts is an Australian flower-ordering platform that connects florists with their flower wholesalers. Florists organise their orders by week, event, arrangement and stem, then send one order to each wholesaler, who accepts it and confirms what they can supply. StemCounts acts as an agent for wholesalers: it does not buy, own or resell flowers, and the supply contract is between the florist and the wholesaler. It runs in the web browser on a computer, tablet or phone. To try it, sign up at [https://app.stemcounts.com/register](https://app.stemcounts.com/register).

**Where:** [https://app.stemcounts.com](https://app.stemcounts.com)

## How much does StemCounts cost for a florist?

Florists pay a subscription, in Australian dollars: $60 AUD for 6 months, or $100 AUD for 12 months. There are no tiers and no per-user fees: one subscription covers everyone in your florist business. Subscriptions are paid by card and renew automatically until cancelled. Every new florist business starts with a 7-day free trial, with no card needed. To start your trial, sign up at [https://app.stemcounts.com/register](https://app.stemcounts.com/register).

**Where:** **Settings** > **Your Organisation** > **Subscription**

## Is there a free trial?

Yes. A new florist business gets a 7-day free trial. It starts automatically when you create a new organisation at sign-up, and no card is needed to start it. People who join an existing organisation later do not start a new trial. When the trial ends you choose a plan ($60 AUD for 6 months or $100 AUD for 12 months) to keep using the florist features; your orders and settings are kept. Sign up at [https://app.stemcounts.com/register](https://app.stemcounts.com/register).

**Where:** trial banner at the top of the app; **Settings** > **Your Organisation** > **Subscription**

[Read more](/billing/trial-and-blocked-access/)

## Do wholesalers pay to use StemCounts?

Wholesalers do not pay a subscription. The subscription is paid by florists for access to the platform. Platform fees are already managed within the online pricing that florists see, so there is no subscription or sign-up charge for a wholesaler. To join as a wholesaler, sign up at [https://app.stemcounts.com/register](https://app.stemcounts.com/register), or email support@stemcounts.com if you would like to talk to the team about setting up your business first.

**Where:** none

## Is StemCounts only for Australian businesses? What currency are prices in?

StemCounts is an Australian business, built for Australian florists and wholesalers. Prices and subscriptions are in Australian dollars (AUD). Arrangement budgets you enter while planning an order are ex GST, as the app states on the budget screen. If you have a question about using StemCounts outside Australia, email support@stemcounts.com and the team will let you know.

**Where:** none

## How do I sign up?

Go to [https://app.stemcounts.com/register](https://app.stemcounts.com/register). First enter your details (name, email, phone and a password), then find your business under **Your Organisation**: pick it if it is already on StemCounts, or create it as a new organisation. A florist business that creates a new organisation starts its 7-day free trial straight away. If you already have an account, log in at [https://app.stemcounts.com/login](https://app.stemcounts.com/login). If anything goes wrong, email support@stemcounts.com.

**Where:** [https://app.stemcounts.com/register](https://app.stemcounts.com/register)

## How do I contact StemCounts?

Email support@stemcounts.com and the StemCounts team will reply. There is no published phone number or postal address for support. If this chat cannot answer your question, you can also leave your email here and the question is passed to the team. If you are ready to try StemCounts, you can sign up yourself at [https://app.stemcounts.com/register](https://app.stemcounts.com/register).

## Is there a mobile app?

There is no native app to download from an app store. StemCounts is a web app that works in the browser on a phone, tablet or computer, and its screens are designed to work on a phone. Open [https://app.stemcounts.com](https://app.stemcounts.com) on your phone and log in with the same account you use on a computer. To keep it on your home screen like an app, use **Add to Home Screen** in Safari on an iPhone or **Install app** in Chrome on Android: it then opens full screen with its own icon. New users can sign up at [https://app.stemcounts.com/register](https://app.stemcounts.com/register).

**Where:** none

## What does StemCounts not do yet?

A few things are not available today. Florists cannot pay wholesaler orders online through StemCounts: settle payment with your wholesaler the way you normally do. There is no native mobile app (it is a web app that works on a phone). There is no report export, and no in-app screen for inviting people to your organisation. If you need something that is not listed in the app, email support@stemcounts.com so the team knows it matters to you.
