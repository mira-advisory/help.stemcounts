## What do the order statuses mean for a florist?

Your order moves through these stages:

- **Draft**: you are still building it and it has not been sent.
- **Sent**: the order has gone to your wholesaler.
- **Accepted**: your wholesaler has accepted responsibility for the order.
- **Supply Confirmed**: your wholesaler has confirmed they can supply it.
- **Ready**: your wholesaler has packed the order and it is ready for delivery.
- **Complete**: the order has been delivered and completed.

Accepted does not mean every flower is confirmed. Supply is confirmed flower by flower after the wholesaler accepts, and the order only reaches **Supply Confirmed** once every flower still on it is confirmed.

**Where:** Orders list and order page

[Read more](/florists/tracking-your-order/)

## What do the order statuses mean for a wholesaler?

On your side the same order shows these labels:

- **New Orders**: a new order from a florist, waiting for you to act.
- **Accepted**: you have accepted responsibility for the order.
- **Supply Confirmed**: availability has been confirmed.
- **Supplier Fulfilled**: the flowers have been fulfilled and are ready for dispatch.
- **Completed**: the order has been delivered and completed.

Which columns you see depends on how your account is set up. The full board runs Sent, Accepted, Confirmed, Fulfilled, Delivered. A simpler setup skips the Fulfilled step and runs Sent, Accepted, Confirmed, Delivered.

**Where:** Order board and order page

[Read more](/wholesalers/the-order-board/)

## What do the statuses on each flower line mean?

Each flower has its own status as well as the order. Florists see:

- **Supply OK**: your wholesaler has confirmed they can supply this flower.
- **Packed & Ready**: packed and ready for delivery.
- **Action Required**: the wholesaler cannot supply it as ordered and needs you to choose a substitute, or accept or decline fewer stems.
- **Substitute Confirmed**: you accepted a substitute.
- **Cancelled**: the flower was unavailable and has been removed from your order.
- **Complete**: delivered.

Wholesalers see **Awaiting Florist** while they wait for the florist to answer a substitute or fewer-stems offer, and **Supplier Fulfilled** once a flower is fulfilled.

**Where:** order page, flower lines

[Read more](/florists/tracking-your-order/)

## When does an order move to Supply Confirmed, Ready or Complete?

StemCounts works the order status out from its flower lines. Flowers that were cancelled are ignored. The order moves forward only when every remaining flower has reached the next stage:

- every flower confirmed (or its substitute confirmed): **Supply Confirmed**
- every flower packed: **Ready**
- every flower delivered: **Complete**

While any flower is waiting on the florist to answer a substitute or fewer-stems offer, the order cannot move forward at all. So one slow line holds the whole order at its current stage.

**Where:** order page

[Read more](/florists/tracking-your-order/)

## Why does my order show an earlier stage than one of my wholesalers has reached?

If your order went to more than one wholesaler, your order's overall status follows the wholesaler who is furthest behind. For example, if one wholesaler has marked their part **Ready** and another is still at **Accepted**, your order shows **Accepted** until the second one catches up. Any part that was cancelled is left out of this. Your overall status never goes backwards once it has moved forward. Open the order to see where each wholesaler's part and each flower is up to.

**Where:** Orders list and order page

[Read more](/florists/tracking-your-order/)

## What happens when I send an order to a wholesaler?

When you press **Send Order**, each wholesaler on the order gets:

- an email with the order PDF attached and a button to accept it
- an in-app notification, **New order received**, if they have a StemCounts account
- a text message with a link to view and accept, when order texts are switched on

You are copied on the wholesaler's email, which is your email confirmation that it went out. There is no separate "order sent" email to you. When order texts are switched on you also get a short text receipt naming the wholesaler and delivery date.

**Where:** order page, **Send Order**

[Read more](/florists/sending-your-order/)

## What is in the new order email a wholesaler receives?

The subject is **New order from [florist business]: [order reference]**. It says who sent it, how many items there are and the delivery date, with a short facts table (Order, From, Delivery, Items).

The main button is **Accept this order**, with **View the order** underneath. The order PDF is attached, along with any Canva mood boards pinned to the order's events. If the PDF cannot be produced in time, the email still goes and the order is still in the app.

It goes to your business's published contact email, or if there is none, to every active user in your business. The florist who sent it is copied, and replying goes straight to them, not to a no-reply address.

**Where:** email

## How does the one-click Accept link work without logging in?

The **Accept this order** button in the order email (and the link in the order text) works without signing in. The link itself proves which order it is for.

Opening it shows you the order, with **Accept this order** and **Log in to manage**. Nothing is accepted just by opening the link, so email security scanners cannot accept an order by accident. You have to press the button.

Once you accept, the page confirms the florist has been told. The link only works once. Everything after accepting, such as confirming quantities and handling substitutions, happens in the app after you log in.

**Where:** email / text message

## What does "This link has already been used" mean?

Accept links work once. After an order is accepted, both the email link and the text link for that order stop working. If you open one again you will see **This link has already been used**, or, if the order is still findable, **Already accepted** or **This order has moved on** with its current status. The usual cause is a second click on an order that is already accepted, perhaps by a colleague. Sign in to StemCounts to see where the order is up to.

**Where:** accept page opened from email or text

## Can I view an order without a StemCounts account?

Yes. If you trade with a florist but do not have a StemCounts account, your order email's **View the order** button opens a shared order page that needs no login. It shows the order details with **Download PDF** and **Download the authorisation letter**, plus an invitation to create an account to manage the order. The email also offers **Accept this order**, which works without signing in.

A shared order link is read-only and keeps working for as long as the order exists, so you can forward it to whoever packs the order.

**Where:** shared order page (link from email or text)

## How do I get the order PDF, and what is on it?

The order PDF is a picking document. It lists the flowers with stem and bunch counts and deliberately shows no prices.

Wholesalers get it three ways: attached to the new order email, from the **Download order PDF** icon on the order in the app, and from **Download PDF** on the shared order page. Either side of an order, the florist or the wholesaler, is allowed to download it.

**Where:** wholesaler order page (download icon), shared order page, email attachment

## What is the authorisation letter link in the order email?

At the bottom of the new order email is a link to open the florist's platform authorisation letter as a PDF, no login needed. It is the florist's letter confirming StemCounts sends orders on their behalf.

The link only appears when the letter can actually be produced. That needs the florist's business name and ABN, plus the wholesaler's business name and address. If the florist has no ABN on file, the email has no letter link at all. Florists can fix this by completing their organisation details.

**Where:** email

## How do I accept an order inside the app?

Open a new order from your board and press **Accept Order**. Dragging a card from Sent to Accepted opens the same order window. You accept the whole order at once. After that you confirm or flag each flower.

You'll see **Order accepted**, and the florist gets an in-app notification saying you accepted. Accepting only means you have taken the order on. Confirming supply is a separate step.

**Where:** Order board, order page

[Read more](/wholesalers/accepting-an-order/)

## What steps does a wholesaler follow after accepting?

The steps depend on your setup.

**Simple setup:** every flower is accepted with the order. Use **Actions** on a flower only if something changes. Press **Confirm Order** once supply is locked in, then **Mark Delivered** after pickup or delivery.

**Full setup:** press **Confirm** on each flower (or **Confirm All**), or **Can't Supply** on any you cannot fill. Mark flowers packed with **Mark Packed**, or the whole order with **Mark Supplier Fulfilled**, then **Mark Delivered**.

While a florist still has to answer a change, the button reads **Awaiting Florist**. If any flower is unconfirmed it reads **Confirm flowers first**.

**Where:** order page

[Read more](/wholesalers/packing-and-delivery/)

## How do I reject an order, and what happens to it?

While an order is new, the wholesaler can press **Reject Order**. The order and its flowers are marked **Rejected**, which means the order will not proceed. The florist gets an in-app notification, **Order rejected**, saying the wholesaler rejected the order. No email or text is sent for a rejection.

In the current version, rejecting does not turn the order back into a draft. The florist should talk to the wholesaler, or send the flowers again as a new order.

**Where:** wholesaler order page, **Reject Order**

[Read more](/wholesalers/accepting-an-order/)

## What can a wholesaler do if they cannot supply a flower?

After accepting an order, the wholesaler can flag any flower still at Accepted. It asks **What would you like to offer for [flower]?** with three choices:

- **Offer Substitutes**: offer between one and five alternative flowers for the florist to choose from.
- **Partial Quantity**: offer fewer stems of the same flower.
- **Unavailable**: remove the flower from the order.

You can add a note for the florist, for example "Out of season until June". Press **Send to Florist** or **Confirm Cancellation**. The florist is told in the app, by email, and by text when order texts are switched on. A change covers the whole line: every arrangement using that flower, colour and length.

**Where:** wholesaler order page, **Can't Supply** or **Actions** on a flower

## How do I offer a substitute or fewer stems?

For **Offer Substitutes**, search your range and add up to five options. Each one needs a flower, a colour, a stem quantity and a price per bunch.

For **Partial Quantity**, enter the number of stems you can supply. It must be at least one bunch, in whole bunches, and fewer than were ordered. A partial quantity is priced at your current price for the original flower.

After you send it, you will see **Substitutes sent. The florist has been told** or **Partial quantity sent. The florist has been told**. The line then shows **Awaiting Florist**, and the order cannot be confirmed until the florist answers.

**Where:** wholesaler order page

## How do I respond when my wholesaler offers a substitute or fewer stems?

Lines that need your answer show **Action Required**. Press **Review Substitutes** on the line to open **Review Substitute Options**. It shows the original flower beside each option and the price difference per stem.

Pick an option and press **Confirm Selection**, or press **Decline All Options**. If you accept, the line becomes **Substitute Confirmed**. If you decline, the line is cancelled and removed. Either way, the wholesaler gets a notification (**Substitute selected** or **Substitute options declined**) and the order can move on again. If a substitute is already in that arrangement, you will be asked to choose a different option.

**Where:** order page, **Review Substitutes**

## What am I charged after a substitute, fewer stems or an unavailable flower?

You are billed for what the wholesaler supplies:

- **Substitute accepted**: you pay the price shown for the substitute you chose, for the stems offered. The prices shown for substitutes include the platform fee, like every other price you see.
- **Fewer stems accepted**: you pay for the reduced number of stems. Your arrangement recipe stays as you designed it, but the bill reflects what is supplied.
- **Declined**: the line is removed and comes off your invoice.
- **Unavailable**: the line is removed and comes off your invoice. You do not need to do anything unless you want to source it elsewhere.

**Where:** order page, invoice

## Why is my order stuck on Awaiting Florist or Action Required?

When a wholesaler offers a substitute or fewer stems, the order waits for the florist's answer. Until the florist responds, the wholesaler cannot confirm, fulfil or deliver the order. Their buttons read **Awaiting Florist**, with the note "Waiting on the florist to respond to a substitute / partial / cancel offer." The florist sees **Action Required** on the line. Once the florist accepts or declines, the order can move forward again. An **Unavailable** flower does not hold the order up, because it is removed straight away.

**Where:** order page

## What emails does a florist get about an order?

Florists receive these order emails:

1. **A copy of the new order email** sent to the wholesaler, when you send an order.
2. **[Wholesaler] accepted your order: [reference]**, when the wholesaler accepts using the Accept link from their email or text.
3. **A supply change email**, when a wholesaler offers a substitute, offers fewer stems, or marks a flower unavailable.

There is currently no email when an order becomes Supply Confirmed, Ready or Complete, or when it is rejected. Those changes show in the app and in your notification bell.

**Where:** email

## When does the florist get the "accepted your order" email?

When a wholesaler accepts through **Accept this order** from their email or text, the florist gets:

- an in-app notification
- an email titled **[Wholesaler] accepted your order: [reference]**, with a **View the order** button
- a text, when order texts are switched on

The email says nothing is needed right now, and that quantities and substitutions will show on the order as the wholesaler works through it.

When the wholesaler accepts with **Accept Order** inside the app, the current version sends the florist the in-app notification only. The email goes to the person who sent the order, or, failing that, to the florist business's contact email.

## What does the supply change email say?

There is one email for each kind of change:

- **Choose a substitute for [flower] on [reference]**: the wholesaler has offered one or more substitutes. The button is **Choose a substitute**.
- **Accept or decline: fewer [flower] on [reference]**: says how many of the ordered stems they can supply. The button is **Accept or decline**.
- **[Wholesaler] cannot supply [flower]: [reference]**: the flower has been removed and comes off your invoice. The button is **View the order**.

The first two explain that the order waits for your answer. Any note from the wholesaler is included. The email goes to the person who sent the order, or, failing that, to your business contact email.

**Where:** email

## What text messages does StemCounts send?

When order texts are on, StemCounts sends these texts:

- **To the wholesaler, new order:** "New StemCounts order from [florist]. Click to view and accept: [link]"
- **To the florist, order sent:** "StemCounts: order [ref] sent to [wholesaler] for [date]. We will text you when they accept it."
- **To the florist, accepted:** "StemCounts: [wholesaler] accepted order [ref]. [link]"
- **To the florist, supply change:** "...can't supply [flower] on [ref]. Choose a substitute: [link]", "...can supply [x] of [y] [flower] on [ref]. Accept or decline: [link]", or "...can't supply [flower] on [ref]. Removed, and off your invoice. [link]"

Emails and in-app notifications are sent either way.

**Where:** text message

## Which phone number do order texts go to?

**Wholesalers:** the text goes to your business phone number if it is a mobile. If not, it goes to the mobile of every active user in your business.

**Florists:** the "sent" text goes to the person who sent the order. The "accepted" and supply change texts go to that person and to your business phone if it is a mobile.

Only mobiles can receive texts. Australian mobiles work in any common format (04..., +61 4...). Landlines and incomplete numbers are skipped. Overseas mobiles need the full international format starting with +. Keep your **Mobile** up to date in Settings > Your Profile. If there is no usable number, the email and in-app notification still go.

**Where:** Settings > Your Profile, **Mobile**

## Can I reply to or stop StemCounts text messages?

Order texts are one-way. Replies are not received, so use the link in the text to act on the order. Every text names StemCounts in the message itself so you know who it is from. They are sent as transactional messages about orders you are part of, not marketing. There is currently no setting in the app to turn order texts off for yourself, and the **Best way to reach you** preference does not change them. If you do not want texts, contact support@stemcounts.com. Resending an order email never sends a second text, so a duplicate text does not mean a duplicate order.

**Where:** text message

## What does the notification bell show?

The bell shows a count of your unread notifications (up to **99+**). Click it to open your recent notifications, newest first. Unread ones are highlighted and each shows how long ago it arrived.

Click a notification to go straight to the order. Use **Mark all read** to clear the count, the X to dismiss one, or refresh to check for new ones. If there is nothing, you will see **No notifications**. The count updates as you move between pages. The full list reloads each time you open the bell.

**Where:** the bell at the top of the app

## What in-app notifications does a florist get?

Florists are notified in the app when a wholesaler:

- accepts an order: **Order accepted**
- confirms supply: **Supply confirmed** or **Order confirmed**
- packs or fulfils it: **Order packed and ready** or **Order fulfilled**
- marks it delivered: **Order delivered**
- rejects it: **Order rejected**
- offers a change: **Substitute flower offered** or **Reduced quantity offered**, asking you to choose, or accept or decline
- removes an unavailable flower: **Flower cancelled**
- messages you on an order: **New message**

Everyone at your business receives these.

**Where:** the bell at the top of the app

## What in-app notifications does a wholesaler get?

Wholesalers are notified in the app when:

- a florist sends you an order: **New order received**, "[florist] has placed order [reference] with you"
- a florist confirms delivery: **Delivery confirmed**
- a florist answers a substitute or fewer-stems offer: **Substitute selected** or **Substitute options declined** (the line is then cancelled)
- a florist messages you on an order: **New message**

Every active user at your business receives new order notifications. Clicking one opens the order.

**Where:** the bell at the top of the app

## How do I confirm I have received my flowers?

Once a wholesaler's part of your order has reached **Supply Confirmed** or **Ready**, you can confirm what arrived:

- **Confirm Received** on a single flower line
- **Confirm Delivery of All Flowers** for everything from that wholesaler
- **Mark as Delivered**, shown when the wholesaler's part is **Ready**

Received lines show **Received**. When every line from that wholesaler is received, their part becomes **Complete**, and the wholesaler gets a **Delivery confirmed** notification.

**Where:** order page

## What is the Order emails switch in my profile?

Under **Notifications** in Settings > Your Profile is a switch called **Order emails**, described as "New orders, changes and updates from your wholesalers." Your choice is saved to your profile.

Be aware that the current version picks order email recipients from the order and the business contact details, and it does not check this switch. So turning it off may not stop order emails yet. It has no effect on in-app notifications or texts. If you need to stop emails, contact support@stemcounts.com.

**Where:** Settings > Your Profile > **Notifications**

## Where do I see what has changed in StemCounts?

Open your user menu (on a phone, tap your initials at the top right) and choose **What's new**. The page, **Recent changes to Stem Counts**, lists each release by date and version number, one line per change. It also tells you which version you are running.

Filter the list with **Everything**, **New**, **Improved** or **Fixed**. A release that needed everyone to update is marked **Major release**.

**Where:** user menu > **What's new**

## What is the "Version ... is ready" bar and the Reload now button?

When a new version of StemCounts is live, a bar appears at the bottom of the screen saying **Version [number] is ready**. Press **Reload now** to switch to it, or dismiss it with the X and carry on. StemCounts never reloads on its own while you work.

Releases that only fix bugs apply quietly, with no bar. For a major release you will see a full-screen message: "Reload the page to carry on with the latest version of Stem Counts." Its only option is **Reload now**. StemCounts checks for a new version when you come back to the tab and at least every 15 minutes.

**Where:** bar at the bottom of the app

## What other emails does StemCounts send?

Besides order emails, StemCounts sends:

- **Reset your StemCounts password**: a **Choose a new password** link that works once and expires after one hour.
- **Inventory requests**: when a florist asks a wholesaler to stock a flower, the wholesaler gets "[florist] would like to buy [flower] from you" with an **Update your inventory** button. The florist is copied, and replies go to the florist.
- **Subscription invoices and receipts**: sent to florists with the StemCounts-branded document attached.

StemCounts also sends some internal emails to its own team, which customers do not receive.

**Where:** email
