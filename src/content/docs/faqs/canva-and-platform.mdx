## What does the Canva add-on do for a florist?

Canva is an optional add-on that links your organisation's own Canva account to StemCounts. Once it is on and connected, each event can carry one Canva mood board. You can pin a design you already have in Canva, or start a new board straight from the event. When you send the order, the mood board goes to the wholesaler as a PDF with the order. Everything happens in your own Canva account, as you: StemCounts does not give you a Canva account or share designs between businesses. In the app it is described as: "Bring your Canva along. Pin a moodboard to an event, start a card or a sign straight from the order, and your boards travel to the wholesaler with it."

**Where:** Settings > **Your Organisation** > **Canva**; the Canva button on an event page

## Is Canva included in my subscription, and how much does it cost?

Canva is not part of the standard subscription. It is a separate add-on for your organisation, and the StemCounts team switches it on for each business. Until it is on, you see **Request access** instead of the Canva controls. The app does not publish a price for the add-on, so ask the StemCounts team (support@stemcounts.com) about cost before relying on it. There is no self-serve checkout for Canva in the app today.

**Where:** Settings > **Your Organisation** > **Canva**

## How do I request access to Canva?

If your business does not have Canva yet, you can still see a Canva button on your events and a **Canva** section in Settings under **Your Organisation**. Click either one and a window titled **Request access to Canva** explains the add-on. Click **Request access** and the StemCounts team is emailed; you see "Request sent". Your organisation can ask once a day; asking again shows "Already requested". The request does not switch anything on by itself: the team will be in touch. If Canva was switched on since the page loaded, the button carries straight on instead. Wholesalers do not see this option.

**Where:** Settings > **Your Organisation** > **Canva** > **Request access**, or the Canva button on an event page

## How do I connect my Canva account?

With the add-on on, the **Canva** section shows "Not connected yet." and a **Connect to Canva** button. Click it and your browser goes to Canva, where you sign in and approve StemCounts. Canva then sends you back and the section shows **Connected as** your Canva name, with the date. You can also start from an event: with no board pinned, the Canva button takes you to Canva's login and brings you back to the same event. If it fails you see "Canva was not connected" with a reason, for example that you chose not to allow StemCounts, or that the attempt took longer than ten minutes. Just try again.

**Where:** Settings > **Your Organisation** > **Canva** > **Connect to Canva**

## How do I add an existing Canva design to an event as its mood board?

Open the event and click the button showing the Canva logo, next to **Add to Weekly Order**. With no board pinned yet, a window titled **Upload from Canva** shows your Canva designs as tiles. Type in **Search your designs, then press Enter** to narrow them, or use **Show more**. Click a tile to select it, then click **Accept**. That design becomes the event's mood board and opens in a preview. Nothing is copied or changed in your Canva. Each event holds one mood board. If Canva is not connected yet you see "Connect your Canva first, then come back here." with **Go to Settings**.

**Where:** Event page > the Canva button (Canva logo) > **Upload from Canva**

## How do I start a new mood board in Canva from an event?

In the same Canva window, under **Or start a new one**, there is a name box already filled with the order reference and event name. Change it if you like, then click **Create in Canva**. StemCounts creates a new design in your own Canva account, sized as an A4 landscape page, pins it to the event, and opens Canva's editor in a new tab. If you want a different shape, change it in Canva. If nothing opens, your browser blocked the new tab: the app says "Your browser blocked the Canva tab. Allow pop-ups for this site and try again." Creating a new board replaces any board already pinned to the event.

**Where:** Event page > the Canva button > **Or start a new one** > **Create in Canva**

## How do I see or edit the mood board on an event?

When an event has a mood board, the Canva button shows a purple count beside the logo. Click it to open a preview of the board as a PDF, described as "A preview of the latest version. Make changes in Canva." It may briefly say "Getting the latest version from Canva...". To change the design, click **Edit in Canva**, which opens the design in Canva in a new tab. Edits you make in Canva show up the next time you open the preview. If you see "Your Canva connection needs renewing before this can be shown.", use **Go to Settings** and connect Canva again.

**Where:** Event page > the Canva button (with a purple number) > **Edit in Canva**

## How do I swap or remove the mood board on an event?

Open the board from the event's Canva button. To use a different design, click **Swap design**: the picker opens as **Swap the mood board**, the current board is marked **Current**, and whatever you **Accept** (or create) replaces it. To take the board off the event, click **Remove from event** and confirm. The confirmation says "It will not be sent with the order. The design stays in your Canva exactly as it is." Removing or swapping never deletes anything from your Canva account. You cannot swap or remove a board once the event's order has been sent.

**Where:** Event page > the Canva button > **Swap design** or **Remove from event**

## How does my mood board get to the wholesaler?

When you send an order, the mood boards pinned to the events in that order are turned into PDFs and attached to the order email to the wholesaler. Each file is named after the order, for example Moodboard\_ followed by the order reference, with \_2 and \_3 added if there is more than one. A wholesaler only gets the boards for the events whose flowers are on their order. If a board cannot be attached in time, or the email would be too large, the order still sends, and the wholesaler can download the board from the order page. A design using premium Canva elements your account has not paid for cannot be exported, so it is skipped.

**Where:** Sent automatically with the order email; also on the wholesaler's order page

## Where does a wholesaler find the florist's mood board?

Wholesalers do not need Canva or any add-on. If the florist pinned a Canva mood board to an event on your order, it comes as a PDF attachment on the order email. It also appears on the order page, whether you are signed in or opened the order link from the email, under **Design boards from the florist, made with Canva**, with a line such as "Sent with this order as PDF. One board." Click **Download PDF** beside a board to save it. If it shows **Preparing PDF...**, the file is being made and will download in a moment. Orders with no board show nothing extra.

**Where:** The wholesaler's order page, and the order link from the email: **Design boards from the florist, made with Canva**

## What does Canva show on an event after the order is sent?

Once any of an event's flowers are on an order sent to a wholesaler, the event's Canva becomes read-only. The Canva button then opens the exact mood board that was sent, described as "The mood board that went to the wholesaler with this order." There is no **Edit in Canva**, **Swap design** or **Remove from event**. If no board went with the order, the Canva button does not appear at all. This keeps a record of exactly what the wholesaler received. You can still edit the original design in your own Canva, but it will not change what was sent.

**Where:** Event page and the order page's event cards

## Where do I see the mood board on the order page?

On an order's page, each event card has a small Canva icon among its top-right buttons when that event has a mood board. Hover it to see "Canva mood board". Click it to open the same PDF preview as the event page, with **Edit in Canva** and **Remove from event**. Swapping to a different design is done from the event page, where the picker lives. After the order is sent, the icon opens the board that was sent instead. Cards for events without a board look the same as always.

**Where:** Order page > event card > the small Canva icon at the top right

## How do I disconnect Canva, and what happens to my designs?

In the **Canva** section, click **Disconnect Canva** and confirm. The confirmation says "StemCounts will forget the connection and tell Canva to revoke it. Designs already in your Canva stay where they are. You can connect again at any time." Your organisation has one Canva connection, so disconnecting affects everyone in your business. If the section warns "The last call to Canva failed. If designs stop opening, disconnect and connect again.", doing exactly that usually fixes it.

**Where:** Settings > **Your Organisation** > **Canva** > **Disconnect Canva**

## Why can't I see the Canva button?

There are a few reasons. Canva is for florists: wholesalers never see it. It is a paid add-on, and it may not be available on StemCounts for your business yet. The Canva button on events, and the icon on the order page's event cards, only appear in the larger-screen layout, not on a phone. Once an event's order has been sent, the button only appears if a mood board went with it. If none of these apply, ask the StemCounts team at support@stemcounts.com.

**Where:** Event page header; Settings > **Your Organisation**

## What is in Settings?

Settings has a sidebar grouped by job. Under **You** are **Your Profile** ("Your name, contact details and password.") and **Your Organisation** ("Your business details, trading areas, payment setup and subscription."). Florists also find Canva under **Your Organisation** if their business has the add-on. Under **Ordering**, florists see **Favourite Arrangements** and **Explore Flowers**, and wholesalers see **Suppliers**. Further groups (Customers, Catalogue, Money, Platform) are for the StemCounts team only.

## Where is the menu on my phone?

On a phone there is no menu button. A bar along the bottom of the screen takes you anywhere in one tap. For florists it has **Home**, **Orders**, a raised **New order** button, **Search** and **Chat**; wholesalers get **Home**, **Orders**, **Library**, **Search** and **Chat**. Inside an order, an event or the flower picker, **Home** becomes **Back** and says where it goes. On some screens the raised button does that screen's main job instead, for example **Add event** on an order. **Search** finds orders and events by name, order number or week. **Chat** opens the Ask StemCounts assistant.

Tap your initials at the top right for **Settings**, **What's new** with your version, and **Sign out**.

**Where:** The bar along the bottom of the screen, and your initials at the top right, on a phone

## What happened to the Show Me button?

The coral **Show Me** tab and its **Show Me How** panel have been removed (September 2026). Ask the Ask StemCounts assistant instead: on a computer it is the round chat button at the bottom right, and on a phone it is **Chat** in the bar along the bottom. Type your question in your own words, for example "how do I round up bunches", and it answers from the same help StemCounts support uses. If it cannot answer, it offers to pass you to support@stemcounts.com.

**Where:** The round chat button at the bottom right, or **Chat** in the bottom bar on a phone

## How do I contact StemCounts support, and is there an FAQ?

There is no FAQ page inside the app any more. The **Help guides** tile on your home page opens the StemCounts help site, help.stemcounts.com, in a new tab, with step-by-step guides for florists and wholesalers. For a quick answer, ask the Ask StemCounts assistant: on a computer it is the round chat button at the bottom right, and on a phone it is **Chat** in the bar along the bottom. It answers from the same help guides, and if it cannot help it offers to pass your question to the team. You can also email support@stemcounts.com directly at any time.

**Where:** Home page > **Help guides** tile; the round chat button at the bottom right, or **Chat** in the bottom bar on a phone

## Where can I see what has changed in StemCounts?

Open your name menu (on a phone, tap your initials at the top right) and click **What's new**. The page is headed **Recent changes to Stem Counts** and says which version you are running. Each release is listed by date with one line per change, tagged **New**, **Improved** or **Fixed**. Use the filters **Everything**, **New**, **Improved** and **Fixed** to narrow the list. Your version number also appears in small grey text under **What's new** in the menu.

**Where:** Your name menu > **What's new**

## What does "Version ... is ready" mean at the bottom of the screen?

A new version of StemCounts has been released. The bar says, for example, "Version ... is ready" with a **Reload now** button. Click it when it suits you, ideally after saving what you are working on, and you get the latest version; then see **What's new** for the changes. You can close the bar with the X. The app checks for updates regularly while it is open and never reloads by itself. Small bug-fix releases do not show the bar. Rarely, a major release shows a full-screen **Major release** message whose only option is **Reload now**.

**Where:** The update bar along the bottom of the screen

## Is there a StemCounts mobile app I can install?

There is nothing to download from an app store, but you can install StemCounts on your phone's home screen, and it then opens full screen like an app, with its own icon and no browser bar. On an iPhone, open app.stemcounts.com in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**. On Android, open it in Chrome, tap the three dots menu, then **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**). Sign in once and it remembers you. On a phone you move around with the bar along the bottom of the screen. It needs an internet connection, as it does not work offline, and it updates itself: when a new version is ready you see a **Reload now** bar.

**Where:** Your phone's browser: **Add to Home Screen** (iPhone, Safari) or **Install app** (Android, Chrome)

## Which web browsers does StemCounts support?

The help site recommends an up-to-date modern browser: Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox, on the latest version. StemCounts runs in the browser with no plug-ins. A few features rely on your browser: PDF previews such as the Canva mood board open in the browser's own PDF viewer, and opening Canva in a new tab needs pop-ups allowed for the site. If something looks wrong, update your browser and reload the page before contacting support@stemcounts.com.

**Where:** Any device's web browser

## Where can I read the terms of service and privacy policy?

Florists and wholesalers have separate terms of service. The florist terms cover agency, purchasing, invoicing and GST; the wholesaler terms cover agency, invoicing, consumer law and GST. The florist terms are governed by the laws of Queensland, Australia. You can read them on the StemCounts website at stemcounts.com/florist-terms and stemcounts.com/wholesaler-terms, and the privacy policy at stemcounts.com/privacy. When the login steps are switched on, the current version of your terms is also shown in the app for you to accept. For questions about the terms, email support@stemcounts.com.

**Where:** stemcounts.com: Florist Terms, Wholesaler Terms and Privacy pages

## Where is my data stored?

StemCounts' privacy policy says data is stored securely using cloud service providers located in Australia and internationally, with reasonable steps taken to protect it, and appropriate safeguards where information is transferred overseas. The StemCounts app itself runs on Amazon Web Services in the Sydney region. If you connect Canva, the connection details are stored encrypted. For the full policy see stemcounts.com/privacy, and for privacy questions contact StemCounts using the details on that page.

**Where:** stemcounts.com/privacy

## What currency, region and language does StemCounts use?

StemCounts is built for Australian florists and wholesalers. Amounts are shown in Australian dollars (AUD). Arrangement budgets are entered excluding GST: the app says "All budget values are Ex GST". Phone numbers default to the Australian +61 code at sign-up, and the ABN step looks businesses up on the Australian Business Register. The app is in English only. The terms of service include a section on GST and tax credits.

**Where:** Everywhere prices and dates appear

## Will StemCounts text me about orders?

Yes. StemCounts sends text messages about orders as well as emails. The wholesaler gets a text when an order is sent to them, with a link to view and accept it, and the florist gets a text that it went and another when it is accepted. Florists can also be texted when a wholesaler cannot supply a flower. Email and in-app notifications work either way, so you never miss an order if a text does not arrive.

**Where:** Text messages to the phone number on your account
