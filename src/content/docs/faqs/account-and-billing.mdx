## What can I sign up as?

When you sign up you choose one of two cards: **Florist** ("Create and manage flower orders.") or **Wholesaler** ("Review and fulfil florist orders."). Those are the only two roles you can pick yourself. Other roles exist on the platform (for example Supplier and Admin), but only the StemCounts team can assign them. If you picked the wrong card, use **Back to Role Selection** before you finish. If you already have an account, click **Oops I have an account** to go back to sign in.

**Where:** Sign-up screen (Create an account link on the Sign In screen)

[Read more](/getting-started/creating-your-account/)

## What details do I need to create an account?

Sign-up has two steps. In **Your details** you enter **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Email**, **Phone Number** and **Password** twice (**Confirm Password**), then click **Continue →**. The phone field has a country picker that defaults to Australia (+61), and a leading 0 is dropped automatically. In **Your Organisation** you search for your business by name. You can either pick an existing organisation or choose **Create "[name]" as a new organisation**. A new organisation also asks for a **PayID Email** (you can **Use my email** or **Use a different email**). Then click **Create Account**.

**Where:** Sign-up, step 1 **Your details** and step 2 **Your organisation**

[Read more](/getting-started/creating-your-account/)

## How do my team members join our organisation?

There is no invite button inside the app. Each person creates their own account and, in the **Your Organisation** step, types at least two letters of your business name (or its order prefix) into **Search for your organisation…**. They pick your business from the results, which show the ABN and member count, and see "Joining existing organisation". Everyone in the same organisation shares its business details and its subscription. Make sure they pick the existing organisation rather than creating a new one with a similar name. If someone has joined the wrong organisation, contact support@stemcounts.com.

**Where:** Sign-up, step 2 **Your Organisation**

## What is the PayID Email asked for at sign-up?

When you create a new organisation, the form shows **PayID Email** with the note: "This is the email address that payers will use to send money to your organisation via PayID. It should be a business email, not a personal one." You can click **Use my email** to reuse your sign-up email, or **Use a different email** and type another address. The field is required and must be a valid email address. It is only asked for new organisations; if you join an existing one, its details are already set.

**Where:** Sign-up, step 2, when creating a new organisation

## What are the password rules?

At sign-up your password must have at least 8 characters, one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter and one number. If it is missing something, the form says "Password must contain:" followed by what is missing, and "Passwords do not match" if the two entries differ. The sign-in system can apply its own rules on top of these; if it rejects a password, its own message is shown explaining which rule failed. On the reset screen the form checks for at least 8 characters and matching entries.

**Where:** Sign-up **Password** field; **Reset Password** screen

## Where does my verification code arrive, and what if it doesn't work?

After **Create Account** you land on **Verify Your Account**. Enter the 6-digit code in **OTP Code** and click **Verify OTP**. The system sends this code by email, even though the screen says "Enter the OTP sent to your phone", so check your inbox and spam folder. If nothing arrives, click **Resend OTP**. Messages you may see: "That code is not correct. Please check and try again.", "That code has expired. Please request a new one.", or "Too many attempts. Please wait a few minutes and try again." On success you see "Registration successful! Logging you in..." and you are signed in.

**Where:** **Verify Your Account** screen after **Create Account**

## Do I get logged out automatically?

Your sign-in is refreshed in the background, so you are not logged out just because you have been working for a while. The app renews your session shortly before it would expire, and tries once more if a request is refused, before it sends you back to the sign-in screen. If you are logged out regularly, clear the site from your browser, sign in again, and if it keeps happening contact support@stemcounts.com.

**Where:** Anywhere in the app

## I forgot my password. How do I reset it?

On the **Sign In** screen click **Forgot password?**, enter your email and click **Send Reset Link**. You always see "If an account with that email exists, you will receive password reset instructions.", whether or not the email is registered. The email contains a link to a page where you enter your new password twice and click **Reset Password**. On success you see "Password reset successfully! Redirecting to login..." The link works for one hour and only once.

**Where:** **Sign In** > **Forgot password?**

[Read more](/getting-started/forgot-password/)

## My reset link says it is invalid or expired. What now?

Reset links last one hour and can be used only once. After that, or after a successful reset, the link stops working and you see "This reset link is invalid or has expired. Please request a new one." or "Invalid or missing reset token. Please request a new password reset link." Go back to **Forgot password?** and request a fresh link, then use the newest email. If emails are not arriving at all, contact support@stemcounts.com; the StemCounts team can also send you a reset link.

Each person has one role. **Florist** builds and sends orders, sees the florist subscription and Favourite Arrangements, and needs an active trial or subscription to use florist features. **Wholesaler** receives and fulfils orders, manages **Suppliers**, and never needs a subscription. ## Who pays for StemCounts? Is it free for wholesalers?

Florists pay a subscription for access. It belongs to the organisation, so one subscription covers everyone in that florist business. Wholesalers do not pay a subscription: they never see the subscription section and are not blocked by trial or subscription checks. Platform fees are already managed within the online pricing florists see, so a wholesaler has nothing extra to pay to use StemCounts. To get started, sign up at [https://app.stemcounts.com/register](https://app.stemcounts.com/register).

**Where:** **Settings** > **Your Organisation** > **Subscription** (florists only)

There are two florist plans, in Australian dollars: **6 months** for $60 and **12 months** for $100. Each renews automatically at the end of its period until cancelled. StemCounts can set a different price for an individual business, which then appears in the same place. There are no per-user fees: one subscription covers the whole organisation.

## How long is the free trial and when does it start?

The free trial is 7 days. It starts automatically when a new organisation is created at sign-up, and no card is needed to start it. The trial belongs to the organisation, so people who join an existing organisation later do not get a new trial. While it runs, the **Subscription** status reads **Trial: X days left** with "Your trial ends on [date]." After that it reads **Trial ended**.

Your plan then renews on the date shown in the **Subscription** section ("Renews on [date].").

## What do the subscription statuses mean?

**Trial: X days left** means your free trial is running. **Trial ended** means it is over with no subscription. **Active** means paid and working ("Renews on [date]." or "Next payment on [date]."). **Past due** means the last payment failed. **Cancelled** means the subscription has ended. **Incomplete** means "Your first payment has not gone through." **No subscription** means there is no trial or plan on record. With **Trial ended**, **Cancelled**, **Incomplete** or **No subscription** you can choose a plan and subscribe again.

## Why does it say my organisation already has a subscription?

Each organisation can have only one live subscription. If someone in your business already subscribed, you see "Your organisation already has a subscription. Manage it instead of starting a new one." If the last payment failed, you see "Your subscription has a payment outstanding. Update your payment details instead of starting a new subscription." Use **Manage subscription** to fix the card rather than subscribing again, so you are not charged twice.

**Where:** **Choose a plan** > Subscribe button

## Where can I see invoices for my flower orders?

On your home page click **Invoices** ("View invoices and order amounts from your wholesalers."). The **Invoices** page lists your sent orders (drafts are left out) with **Reference**, **Wholesaler**, **Delivery Date**, **Payment Date**, **Amount (inc. fee)** and **Status**. Click **View Invoice** to open the full invoice with the order items, subtotal, fee and total. If there are none yet you see "No invoices found." This is separate from your subscription receipts, which are in **Your Organisation**.

**Where:** Florist home page > **Invoices** tile

## What is the platform fee on my order invoices?

Order invoices show the wholesaler's price as **Subtotal**, then a **Platform fee** line (10% of the subtotal), then **Total (inc. fee)**. The fee is added once to the order total, and each line on the invoice is its share of that total, so the lines always add up to it. The fee is StemCounts' charge on products bought through the platform, separate from the subscription. The rate is fixed onto an order when it is created, so later changes never alter an order you have already been quoted. Your pricing and retail markup let you pass the fee on to your customers. Wholesalers see their own total without the fee line.

**Where:** **Invoices** > **View Invoice**
