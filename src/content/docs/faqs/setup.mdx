These are the same setup-focused FAQ entries shown inside the StemCounts app under **FAQ** in the user menu. Each entry links to a step-by-step Scribehow walkthrough where one exists.

## How do I get started with StemCounts?

After creating your account, start by setting up your organization profile in Settings. Add your business information, set your markup preferences, and connect with your wholesaler. Then explore the flower library and create your first test order to familiarize yourself with the workflow.

**Where:** `/settings` in the app · **UI element:** Settings Page

## How do I add team members to my account?

Navigate to Settings → Organization tab. Click "Add User" to invite team members by email. You can set their role (admin, florist, or wholesaler) and permissions. They'll receive an invitation email to join your organization.

**Where:** `/settings` in the app · **UI element:** Add User Button

## Can I customize the flower library for my shop?

Yes! However we are still developing some of the more advanced features - In Settings, you can mark flowers as Favorites, set custom pricing, add notes about seasonality, and hide flowers you don't typically use. This customization makes it faster to find and select flowers when building orders.

**Where:** `/flower-library` in the app · **UI element:** Flower Library Settings

## How do I connect with a wholesaler?

During this inital phase Stemcounts will manage the onboarding of Wholesalers, so if you have a spefici one in mind reach out and we will get the ball rolling.

**Where:** `/settings` in the app · **UI element:** Wholesaler Connection

## What notification settings are available?

In Settings Notifications, you can enable/disable email and in-app notifications for order status changes, delivery updates, new messages from wholesalers, and weekly order summaries. Customize which events trigger notifications.

**Where:** `/settings` in the app · **UI element:** Notification Settings

## How do I choose my account type?

Click either 'Florist' or 'Wholesaler' depending on your business type - this determines which features you'll see

**Where:** `/register` in the app · **UI element:** Role Selection

## What do the settings tabs do?

Switch between Personal (your profile), Organization (company settings), and Admin (user management) sections

**Where:** `/settings` in the app · **UI element:** Tabs

## What does the Save Changes button do in Personal settings?

Saves updates to your name, email, phone number, and notification preferences

**Where:** `/settings` in the app · **UI element:** Save Personal Button

## What does the Update Password button do?

Changes your account password after you enter your current password and a new one

**Where:** `/settings` in the app · **UI element:** Update Password Button

## What does the Save Changes button do in Organization settings?
