**Assign & Send** is the middle stage of the board, and the most
complex. Three things happen here:

1. **Aggregate** — combine the same flower across all accepted orders
2. **Assign** — pick a supplier for each aggregated flower
3. **Send** — generate and send supplier orders, then track for
   confirmation

<HelpMedia id="assign-and-send-overview" kind="video" caption="Walking through Assign & Send end to end" />

## The three tables

The page is divided into three tables that you work through top to
bottom:

### 1. Combined Flower Requirements (unassigned)

Every flower you need this week, aggregated across all florist orders
into a single row per `(flower, colour)`. Columns:

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Flower name | e.g. "David Austin Rose" |
| Colour | e.g. "Blush" |
| Stems per bunch | Pack size |
| Total bunches | Rounded up from total stems |
| Total stems | Sum across all florist orders |
| Delivery date(s) | When you need it |
| **Supplier** | Empty dropdown — click to select |

When you pick a supplier, that flower moves out of this table and into
the next one.

<HelpMedia id="assign-supplier-dropdown" caption="Selecting a supplier for an aggregated flower row" />

### 2. Send to Supplier (assigned, not yet sent)

Flowers grouped by selected supplier. Each supplier-group has a **Send**
button.

<Steps>

1. Review the lines under each supplier — quantities, dates, notes.

2. Click **Send**. StemCounts generates a supplier order and emails it (or sends it directly to your integrated supplier if you have one set up).

3. You'll see: **"Sent to Supplier"** confirmation.

4. The flowers move to the third table.

</Steps>

### 3. Sent to Supplier — Not Confirmed

Supplier orders you've sent but the supplier hasn't yet confirmed.
Wait for the supplier to confirm or click **Confirm** manually once
they email you.

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | Waiting on supplier |
| **Confirmed** | Supplier acknowledged; flowers are now sourced |

<HelpMedia id="confirm-supplier-order" caption="Confirming a supplier order moves it to Confirmed & Ready" />

Once confirmed, the lines disappear from this table and land in the
**Confirmed & Ready** column on the board.

## Per-flower-type assignment

Assignment is **per flower, per colour**, not per order. This means:

- Five different florists ordering red roses = one aggregated row, one
  supplier
- One florist ordering red roses + white roses = two rows, potentially
  two suppliers

## Choosing a supplier

The supplier dropdown shows everyone in your **Suppliers** list
(configured in Settings). If a flower needs a specific supplier and
they're missing, add them in **Settings → Suppliers**.

<Aside type="tip">
For staple stems, default to the same supplier every week. For
special-occasion stems or hard-to-source colours, check who has it
seasonally before assigning.
</Aside>

## Substitutions

If a supplier can't fulfil a flower, you've got two options:

1. Re-assign to a different supplier — move the row back to the first table
2. Talk to your florist directly about a substitution

A built-in substitution flow is on the way. See [the substitution FAQ](/faqs/wholesalers/) for more.

## What the florist sees during this stage

- **Accepted** — until at least one of their flowers is sent to a supplier
- Each flower has its own status, moving along: assigned → sent → pending → confirmed

When *every* line is confirmed, the order overall becomes **Supply
Confirmed** on the florist side.
