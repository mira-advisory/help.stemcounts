A new order from a florist lands in **New Orders**. Your first job is
to open it, look it over, and accept it (or, rarely, reject it). You
accept or reject the whole order at once.

## Opening a new order

Click the order in the Calendar, Board or List view of your Order
Summary. It opens in a window with:

- The order reference at the top, with the **Download order PDF**
  printer icon, the **Authorisation letter** button and the **Chat**
  button beside it
- One line saying what to do next. For a new order that is **Review
  order details before accepting or rejecting.**
- **Pickup Date**, **Event Name**, **Florist**, **Wholesaler** and
  **Total**, plus the **Florist note** if they left one
- An **Inventory List** of every flower, with its colour, stem length
  where the florist asked for one, stems, bunches and line cost. Notes
  the florist left against a flower show under it as **Florist note**

## Accepting

<Steps>

1. Read through the Inventory List. Can you source everything by the
   pickup date? Do the quantities look right?

   <Tabs syncKey="device">
     <TabItem label="Computer">
       <HelpMedia id="accept-desktop-05-order-detail-bottom" caption="Scroll to the foot of the order: the last flower lines, then Reject Order and Accept Order." />
     </TabItem>
     <TabItem label="Phone">
       <HelpMedia id="accept-phone-02-order-accept" caption="Open the order: the flowers and the florist's note, then Accept order at the bottom. Reject order is under More." />
     </TabItem>
   </Tabs>

2. Click **Accept Order**.

   <HelpMedia id="accept-desktop-06-accept-reject-buttons" caption="Click Accept Order to take the order, or Reject Order if you cannot supply it." />

3. You will see **Order accepted**, and the order moves to **Accepted**.
   The florist is notified in the app.

   <HelpMedia id="accept-desktop-07-accepted-order" caption="An accepted order: the status badge reads Accepted and the line under the number tells you the next step, Confirm supply for each flower below." />

</Steps>

Accepting means you have taken the order on. It does not confirm
supply. That is the next step, and it depends on your tier: see
[Confirming supply](/wholesalers/confirming-supply/). On Tier 1 there is
no supply step, and the next button is **Mark Delivered**.

<Aside type="note">
If a single flower is a problem, accept the order anyway. Then use
**Can't Supply** (Tier 3) or **Actions** (Tier 2) on that flower line
to offer substitutes, offer fewer stems, or mark it unavailable. The
rest of the order carries on. See
[Confirming supply](/wholesalers/confirming-supply/).
</Aside>

## Rejecting

<Steps>

1. Open the order.

2. Click **Reject Order**. On a phone you are asked **Reject this
   order?** first.

3. You will see **Order rejected**. The order leaves your board.

</Steps>

The florist gets an in-app notification, **Order rejected**. No email
is sent for a rejection, and the order does not go back to the
florist's drafts. If they still want the flowers, they will need to
talk to you or send a new order.

Where you can, accept and sort out the detail through
[order chat](/wholesalers/order-chat/) rather than rejecting.

## Accepting a whole week at once

On the Board, drag a week's banner from **New Orders** to **Accepted**.
StemCounts opens each order in that week in turn in a review window
(**Review order details before accepting or rejecting.**), and you
accept them one by one.

## Accepting from the email

You can also accept from the **Accept this order** button in the order
email, without logging in. See
[Accepting from the email](/wholesalers/accepting-from-the-email/).

## On a phone

The order opens as its own page. The one big button at the bottom reads
**Accept order**. **Reject order** sits under **More**, along with
**Message the florist**, **Order PDF** and **Authorisation letter**.
