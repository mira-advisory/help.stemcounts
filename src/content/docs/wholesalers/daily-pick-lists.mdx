**Pick-lists** are paper-friendly views of an order, designed for the
warehouse floor: walk along the cool-room shelves, grab the stems, tick
them off.

<HelpMedia id="pick-list-printable" caption="A printable pick-list for one florist's order" />

## What's on a pick-list

Each pick-list page covers **one florist order**. It shows:

- Order reference (e.g. `W-1234`)
- Florist name and contact
- Delivery date and method (delivery / pickup)
- Event date and venue (per event in the order)
- Sortable flower table:

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Flower name | Display name |
| Colour | Variant |
| Total stems | How many to pick |
| Bunches | Pack count |
| Price | Cost line (for cross-reference) |
| Notes | Per-line notes from the florist or your team |

Pick-lists are stripped of platform chrome so they print cleanly on A4
or letter paper.

## Printing a pick-list

<Steps>

1. Open an order from the Order Board or Order Summary.

2. Click **Print Pick-List** at the top of the order detail page.

3. Your browser's print dialog opens. From here:

   - **Print to a physical printer** for the warehouse
   - **Save as PDF** for archival or sending to a fulfilment partner

</Steps>

The page URL is stable (`/orders/{ref}/print`), so you can bookmark
specific pick-lists or share them with staff who have a StemCounts
login.

## Bulk pick-lists for a week

For day-end planning, the **Calendar view** in Order Summary lets you
print all pick-lists for a day or week:

<Steps>

1. Switch to Calendar view in Order Summary.

2. Pick a day or week.

3. Click **Print All**.

4. Each florist order gets a separate page in the print job.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
For warehouses with multiple shifts, print twice — once at the start of
day for the floor team, once at end-of-day reconciliation for the
office team.
</Aside>

## Format tweaks

The pick-list uses your browser's print stylesheet. To adjust:

- **Page orientation** — landscape fits more columns; portrait fits
  more rows
- **Margins** — narrow margins maximise usable area
- **Background colours** — most printers default to "off"; enable
  *Background Graphics* for status badges and headers to render in
  colour

## What's NOT on the pick-list

- The full supplier breakdown (the warehouse picks from your stock, not
  from individual supplier deliveries)
- Cost-side platform fees
- Subscription / billing info

Use the Order Summary view for those, not the pick-list.

## Outside-the-app printing

If you need to share with someone without a StemCounts login, save as
PDF and email the file. The pick-list URL itself requires authentication.
