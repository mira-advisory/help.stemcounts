Your **Suppliers** are the growers and suppliers your organisation buys
from. On Tier 3, the supplier dropdown on every flower line of an order
lists exactly this set, so keep it current.

## Where it lives

Go to **Settings > Suppliers**, under **Ordering**. The page describes
itself as "The growers and suppliers your organisation buys from." Until
you add one, it says **No suppliers yet**.

## Adding a supplier

<Steps>

1. Click **Add supplier**.

2. Fill in **Supplier name**, **Contact first name**, **Contact last
   name** and **Contact email**. **Contact phone** is optional.

3. Click **Add supplier** to save.

</Steps>

The supplier now appears in the list, and in the supplier dropdown on
any accepted order.

## The list

The list shows **Name**, **Contact Name** and **Contact Email**, with a
**Search** box above it. Each row has **Edit supplier** and **Delete
supplier**. Opening a supplier also offers **Orders and details**, which
shows what you have ordered from that supplier.

On a phone the same list is a set of rows: tap one to edit it.

<Aside type="note">
StemCounts does not send anything to your suppliers. A supplier here is
a contact record used to track where each flower is coming from.
Suppliers have no screens in StemCounts, no order board and no emails
from it. Your pricing and ordering with them stay between you and the
supplier.
</Aside>

## What this is used for

On an accepted order, choosing a supplier on a flower line records that
the flower is coming from them, and the supplier's name shows against
the line for the rest of the order. For a flower you supply from your
own stock, choose **Not applicable** instead. See
[Confirming supply](/wholesalers/confirming-supply/).
