Every order has its own conversation with the florist who sent it. Use
it for the questions that used to need a phone call: a colour you want
to check, a quantity that looks odd, a pickup time.

## Opening the chat

<Steps>

1. Open the order from your Order Summary.

2. Click **Chat**, beside the order reference at the top. The button
   shows a count of unread messages when there are any. On a phone, tap
   **Message the florist** under **More**.

   <Tabs syncKey="device">
     <TabItem label="Computer">

3. On a wide screen the conversation opens beside the order, so you can
   keep the flowers in view while you type. On a narrower screen it
   opens over the order. On a phone it fills the screen.

## Sending a message

Type in the box at the bottom (**Chat with [florist]**) and press the
send arrow. The florist gets a **New message** notification in their
app. Until anyone has written, the panel says **No messages yet.**

You can keep more than one thread on an order. Click **New topic**,
give it a **Topic Name**, for example "Pickup time" or "Rose colour",
and write the first message.

## When the florist replies

You get a **New message** notification in the bell at the top of the
app, and the unread count appears on the **Chat** button of that order.
Click the notification to go straight to the order.

<Aside type="note">
Chat is not available on Tier 1, where orders are handled by email and
the order link rather than a login. Reply to the order email instead:
the florist who sent the order is copied in, and replies go to them.
</Aside>

## On a phone

Open the order, then tap **Message the florist** under **More**.
