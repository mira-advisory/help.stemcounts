When you log in as a wholesaler, you land on the **wholesaler
dashboard**. Two primary destinations dominate the page:

- **Order Board** — the Kanban-style workflow view
- **Order Summary** — calendar + board hybrid for week-level planning

<HelpMedia id="wholesaler-landing-overview" kind="video" caption="A quick look at the wholesaler home screen" />

## The two destinations

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Order Board">
    Drag-and-drop Kanban with columns for each stage of fulfilment.
    Best for handling individual orders one at a time. See [The Order
    Board](/wholesalers/the-order-board/).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Order Summary">
    Switch between **Calendar view** (orders by week, then by day) and
    **Board view**. Best for week-level planning and seeing total volume
    across all florists.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Inventory at a glance

The landing page surfaces an inventory indicator: how many flowers are
**selected** in your library and available to florists. If it reads
zero, you haven't picked any stems to sell yet — see [Managing your
inventory](/wholesalers/managing-inventory/).

## Florist count

Beneath the Order Board card, you'll see a count of **florists with
active orders this week**. Tap or click through to filter the board to
a specific florist.

## What you won't find on the landing page

A few things live elsewhere on purpose:

| Thing | Where it actually lives |
|---|---|
| Detailed financials / invoicing | **Settings → Wholesale Orders** (admin) |
| Supplier price negotiation | Direct with your suppliers, off StemCounts |
| Subscription billing | Wholesalers don't need a StemCounts subscription |
| Per-florist message thread | Coming later |
