When you log in as a wholesaler you land on your home page. It opens
with a welcome banner and three counts, then a set of cards that open
everything else.

## The three counts

| Count | What it means |
|---|---|
| **New Orders** | Orders from florists waiting for you to accept or reject |
| **In Progress** | Orders you have accepted that are not yet delivered |
| **Due This Week** | Orders with a delivery date in the current week (Monday to Sunday) that are not yet delivered |

## The cards

| Card | What it opens |
|---|---|
| **Order Summary** | Your orders page, opening in the Calendar view |
| **Order Board** | The same orders page, opening in the Board view |
| **Invoices** | Your florist orders with their amounts and status |
| **Inventory Management** | The flowers you carry, with your prices and stems per bunch. The small counter on the card shows how many flowers you have selected against how many you have not |
| **Settings** | Your profile, your organisation and your suppliers |
| **Help guides** | This help site, in a new tab |

**Order Summary** and **Order Board** open the same page. The only
difference is which view it starts in. See
[Order Summary: Calendar, Board and List](/wholesalers/the-order-board/).

In the top menu, **Orders** also takes you straight to the Order
Summary.

## On a phone

The phone home shows the same three counts as tiles, then one list of
rows: **Order Board**, **Library** (your inventory), **Invoices**,
**Settings** and **Help guides**. There is no separate Order Summary row
on a phone, because the summary and the board open the same orders
list.

<Aside type="tip">
If **Inventory Management** shows zero flowers selected, florists have
nothing of yours to order. Start with
[Managing your inventory](/wholesalers/managing-inventory/).
</Aside>

## Where to next

If today is your first day, the easiest path is:

1. [Managing your inventory](/wholesalers/managing-inventory/), so
   florists can order from you.
2. [Your suppliers](/wholesalers/suppliers/), if you confirm supply
   flower by flower.
3. [Accepting an order](/wholesalers/accepting-an-order/), for when the
   first order lands.
