**Receive & Accept** is the first stage of the board. Florist orders
arrive here once sent. Your job: review and accept (or, rarely, reject).

<HelpMedia id="receive-and-accept-view" caption="The Receive & Accept stage" />

## What you see

The Receive & Accept page shows incoming orders with status **Sent**
or already **Accepted** but not yet progressed. For each:

- Florist name (and their organisation)
- Delivery date
- Total value
- Number of stems
- A status badge

You can drill into any order to see the full line-by-line breakdown
before accepting.

## Accept an order

<Steps>

1. Click into an order to see the full detail — every event, every
   arrangement, every flower line.

2. Review the stems. Check:

   - Can you source everything by the delivery date?
   - Does the florist have unusual requirements (specific varieties,
     spares, notes)?
   - Are quantities sensible?

3. Click **Accept Order**.

   <HelpMedia id="accept-order-button" />

4. You'll see a confirmation toast: **"Order W-1234 has been accepted
   and moved to Assign & Send."** (your order reference, not literally
   "W-1234")

5. The order disappears from this column and lands in **Assign & Send**.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
Accept is **order-level only**. You can't accept some lines and reject
others. If a single line is problematic, accept the order and either
work it out with the florist directly or substitute that line later
(when substitution ships).
</Aside>

## Reject an order

Rejection sends the whole order back to the florist as a draft. They
can edit and re-send, or contact you to discuss.

<Steps>

1. Open the order detail modal.

2. Click **Reject Order**.

3. Confirm. You'll see: **"Order W-1234 has been rejected."** (your
   actual order reference appears in place of "W-1234")

4. The order disappears from your board.

</Steps>

Reject sparingly. It's better to accept and resolve issues by direct
contact unless you genuinely can't fulfil any of the order.

## Empty state

If there are no orders to process, the page shows:

> No orders found
>
> No assigned orders are currently available

This is normal at the start of a week or after a busy session.

## What "accepted" means on the florist side

When you accept an order, the florist's view updates to **Accepted**.
They get an email notification. They expect to see **Supply Confirmed**
next, which happens after you move stems through *Assign & Send*.

## Cut-offs

Each wholesaler has a **cut-off time** for accepting orders. Orders
arriving after the cut-off may need to be flagged for the next sourcing
window. The platform doesn't enforce a cut-off automatically — you set
the policy with florists directly.
