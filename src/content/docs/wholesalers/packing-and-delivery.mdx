Once supply is confirmed, the last steps are packing the order and
marking it delivered. Which buttons you see depends on your tier.

<Tabs>

    confirmed flower with a **Mark Packed** button.

    <Steps>

       lines move under **Packed** and show **Supplier Fulfilled**.

    If any flower is not yet confirmed or removed, the order button
    reads **Confirm flowers first**. Finish
    [confirming supply](/wholesalers/confirming-supply/), then come back.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Tier 2: deliver">
    After **Confirm Order**, the order says **Order confirmed. Mark
    delivered once pickup or delivery is complete.** There is no
    per-flower packing step.

    Once the florist has the flowers, click **Mark Delivered**. The
    order moves to **Completed** and shows **Complete** to the florist.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Tier 1: deliver">
    After **Accept Order**, the order says **Accepted. Mark it
    delivered once it is picked up or delivered.**

    Click **Mark Delivered** when the flowers are with the florist.
  </TabItem>

<Aside type="caution">
**Mark Delivered** and **Mark Supplier Fulfilled** are disabled while a
flower is waiting on the florist's reply to a substitute or partial
quantity offer. The button reads **Awaiting Florist** until they answer.
</Aside>

## A whole week at once (Tier 3)

On the Board, drag a week's banner from **Supply Confirmed** to
**Supplier Fulfilled**. **Mark Ready for Dispatch** opens for that
week. Its **Confirmed Flower Preparation** table lists each flower with
**Bunches / Stems**, **Supplier**, **Prep Day** and **Status**. **Prep
Day** is the weekday (Monday to Friday) you will prepare that flower for
dispatch. Select lines to set a prep day or status for several at once.
When every line is set to fulfilled with a prep day, **Mark Ready for
Dispatch** moves the week's orders on.

To complete a whole week, drag its banner from **Supplier Fulfilled**
to **Completed**. Every order in that week is completed.

## The florist can also confirm delivery

Once your part of an order is **Supply Confirmed** or **Ready**, the
florist can confirm what arrived on their side, flower by flower or all
at once. When they confirm every line, your part of the order becomes
**Complete** and you get a **Delivery confirmed** notification. So an
order can reach **Completed** without you clicking **Mark Delivered**.

## On a phone

The one big button at the bottom of the order reads **Mark supplier
fulfilled** or **Mark delivered**, depending on the stage. To pack flower
by flower, open **Pack flower by flower** under **More**. It opens the
**Flowers** sheet; swipe a row sideways for its **Mark Packed** button.
