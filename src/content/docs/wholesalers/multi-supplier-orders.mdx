A typical florist order spans multiple flower types. Each type can be
sourced from a different supplier. StemCounts handles this naturally
by tracking each flower line independently.

<HelpMedia id="multi-supplier-diagram" caption="One florist order → multiple supplier orders → reconstituted at handover" />

## How it works in practice

Imagine a florist has placed an order containing:

- 100 stems of red roses
- 50 stems of white lilies
- 200 stems of eucalyptus

You source from three suppliers:

- Roses → **Supplier A**
- Lilies → **Supplier B**
- Eucalyptus → **Supplier C**

Behind the scenes:

1. The florist order = **one** AssignedOrder in StemCounts
2. Each flower aggregates across all your accepted florist orders (so
   if two other florists also ordered red roses, those 100 stems
   combine into a single supplier order line)
3. Three supplier orders are created — one per supplier
4. Each supplier order has its own status (sent → confirmed →
   fulfilled)

## What this means for status tracking

Because each line is tracked independently, the florist order's
**overall status is the least-advanced status of any of its lines**.

Example: an order with 9 lines confirmed and 1 line still pending shows
as **Sent** on the florist side until that last line catches up.

<Aside type="note">
This is also why the Order Board can show an order in *Confirmed* but a
specific line is still moving through *Assign & Send* — the per-line
view is the ground truth.
</Aside>

## Partial delivery to the florist

You can mark **some** flowers as ready for the florist while others are
still with suppliers. The florist sees per-line **Ready** badges and
can come pick up early or wait for the whole order.

This works well for:

- Foliage and base stems available early (sent up front)
- Specialty flowers with longer lead time (sent later)

## When a supplier can't deliver

If a supplier fails to deliver a line, you have three options:

1. **Re-assign** to a different supplier (drag the line back to *Assign
   & Send*)
2. **Substitute** outside the platform — agreed with the florist by
   phone or email; manually adjust the line in StemCounts to reflect
   what's actually being delivered
3. **Reject the line** — sends a notification to the florist that this
   flower isn't available

In-platform substitution UI is on the roadmap. For now, use options 1
or 2.

## Reporting

The Order Summary view (calendar mode) shows aggregate stem counts
across all suppliers for any selected week. Useful for forecasting
volume.

## Best practices

- For volume stems (roses, eucalyptus), commit to one or two primary
  suppliers per week
- For unusual stems, lean on relationships outside the platform; assign
  to the right supplier once you've confirmed by phone
- Use the *Florist view* in **Confirmed & Ready** to spot when one
  florist's order is mostly ready but one line is lagging
