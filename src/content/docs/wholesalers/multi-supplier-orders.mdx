A florist order usually spans several flowers, and any one of them can
come from a different supplier. On Tier 3, StemCounts handles that by
tracking each flower line on its own.

## How it works

Imagine a florist has ordered:

- 100 stems of red roses
- 50 stems of white lilies
- 200 stems of eucalyptus

In the order's **Inventory List**, pick a supplier on each line:

- Roses from **Supplier A**
- Lilies from **Supplier B**
- Eucalyptus from your own stock, so **Not applicable**

Then click **Confirm** on each line. If most lines share one supplier,
use **Set supplier for all** in the header and change the exceptions
afterwards.

StemCounts groups the lines by supplier behind the scenes and shows the
supplier name against each flower for the rest of the order. It does
not send anything to your suppliers: you order from them the way you
always have.

## The order waits for its last flower

The order moves to **Supply Confirmed** only when every flower line is
confirmed or removed. So one slow line holds the whole order at
**Accepted**, and the florist sees **Accepted** until that last flower
is confirmed.

Each flower has its own status as well. On your side a confirmed line
shows **Confirmed**, a line waiting on the florist shows **Awaiting
Florist**, and a removed line shows **Not Supplied**. The florist sees
**Supply OK** against each confirmed flower, so they can watch the order
fill in line by line.

<Aside type="note">
If the florist's order went to more than one wholesaler, their overall
order status follows whichever wholesaler is furthest behind. That is
their view, not yours: your part of the order moves as soon as you move
it.
</Aside>

## When a supplier lets you down

If a supplier cannot deliver a flower after all:

1. If you can get it elsewhere and the line is not yet confirmed,
   change the supplier in the dropdown and confirm it as normal. Once a
   line is confirmed its supplier is locked, so if you source it
   elsewhere the order still shows the supplier you first chose. The
   florist only sees that the flower is confirmed.
2. If you cannot get it, open **Can't Supply** on the line and choose
   **Offer Substitutes**, **Partial Quantity** or **Unavailable**. See
   [Confirming supply](/wholesalers/confirming-supply/).

## Packing when suppliers arrive at different times

Once the order is **Supply Confirmed**, each line has its own **Mark
Packed** button, so you can pack the eucalyptus on Monday and the roses
when Supplier A delivers on Wednesday. The order becomes **Supplier
Fulfilled** when you click **Mark Supplier Fulfilled**. See
[Packing and delivery](/wholesalers/packing-and-delivery/).
