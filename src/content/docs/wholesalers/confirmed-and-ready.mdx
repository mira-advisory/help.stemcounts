**Confirmed & Ready** is the final wholesaler-managed stage. Two
sub-sections:

1. **Confirmed by Supplier** — suppliers have confirmed but haven't
   fulfilled yet
2. **Ready for Florist** — flowers are with you, ready to hand over

<HelpMedia id="confirmed-and-ready-overview" caption="Both sub-sections visible on the same page" />

## Confirmed by Supplier

Supplier orders with status **Confirmed**. Grouped by supplier, then by
delivery date.

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Flower name, colour, pack size, bunches, stems | Standard line detail |
| **Status** | "Confirmed" (yellow badge) |

### Marking supplier-fulfilled

When the supplier delivers a line to your warehouse:

<Steps>

1. Find the supplier order row.

2. Click **Mark as Supplier Fulfilled**.

   <HelpMedia id="mark-supplier-fulfilled" />

3. The lines move to **Ready for Florist** below.

</Steps>

## Ready for Florist

Two view modes here — choose whichever fits your workflow.

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="List view">
    Flat table of every flower with status **Fulfilled**. Columns: name,
    colour, pack size, bunches/stems, delivery date, status, supplier, and
    a per-row checkbox.

    Use the **checkbox** to mark a single flower as **delivered** to the
    florist. This moves the line to *Delivered* on the board.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Florist view">
    Same data, **grouped by florist**. Best for day-end pick lists and
    handovers — "here's everything for Smith Florist this week".

    Use the floral header buttons to quickly mark all of one florist's
    fulfilled flowers as delivered.
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

<HelpMedia id="ready-florist-view-toggle" caption="Toggle between List view and Florist view" />

## Per-flower ready toggle

Marking ready is **per flower line**, not per order. This means a
florist's order can be **partially Ready** — some flowers are ready
for pickup while others are still confirmed but not fulfilled.

The florist's order status updates as each line transitions:

| Florist-side status | When it appears |
|---|---|
| **Supply Confirmed** | All lines are at least *Confirmed* |
| **Ready** | All lines are *Delivered* (handed to the florist) |

## Sharing a status update with the florist

The florist sees status changes automatically — no manual action needed.
Email notifications go out when:

- Their order is fully accepted
- Their order is fully ready

For mid-week updates outside those triggers, message or call the florist
directly (in-platform messaging not yet shipped).

## Empty states

If both sub-sections are empty, you've worked through everything for
the current visible timeframe. Switch the filter to a different week to
check upcoming work.

## What "delivered" means

**Delivered** = the flowers have left your warehouse and are with the
florist. Whether that's pickup or delivery is determined by the
florist's preference and your local policy.

<Aside type="note">
Once an order is fully Delivered, it can't be reverted. Make sure you've
ticked the right florist's lines before marking ready.
</Aside>
