The **Order Board** is your view of every order moving through
fulfilment. Columns sit left to right in the order work happens —
grab an order and move it along as you complete each step.

<HelpMedia id="order-board-overview" kind="video" caption="The Order Board in action" />

## Columns by tier

StemCounts has three wholesaler tiers, and your board's columns reflect
the workflow you actually run.

| Tier | Columns | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **Integrated** | Sent · Accepted · Confirmed · Fulfilled · Delivered | Full workflow including supplier procurement |
| **Non-integrated** | Sent · Accepted · Delivered | Skips supplier procurement (you source directly) |
| **Shadow** | Accepted · Delivered | A minimal view, used where StemCounts is mirroring a workflow you run elsewhere |

<Aside type="note">
Not sure which tier you're on? Count the columns on your board. Your
tier is set when your account's created. If you ever need to change
it, get in touch with us and we'll sort it.
</Aside>

## What each column means

- **Sent** — order has arrived from the florist, waiting for you to accept
- **Accepted** — you've accepted; time to assign stems to suppliers
- **Confirmed** — your supplier(s) have confirmed availability
- **Fulfilled** — supplier has delivered to you; flowers are ready for the florist
- **Delivered** — flowers handed over; all done

## Drag-and-drop

Grab an order and move it to the next column. It has exactly the same
effect as clicking the **Accept** or **Mark Ready** button on the order
itself — whichever feels more natural in the moment.

<HelpMedia id="order-board-drag" kind="video" caption="Dragging an order from Sent to Accepted" />

You can also drag a **whole week** at once by grabbing the week banner
at the top of a column. Handy when a batch of orders is ready to move
together.

<Aside type="caution">
The board only allows valid moves. You can't drag an order straight
from *Sent* to *Delivered* — it'll need to flow through the stages in
between.
</Aside>

## Calendar view

Need to plan by week or day instead? Switch from Board to **Calendar**
in the Order Summary view. Orders are grouped by week then by day —
useful for spotting busy windows or capacity squeezes.

## Filtering the board

Up the top of the board you'll find filters for:

- **Week** — focus on a specific week
- **Florist** — single-florist view, useful when you're on the phone with one
- **Status** — hide columns you're not working in right now

## Empty state

- An empty **Sent** column means no new orders to process — relax for a minute
- An empty **Accepted** column means you've moved everything on — nicely done
- An empty board overall means nothing's open in the window you're viewing; switch the week filter to check what's coming
