The **Order Summary** is where you work your orders. It has three
views, switched at the top of the page: **Calendar**, **Board** and
**List**. Every view shows the same orders. Clicking an order in any of
them opens that order, where you accept it, confirm supply and complete
it.

<HelpMedia id="ready-florist-view-toggle" caption="The Order Summary banner with its Calendar, Board and List switch, here with Board selected." />

## The three views

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Calendar">
    The default view. Your orders sit in the **Orders and Weekly
    Planner**, laid out by delivery date across a run of weeks (twelve
    by default, and you can choose how many to show). Use it to spot
    busy weeks and days coming up. Click an order to open it.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Board">
    Orders grouped into columns, one per stage, left to right in the
    order work happens. Inside each column, orders are grouped by
    delivery week, and each column shows a count. The **Order Board**
    card on your home page opens straight into this view.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="List">
    A searchable table with **Order Reference**, **Florist**,
    **Status**, **Amount** and **Delivery Date**. Best when you know the
    order you want.
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Filters

Every view has a search box (**Search florists, events or orders**),
plus a **Florist** filter and a **Status** filter. Board and List can also be
sorted by **Delivery Date** or **Received Date**. Click the sort button
again to flip the direction.

## The Board columns

The full set of columns is:

| Column | What it means |
|---|---|
| **New Orders** | A new order from a florist, waiting for you to act |
| **Accepted** | You have accepted the order and are sourcing it |
| **Supply Confirmed** | Supply is confirmed for every flower |
| **Supplier Fulfilled** | The order is packed and ready for delivery or pickup |
| **Completed** | Delivered and done |

Which columns you see depends on how your account is set up. StemCounts
sets each wholesaler to one of three tiers:

| Tier | Columns you see | How an order moves |
|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1** | New Orders, Accepted, Completed | Orders arrive by email and link. Accept, then mark delivered |
| **Tier 2** | New Orders, Accepted, Supply Confirmed, Completed | Accept, confirm the whole order, then mark delivered |
| **Tier 3** | All five | Accept, confirm each flower with its supplier, pack, mark supplier fulfilled, then mark delivered |

<Aside type="note">
Not sure which tier you are on? Count the columns on your Board, or
open an accepted order: a **Confirm Order** button means Tier 2, and a
supplier dropdown on each flower line means Tier 3. Your tier is set on
your account by StemCounts. To change it, email
**support@stemcounts.com**.
</Aside>

## Dragging on the Board

You can drag an order one column to the right. Dragging anywhere else
shows **Drag to the next workflow stage to continue.**

Dragging a single order does not move it on its own. It opens the
order, where you finish the step (for example, **Accept Order**). The
order moves once you have done that.

You can also drag a whole week's group of orders at once, by its week
banner:

| Drag a week from | To | What opens |
|---|---|---|
| **New Orders** | **Accepted** | A review window for each order in turn, so you accept them one by one |
| **Accepted** | **Supply Confirmed** | The week's **Accepted Workflow**, which lists the week's flowers in a **Flower Requirements** table so you can set suppliers and confirm lines together |
| **Supply Confirmed** | **Supplier Fulfilled** | **Mark Ready for Dispatch** for the week, where each flower gets a **Prep Day** and a status |
| **Supplier Fulfilled** | **Completed** | Every order in that week is completed |

The eye icon on a week banner opens a **Week Overview**: every order in
that week and its combined flower requirements.

<Aside type="caution">
An order cannot reach **Supply Confirmed** until every flower on it is
confirmed. If you drag a week across and see **This order cannot move
to Supply Confirmed until all flowers are confirmed.**, open that order
and finish confirming it. See
[Confirming supply](/wholesalers/confirming-supply/).
</Aside>

## On a phone

On a phone the orders page is a single list. Search by florist, event
or order reference, filter by **Order stage** with the pill row, and
orders are grouped by delivery week. Tap an order to open it.
