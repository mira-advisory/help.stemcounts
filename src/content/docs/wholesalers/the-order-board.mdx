The **Order Board** is the Kanban view of every order moving through
fulfilment. Each column is a stage; orders flow left to right as work
progresses.

<HelpMedia id="order-board-overview" caption="The Order Board for an integrated wholesaler — five columns" />

## Columns depend on your tier

StemCounts has three wholesaler tiers, and each sees a different set
of columns. This is intentional — the columns map to the workflow each
tier actually runs.

| Tier | Columns | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| **Integrated** | Sent · Accepted · Confirmed · Fulfilled · Delivered | Full workflow including supplier procurement |
| **Non-integrated** | Sent · Accepted · Delivered | Skips the supplier procurement steps (you source stems directly) |
| **Shadow** | Accepted · Delivered | Minimal — used for non-platform wholesalers being mirrored for tracking |

<Aside type="note">
If you're not sure which tier you're on, check the column count on your
board. Your tier is set by StemCounts admin when your account is
provisioned and doesn't change without a manual update.
</Aside>

## What each column means

- **Sent** — order has arrived from the florist, awaiting your accept
- **Accepted** — you've accepted; stems need to be assigned to suppliers
- **Confirmed** — your supplier(s) have confirmed availability
- **Fulfilled** — supplier has fulfilled; flowers are with you ready for
  the florist
- **Delivered** — flowers handed over to the florist; complete

## Drag-and-drop

Orders are dragged column to column. Each move triggers the
appropriate status transition behind the scenes — same as if you'd
clicked the explicit *Accept* / *Mark Ready* buttons.

<HelpMedia id="order-board-drag" caption="Dragging an order from Sent to Accepted" />

You can also drag **a whole week** (the week banner at the top of a
column) to bulk-move every order in that week.

<Aside type="caution">
The board respects allowed transitions only. You can't drag an order
from *Sent* directly to *Delivered* — it has to flow through the
intermediate stages.
</Aside>

## Calendar view

Switch from Board to **Calendar** in the Order Summary view. Calendar
shows orders grouped by week, then by day. Useful for seeing daily
volume and spotting capacity issues.

## Filtering the board

The top of the board has filters for:

- **Week** — limit to specific weeks
- **Florist** — single-florist view (useful for talking to one customer)
- **Status** — hide columns you don't care about right now

## Empty states

- An empty **Sent** column means no new orders to process
- An empty **Accepted** column means everything you've accepted has
  moved on — congratulations
- An empty board overall means no orders at all in the visible
  timeframe
