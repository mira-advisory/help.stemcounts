Your inventory is the list of flowers you make available to florists on
StemCounts, with your price per bunch and your stems per bunch for
each. It is built on the StemCounts flower library. Florists are
offered your in-stock flowers, in the colours you have made available,
at your prices.

## Where it lives

Open the **Inventory Management** card on your home page. The counter
on the card shows how many flowers you have selected against how many
you have not.

On a phone the same page is called **Library**.

## The four views

Filter the list with the buttons across the top. Each shows a count.

| Button | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **My Inventory** | The flowers you carry |
| **Available to Add** | Flowers, or colours of flowers you already carry, that you could add |
| **Differs from Library** | Flowers where your saved price or stems per bunch differ from the library's |
| **All** | Every flower in the library |

On a phone the same views are **Mine**, **To add**, **Differs** and
**All**.

## Adding a flower

<Steps>

1. Find the flower in **All** or **Available to Add**. Search or filter
   to narrow the list.

2. Select it with the round tick at the start of the row.

3. In **Variants**, tick the colours you carry. Only the colours you
   tick are offered to florists for that flower.

   <HelpMedia id="inventory-desktop-08-colour-rows-expanded" caption="Click a flower to open its colours. Tick the colours you stock; each colour can carry its own bunch size." />

4. Type your **Price (Bunch)** in dollars and your **Stems per bunch**.
   A new flower needs both before it will save.

5. Click **Save Inventory**.

</Steps>

Each row also has a **Name** column, your internal name for the flower,
beside the library's **Flower** name.

## Changing prices and stems per bunch

Type the new value straight into the row. Changes are not saved as you
type: a **Save Inventory** button appears with a count of the flowers
you have changed. Click it when you are done.

Stems per bunch can also be set per colour variant. A colour with its
own stems per bunch, or its own price, is ordered and billed at those
figures ahead of the flower's.

If your stems per bunch differ from the library's, you are asked to
**Confirm Stems Per Bunch Change** before saving. Click **Continue with
Save** to keep your figure. On a phone the same check is called **Stems
differ from the library**, with a **Save my bunch size** button.

<Aside type="note">
On a phone, tapping a flower opens a sheet for that one flower, and
each value saves when you close its row. There is no page-wide Save.
</Aside>

## Bulk upload from a CSV

For a long list, use **Bulk Upload** (on desktop and tablet).

<Steps>

1. Click **Bulk Upload**, then **Download Template**.

2. Fill in the template. Its columns are `scCode`, `colour`, `price`,
   `stemsPerBunch`, `wholesalerCode` and `wholesalerName`. `scCode`
   (the StemCounts flower code) and `price` (dollars per bunch) are
   required. `colour` can list several colour names separated by
   commas to identify one colour variant. A blank optional cell leaves
   the existing value unchanged.

3. Drop your CSV into the window, or click **Browse files**.

   <HelpMedia id="inventory-desktop-07-bulk-upload-dialog" caption="Bulk Upload takes a CSV to update prices and stock in one go. Download Template, fill it in, Browse files to choose it, then Validate before it is applied." />

4. Click **Validate**. Each row is checked and any invalid rows are
   listed. A missing colour can be added on the spot with **Add
   colour**.

5. When every row is valid, click **Upload**. You will see **Upload
   complete**.

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution">
The upload is all or nothing. If any row is invalid, nothing is saved.
Fix the rows it names and validate again. New flowers in the file are
added to your inventory as selected.
</Aside>

## How your prices reach florists

Your price per bunch is your wholesale price. Florists see it with a
StemCounts platform fee added on top; the fee is not charged to you.
Your own order totals and invoices show your price without the fee.
When a florist chooses you as the wholesaler for an order, every line
is recounted to your bunch sizes.

If you supply a regular price list file, the StemCounts team can load it
for you. Email **support@stemcounts.com** to arrange it.
