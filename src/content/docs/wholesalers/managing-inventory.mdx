Your **inventory** is the list of flowers you make available to
florists ordering on StemCounts. Florists only see flowers you've
**selected** in your library — everything else stays hidden.

<HelpMedia id="inventory-management-page" caption="Inventory Management with selected flowers + price overrides" />

## Selecting flowers

By default no flowers are selected. To make a flower visible to
florists:

<Steps>

1. Go to **Settings → Wholesaler Inventory** (or the **Inventory
   Management** link from your landing page).

2. Filter or search to find the flower.

3. Tick the checkbox or **Selected** indicator.

4. The flower is now visible to florists in their Flower Library and
   Flower Picker.

</Steps>

## Setting a custom price

Each flower has a default wholesale price (set by StemCounts). You can
override it per flower:

<Steps>

1. In the inventory row, click the **price field**.

2. Enter your custom price (numeric, no `$` sign).

3. Click out of the field — the value saves automatically.

</Steps>

If a save fails (e.g., session expired), you'll see **"Please log in to
save prices"**. Log out and back in to refresh.

## Variants (colours)

For flowers with multiple colour variants, tick the colour variants
you carry. Florists won't see colours you haven't selected — even if
the flower itself is selected.

## Bulk upload

For wholesalers with hundreds of stems, the **Bulk Upload** button
opens a CSV upload modal. Format:

| Column | Required? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Flower name | Yes | Must match a known catalog entry |
| Colour variant | Optional | Multiple rows per flower for multiple colours |
| Price | Optional | Numeric; leave blank to use the default |
| Selected | Optional | TRUE/FALSE; defaults TRUE on upload |

<Aside type="caution">
Bulk upload **adds or updates** rows; it doesn't deselect anything. To
remove flowers, deselect them manually in the UI.
</Aside>

## What you can't edit

A few things are read-only from the wholesaler view:

- **Photos** — the catalog photo is the photo, shared across all
  wholesalers
- **Seasonality** — the catalog defines which seasons a flower is
  available; you can carry it out of season but it'll be flagged
- **Stems per bunch** — fixed at the catalog level

If you spot a catalog error (wrong photo, missing variant, wrong pack
size), email **support@stemcounts.com**.

## Filters and search

The inventory page has filters by:

- **Category** — roses, dahlias, foliage, etc.
- **Colour** — multi-select
- **Season** — current season / out-of-season
- **Selected only** — quickly see your current list

The fuzzy search toggle lets you find flowers despite minor typos.

## Why selection matters

Florists place orders based on **what they can see**. A flower not in
your library = a missed sale. Conversely, leaving every flower
selected with no price overrides means you've effectively accepted
default platform pricing for everything.

<Aside type="tip">
Most wholesalers maintain a *core list* of staple stems they always
carry and override prices on, plus an opportunistic *seasonal list*
they enable for short windows.
</Aside>
