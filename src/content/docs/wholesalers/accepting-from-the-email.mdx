Every order a florist sends you also arrives by email. You can accept it
straight from that email, with no login.

## The new order email

The subject is **New order from [florist business]: [order
reference]**. The email says who sent it, how many items there are and
the delivery date, with a short facts table (Order, From, Delivery,
Items).

It goes to the **Contact email** set in **Settings > Your
Organisation**. If you have not set one, it goes to every active user in
your organisation. The florist who sent the order is copied in, and a
reply goes straight to them.

Attached is the order PDF, the picking document with every flower, its
stems and bunches. If the florist pinned Canva mood boards to the order,
those are attached too.

Some wholesalers also receive a text message with the same link to view
and accept the order.

## Accepting from the link

<Steps>

1. Click **Accept this order** in the email. The order opens in your
   browser. Nothing is accepted just by opening the link, so a mail
   scanner cannot accept an order by accident.

2. The page shows **New order from [florist].**, with the order's
   details and flowers, and two buttons: **Accept this order** and
   **Log in to manage**.

3. Click **Accept this order**.

4. The page confirms **Accepted. [Florist] has been told.** The florist
   gets an email and an in-app notification saying you accepted.

</Steps>

Accepting from the link only tells the florist you have taken the order
on. Quantities, substitutions and delivery are managed in the app after
you log in.

<Aside type="caution">
The link works once. After the order is accepted, the link in the email
and the link in the text both stop working. Opening one again shows
**This link has already been used**, or **Already accepted**, or **This
order has moved on** with the order's current status. The usual cause is
a colleague getting there first. Log in to see where the order is up to.
</Aside>

## Viewing an order without an account

The **View the order** button in the email opens a shared order page
that needs no login. It shows the order details, with **Download PDF**
and **Download the authorisation letter**, and offers **Create account**
if you do not yet have one. The shared page is read only and keeps
working for as long as the order exists, so you can forward it to
whoever packs the order.

## The authorisation letter

Each order carries a platform authorisation letter from the florist to
you. It is linked at the bottom of the order email, and in the app it
is the **Authorisation letter** button in the order window.

The letter states that orders you receive through StemCounts on that
florist's account are placed with the florist's authority, and that
order documents issued through the platform are covered by it. It does
not transfer liability for goods, and it does not let StemCounts vary
pricing, credit or trading terms agreed between you and the florist. It
lasts until the florist withdraws it in writing.

<Aside type="note">
The letter link only appears in the email when the letter can be
produced. That needs the florist's business name and ABN, and your
business name and address. If the email has no letter link, the florist
has not completed their organisation details yet.
</Aside>
