StemCounts tracks two prices for every stem: the **wholesale cost** you
pay your wholesaler, and the **customer price** your client pays you.
A few rules connect them.

<HelpMedia id="pricing-overview" caption="The cost-vs-customer-price relationship" />

## The two prices

| Price | Who it's for | Where it's set |
|---|---|---|
| **Wholesale cost** | What you pay your wholesaler | Set by the wholesaler's price list — fixed |
| **Customer price** | What your client pays you | Calculated as wholesale × retail markup |

## Your retail markup

Your **retail markup** is set once on your **Organisation** settings
page. It's a percentage — e.g. 250% means a stem costing $2 wholesale
is quoted at $5 customer.

To change it: **Settings → Organization → Retail Markup (%)**. See
[Your organisation](/billing/your-organisation/).

<Aside type="caution">
Changing your retail markup affects every open quote and budget.
Existing sent orders aren't recalculated — they keep the markup that
was in force when they were sent.
</Aside>

## How arrangement budgets work

When you create an arrangement, you set a **budget in customer price,
excluding GST**.

- This is the figure you'd quote your client for that design
- StemCounts works backwards using your retail markup to determine
  how much wholesale cost the budget can support
- As you add stems, the **Spent** number ticks up — using customer-price
  math
- The meter goes red when *Spent > Budget*

## GST

Budgets are entered **excluding GST**. The app calculates GST when
generating invoices and reports. Quoting clients including GST? Multiply
the budget by 1.10 (Australia) or your local rate.

## What "Revenue This Week" means

The metric on your landing page sums **customer-price totals** for all
arrangements in events landing this week. It's the gross revenue you'd
invoice the client(s), excluding GST.

## Pricing visibility — what the wholesaler sees

Wholesalers see only:

- Your wholesale-cost-side flower list (qty, stem, colour)
- Delivery date and venue

They **don't see** your customer price, your retail markup, or your
budget meter. That stays on your side.

## Per-line price overrides

Inside the Flower Picker, you can override the customer price for a
specific stem in a specific arrangement. This only affects *your*
customer-side math — what you'd quote and what shows on **Revenue This
Week**. The wholesale cost is unchanged.

Useful for rare or premium stems where your usual markup doesn't fit.

## Bulk discounts and supplier pricing

Wholesale prices are set by your wholesaler. If you order in volume and
think a different price applies, contact your wholesaler directly —
the platform doesn't negotiate prices automatically.
