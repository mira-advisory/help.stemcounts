After accepting your order, a wholesaler can flag any flower they cannot
supply as ordered. They choose one of three things to offer, you answer
on the order page, and the bill follows what is actually supplied.

## The three things a wholesaler can offer

| The wholesaler chooses | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| **Offer Substitutes** | Between one and five alternative flowers for you to choose from. |
| **Partial Quantity** | Fewer stems of the same flower, in whole bunches. |
| **Unavailable** | The flower is removed from your order. |

They can add a note, for example "Out of season until June". A change
covers the whole line: every arrangement using that flower, colour and
length.

## How you are told

- In the app, a notification: **Substitute flower offered**, **Reduced
  quantity offered** or **Flower cancelled**.
- By email: **Choose a substitute for [flower] on [reference]**, **Accept
  or decline: fewer [flower] on [reference]**, or **[Wholesaler] cannot
  supply [flower]: [reference]**. Any note from the wholesaler is
  included.
- By text, when order text messages are switched on.

On the order page, lines that need your answer show **Action Required**.
An unavailable flower needs no answer: it is removed straight away.

<HelpMedia id="substitutes-desktop-02-order-action-required" caption="The order the notification opens: the header shows Actions Required with a count, and each affected flower reads Action Required in Stem Status." />

## Answering a substitute or fewer-stems offer

<Steps>

1. Open the order. Press **Review Substitutes** on the line marked
   **Action Required**.

2. **Review Substitute Options** shows the original flower beside each
   option and the price difference per stem.

3. Pick an option and press **Confirm Selection**, or press **Decline All
   Options**.

</Steps>

If you accept, the line becomes **Substitute Confirmed**. If you decline,
the line is cancelled and removed from the order. Either way the
wholesaler gets a notification (**Substitute selected** or **Substitute
options declined**) and the order can move on again. If a substitute is
already in that arrangement, you will be asked to choose a different
option.

<Aside type="caution">
Until you answer, the wholesaler cannot confirm, pack or deliver the
order. Their buttons read **Awaiting Florist**, and your order stays at
its current stage. Answer promptly so the rest of the order is not held
up.
</Aside>

## What you are billed

You are billed for what the wholesaler supplies:

- **Substitute accepted**: you pay the price shown for the substitute you
  chose, for the stems offered. The prices shown for substitutes include
  the platform fee, like every other price you see.
- **Fewer stems accepted**: you pay for the reduced number of stems. Your
  arrangement recipe stays as you designed it, but the bill reflects what
  is supplied.
- **Declined**: the line is removed and comes off your invoice.
- **Unavailable**: the line is removed and comes off your invoice. You do
  not need to do anything unless you want to source it elsewhere.
