StemCounts tells you about your order three ways: in the app through the
bell, by email, and by text message when order texts are switched on. You
can also message your wholesaler directly on an order.

## The notification bell

The bell at the top of the app shows a count of your unread notifications
(up to **99+**). Click it to open your recent notifications, newest first.
Unread ones are highlighted and each shows how long ago it arrived.

Click a notification to go straight to the order. Use **Mark all read** to
clear the count, the X to dismiss one, or refresh to check for new ones.
If there is nothing, you will see **No notifications**.

Everyone at your business is notified in the app when a wholesaler:

- accepts an order: **Order accepted**
- confirms supply: **Supply confirmed** or **Order confirmed**
- packs or fulfils it: **Order packed and ready** or **Order fulfilled**
- marks it delivered: **Order delivered**
- rejects it: **Order rejected**
- offers a change: **Substitute flower offered** or **Reduced quantity
  offered**
- removes an unavailable flower: **Flower cancelled**
- messages you on an order: **New message**

## Emails

You receive these order emails:

1. **A copy of the new order email** sent to the wholesaler, when you send
   an order. This is your confirmation that it went out; there is no
   separate "order sent" email.
2. **[Wholesaler] accepted your order: [reference]**, when the wholesaler
   accepts using the **Accept this order** link in their email or text. It
   has a **View the order** button. When the wholesaler accepts inside
   the app instead, you get the in-app notification only.
3. **A supply change email** when a wholesaler offers a substitute, offers
   fewer stems, or marks a flower unavailable. See
   [Substitutes and supply changes](/florists/substitutes-and-supply-changes/).

There is currently no email when an order becomes Supply Confirmed, Ready
or Complete, or when it is rejected. Those changes show in the app and in
your bell.

Order emails go to the person who sent the order, or, failing that, to
your business contact email.

## Text messages

Order text messages are switched on for the whole platform by the
StemCounts team. When they are on, you get:

- **Order sent**: "StemCounts: order [ref] sent to [wholesaler] for
  [date]. We will text you when they accept it."
- **Accepted**: "StemCounts: [wholesaler] accepted order [ref]. [link]",
  when the wholesaler accepts from the link in their email or text.
- **Supply change**: a text saying the wholesaler cannot supply a flower,
  or can supply fewer, with a link to choose or accept.

Texts are one-way. Replies are not received, so use the link in the text
to act on the order. They go to the mobile of the person who sent the
order, and the accepted and supply change texts also go to your business
phone if it is a mobile. Keep your **Mobile** up to date in **Settings >
Your Profile**. Landlines and incomplete numbers are skipped, and the
email and in-app notification still go.

There is no setting in the app to turn order texts off for yourself. If
you do not want them, contact support@stemcounts.com.

## Chat with your wholesaler on an order

On a sent order, the **Weekly Order Summary** groups flowers by
wholesaler, and each wholesaler's section has a **Chat** button. It opens
a **Messages** panel for that wholesaler's part of the order. Start a
conversation with a short topic name and your message, then press the
send button. Replies arrive as **New message** notifications in your
bell.

If the wholesaler is not on StemCounts chat yet, the panel says so and
suggests you give them a nudge to join.

## The Order emails switch

Under **Notifications** in **Settings > Your Profile** is a switch called
**Order emails**: "New orders, changes and updates from your
wholesalers." Your choice is saved to your profile.

<Aside type="caution">
The current version picks order email recipients from the order and your
business contact details and does not check this switch, so turning it
off may not stop order emails yet. It has no effect on in-app
notifications or texts. If you need to stop emails, contact
support@stemcounts.com.
</Aside>

## Other emails you may get

- **Reset your StemCounts password**: a **Choose a new password** link
  that works once and expires after one hour.
- **Subscription invoices and receipts**, with the StemCounts-branded
  document attached.
- A copy of any **inventory request** you send a wholesaler asking them
  to stock a flower. Their reply comes to you.
