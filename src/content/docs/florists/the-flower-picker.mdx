The **Flower Picker** is where you actually order stems. It opens when
you click **Pick Flowers** inside an arrangement.

<HelpMedia id="flower-picker-overview" kind="video" caption="Walking through the Flower Picker" />

## The layout

- **Filter bar** at the top — Category, Colour, Season, fuzzy search
  toggle
- **Flower grid** — one card per flower
- **Cart panel** (right or bottom) — running list of what you've added

## Filters

| Filter | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Category** | Roses, dahlias, foliage, etc. — top-level botanical groups |
| **Colour** | One or more colours. Combine with Category to find e.g. "white roses" |
| **Season** | Hides out-of-season stems |
| **Fuzzy search** toggle | Loose matching on typos — finds "rununculus" → ranunculus |

## Per-flower controls

When you click a flower card, you see:

- **Colour variants** — each colour has its own swatch and list price
- **Quantity input** — defaults to stems
- **Stem/Bunch toggle** — switch between counting individual stems and
  whole bunches
- **Notes** — optional free text the wholesaler sees

## Stems vs bunches

Florists buy in different units depending on the flower:

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Stems">
    Default mode. Enter the exact number of individual stems you need.
    StemCounts rounds up to whole bunches at the wholesaler end
    (`bunches = ceil(stems / stems_per_bunch)`).
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Bunches">
    Switch to bunch mode if you'd rather count whole bunches. Useful
    for flowers always sold in bunches of 10, 20, etc. The actual stem
    count is calculated automatically.
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

The **stems-per-bunch** varies by flower and is shown on the card.

## Pinning favourites

Click the **pin icon** on a flower card to keep it at the top of the
grid for future sessions. Useful for staple stems you order every week
(eucalyptus, baby's breath, the rose variety you always carry).

## Price overrides

Hovering over a price shows a **pencil icon**. Click it to enter a
different price for this single arrangement. The system remembers it
for the rest of the order session.

<Aside type="caution">
Price overrides only affect *your* customer-side math (revenue,
budget). They don't change what the wholesaler charges you — the
wholesale price is fixed by the wholesaler's price list.
</Aside>

## Adding to all arrangements (bulk mode)

If you opened the Picker from the **Pick Flowers** button next to *Add
Arrangement* (not inside a specific card), you're in **bulk mode**.

Quantities you set are distributed across **all arrangements in this
event**, proportional to each arrangement's quantity.

Example: 60 roses, distributed across 4 arrangements of quantity 3, 3,
3, 3 → each gets 15 roses.

## Confirming your choices

When you're done, click **Done** to return to the event details page.
Your stem list is saved.

You can re-open the Picker any time to adjust. See the FAQ entry [How
do I add more stems to an arrangement?](/faqs/florists/) for in-detail
tweaks.
