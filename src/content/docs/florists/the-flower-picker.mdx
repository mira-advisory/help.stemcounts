The **Flower Picker** is where you choose the flowers for an
arrangement. It opens from **Pick Flowers** on an empty arrangement card
(that arrangement only), from **Add Flowers** in the Arrangements header
(every arrangement in the event), or from **Add Flowers to All** in the
**Add Arrangements** form.

Each colour you pick goes in as 1 stem (or 1 bunch in a bunches
arrangement). Set the real quantities on the arrangement card afterwards:
see [Building an arrangement](/florists/building-an-arrangement/). If a
flower is already in the arrangement it is topped up rather than
duplicated. **Back to Order Part** leaves without adding.

## Search and filters

- **Search**: "Search flowers by name, common name, or code...". It also
  matches colour names.
- **Categories**: narrow to one category, or **All Categories**.
- **Colours**: under **Pick colours**, choose one or more. The **Similar
  shades** switch (on by default) also includes colours close to the ones
  you pick. Turn it off for exact matches only.
- **Season**: show flowers in season in the months you choose.

Filters combine, and **Clear** resets colours or seasons. Pinned flowers
always sort to the top.

## What each card shows

Each card shows **Price:** and **Stems per bunch:**. When the event has a
preferred wholesaler, it is that wholesaler's price and bunch size for the
flower, and that is the price the flower is costed at on the event. If
that wholesaler does not list the flower, or the event has no preferred
wholesaler, it is the catalogue price and bunch size. It is a wholesale
price, not your customer price.

Hover over the name at the bottom of the photo for more: the other names,
**Category**, **Price**, **Stems per bunch**, **Length** (if chosen) and
**Season**.

## Stem lengths

When a wholesaler lists a flower in more than one length, the card shows
length pills under the colours. Hover over a pill to see who lists that
length and at what price. If your event's preferred wholesaler does not
list it you can still pick it: the hover says "Not in [wholesaler]'s
list" and names who does, and the line carries a note saying so.

A line at a length is kept separate from the same flower at another
length. It shows the length next to its name and is rounded to whole
bunches on its own.

## Pinning the flowers you always order

Click the heart in the top-left corner of a flower's photo (**Pin
flower**). Click it again to **Unpin flower**. Pinned flowers show before
everything else, most recently pinned first, and stay pinned next time you
open the picker. You can also pin from [Explore Flowers](/florists/browsing-the-catalog/)
with the **Pin to the top** switch.

## Adding to every arrangement at once

When you open the picker from **Add Flowers** (or **Add Flowers to All**),
every flower you pick is added to each arrangement in the event, except
Spares: 1 stem each, or 1 bunch in a bunches arrangement. It is not
divided between them. An arrangement that already holds that flower is
topped up. If some arrangements already have flowers, StemCounts tells you
before adding on top.

## Why can't I find a flower?

A few things hide flowers:

- The picker only shows flowers, and colours, that at least one
  wholesaler currently offers.
- Colours already in the arrangement are taken off the card, and a flower
  disappears once all its colours are in. When adding to all
  arrangements, a colour is hidden only if every arrangement already has
  it.
- A search, category, colour or season filter is still on. Check
  **Similar shades** too.

If the page says **No flowers available**, try **Refresh Flowers**. If a
wholesaler does not stock a flower you need, you can ask them to add it
from the weekly order summary: see [Sending your order](/florists/sending-your-order/).

<Aside type="note">
The picker has no quantity boxes, no notes and no stem or bunch switch.
Quantities are set on the arrangement card, comments for the wholesaler
are added on the weekly order, and counting in bunches is a property of
the arrangement or the order, not of the pick.
</Aside>
