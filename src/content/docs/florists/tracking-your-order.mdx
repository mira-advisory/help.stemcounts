Once your order is sent, you can watch it move through a handful of
clear stages. Your wholesaler sees the same stages with a little more
detail on their side.

<HelpMedia id="order-status-flow" caption="The five stages florists see" />

## The stages

| Status | What it means | Who moves it forward |
|---|---|---|
| **Draft** | You're still building it | You |
| **Sent** | Your wholesaler has the order; not yet accepted | — |
| **Accepted** | Wholesaler has accepted; they're now sourcing | Wholesaler |
| **Supply Confirmed** | All flowers confirmed by suppliers | Wholesaler |
| **Ready** | Flowers ready for pickup or delivery | Wholesaler |
| **Complete** | With you, all done | Wholesaler |

## Where you'll see the status

- **Orders list** — each row shows a status badge
- **Order detail page** — banner up top with the overall status
- **Inside an order** — each individual flower shows its own status; when most are *Ready*, the order is *Ready*

## Status by flower

Your wholesaler doesn't always source every flower from the same
supplier — so on a single order, some flowers might be confirmed
before others. Open the Order Detail page to see exactly where each
flower is.

For example, your order might show **Supply Confirmed** overall but
one specific stem still shows **Sent** because it's coming from a
slower supplier.

<Aside type="note">
The overall order status reflects the slowest flower. If nine flowers
are confirmed and one is still on the way, the order will still read
**Sent** until the last one catches up.
</Aside>

## What we'll email you about

You'll get an email when:

- Your order is **Accepted** by the wholesaler
- Your order is **Ready** for pickup or delivery
- A flower is **rejected** (rare — usually means a supplier couldn't get it)

You can change your notification preferences in **Settings → Personal**
with the "Email notifications enabled" toggle.

## What to do at each stage

| Status | Your move |
|---|---|
| **Sent** | Wait for accept. If it's still sitting at Sent after 24 hours, give your wholesaler a nudge. |
| **Accepted** | Nothing to do. If you want a closer look, the Order Detail page shows each flower's progress. |
| **Supply Confirmed** | You know exactly what's arriving. Plan your studio time. |
| **Ready** | Pick up, or expect delivery, per your wholesaler's schedule. |
| **Complete** | All done. Invoice / payment captured per your normal billing arrangement. |

## If your order is rejected

If your wholesaler rejects an order (capacity, sourcing, or a question
they want to talk through), it goes back to **Draft** in your Orders
list with a short note explaining why. Edit and re-send, or reach out
to the wholesaler directly to chat it through.
