Once your order is sent, you can watch it move through a handful of
stages. Each flower on the order has a status of its own as well, and the
order's status is worked out from them.

<HelpMedia id="order-status-sent" caption="An order page heading: Order Details - Sent" />

## The order stages

1. **Draft**: you are still building it and it has not been sent.
2. **Sent**: the order has gone to your wholesaler.
3. **Accepted**: your wholesaler has accepted responsibility for the
   order.
4. **Supply Confirmed**: your wholesaler has confirmed they can supply
   it.
5. **Ready**: your wholesaler has packed the order and it is ready for
   delivery.
6. **Complete**: the order has been delivered and completed.

Accepted does not mean every flower is confirmed. Supply is confirmed
flower by flower after the wholesaler accepts, and the order only
reaches **Supply Confirmed** once every flower still on it is confirmed.

## Where you see the status

- **The order page**: the heading reads **Order Details** followed by the
  status, for example "Order Details - Sent".
- **The Orders page**: switch between **Calendar** ("View orders in the
  weekly calendar planner.") and **Order Board** ("View orders grouped by
  status."). The board has four columns: **Draft**, **Sent**, **In
  Progress** (accepted and being fulfilled) and **Done** (delivered or
  completed). The **Orders** or **Events** switch shows whole weekly
  orders or individual events.
- **Each flower line** in the **Weekly Order Summary** on a sent order.

## The status on each flower

| Line status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Supply OK** | Your wholesaler has confirmed they can supply this flower. |
| **Packed & Ready** | Packed and ready for delivery. |
| **Action Required** | The wholesaler cannot supply it as ordered and needs you to choose a substitute, or accept or decline fewer stems. |
| **Substitute Confirmed** | You accepted a substitute. |
| **Cancelled** | The flower was unavailable and has been removed from your order. |
| **Complete** | Delivered. |

When a line shows **Action Required**, see
[Substitutes and supply changes](/florists/substitutes-and-supply-changes/).

## How the order status is worked out

StemCounts works the order status out from its flower lines. Cancelled
flowers are ignored. The order moves forward only when every remaining
flower has reached the next stage:

- every flower confirmed (or its substitute confirmed): **Supply
  Confirmed**
- every flower packed: **Ready**
- every flower delivered: **Complete**

While any flower is waiting on you to answer a substitute or fewer-stems
offer, the order cannot move forward at all. One slow line holds the
whole order at its current stage.

<Aside type="note">
If your order went to more than one wholesaler, the overall status
follows the wholesaler who is furthest behind. If one has marked their
part **Ready** and another is still at **Accepted**, the order shows
**Accepted** until the second catches up. The overall status never goes
backwards. Open the order to see where each wholesaler's part and each
flower is up to.
</Aside>

## Confirming what arrived

Once a wholesaler's part of your order has reached **Supply Confirmed**
or **Ready**, you can confirm what arrived, on the order page:

- **Confirm Received** on a single flower line
- **Confirm Delivery of All Flowers** for everything from that wholesaler
- **Mark as Delivered**, shown when the wholesaler's part is **Ready**

Received lines show **Received**. When every line from that wholesaler is
received, their part becomes **Complete**, and the wholesaler gets a
**Delivery confirmed** notification.

## What to do at each stage

| Status | Your move |
|---|---|
| **Sent** | Wait for the accept. If it is still at Sent after a day, give your wholesaler a nudge, or message them with **Chat** on the order. |
| **Accepted** | Nothing yet. Watch for **Action Required** on any line. |
| **Supply Confirmed** | You know what is arriving. Plan your studio time. |
| **Ready** | Pick up, or expect delivery, on the date you set when sending. |
| **Complete** | All done. Confirm what arrived if you have not already. |

## If your order is rejected

While an order is new, the wholesaler can reject it. The order and its
flowers are marked **Rejected** and you get an in-app notification,
**Order rejected**. It does not go back to draft. Talk to the wholesaler,
or send the flowers again as a new order.
