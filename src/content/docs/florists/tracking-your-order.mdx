Once sent, your order moves through a small number of clearly named
status stages. Florists see a simplified view — wholesalers see more
detail behind the scenes.

<HelpMedia id="order-status-flow" caption="The five statuses florists see" />

## The status sequence

| Status | What it means | Who advances it |
|---|---|---|
| **Draft** | You're still editing | You |
| **Sent** | Order with wholesaler, not yet accepted | — |
| **Accepted** | Wholesaler has accepted; they're sourcing | Wholesaler |
| **Supply Confirmed** | All flowers confirmed by suppliers | Wholesaler |
| **Ready** | Flowers ready for pickup or delivery | Wholesaler |
| **Complete** | Delivered / picked up | Wholesaler |

## Where you see status

- **Orders list** — each row shows a status pill
- **Order detail page** — overview banner shows the order's overall
  status
- **Per-arrangement** — each individual flower line tracks its own
  status; when most are *Ready*, the order is *Ready*

## Per-flower-line status

A wholesaler doesn't always source every flower from the same supplier.
This means individual flower lines can be in **different states** within
the same order. The Order Detail page surfaces this per-line.

Example: an order might have **Supply Confirmed** overall, but one
specific stem is still **Sent** because it's coming from a slow supplier.

<Aside type="note">
The overall order status is the *least advanced* status across its
lines. So an order with 9 confirmed lines and 1 sent line shows as
**Sent** until the last line catches up.
</Aside>

## Notifications

StemCounts sends an email when:

- Your order is **Accepted** by the wholesaler
- Your order is **Ready** for pickup/delivery
- A line is **rejected** (rare; usually means a supplier can't source
  something)

You can adjust notification preferences in **Settings → Personal** (the
"Email notifications enabled" toggle).

## What to do at each stage

| Status | Your action |
|---|---|
| **Sent** | Wait for accept. If still sent after 24h, ping the wholesaler. |
| **Accepted** | No action. Track the per-line statuses on the Order Detail page if you want detail. |
| **Supply Confirmed** | Plan your studio time — you know what's actually arriving. |
| **Ready** | Pick up (or expect delivery) according to your wholesaler's schedule. |
| **Complete** | Done. Invoice / payment captured per your billing arrangement. |

## Rejected orders

If a wholesaler rejects your order (capacity, sourcing, dispute), it
goes back to **Draft** in your Orders list with a rejection note.
Edit and re-send, or contact the wholesaler to discuss.
