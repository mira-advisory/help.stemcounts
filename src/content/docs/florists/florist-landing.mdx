When you log in, you land on the **florist dashboard**. It's designed
to answer three questions in a single glance: *what's in flight,
what's coming up this week, and how am I tracking financially?*

<HelpMedia id="florist-landing-overview" caption="The florist dashboard on first load" />

## The three metrics

Three big tiles sit at the top of the page:

| Tile | What it counts | Click to |
|---|---|---|
| **Open Drafts** | Draft orders not yet sent | Filtered Orders list — draft only |
| **Events This Week** | Events landing in the current week | This week's Orders view |
| **Revenue This Week** | Customer-side total for the week, with your retail markup applied | Same as Events This Week |

<Aside type="note">
Revenue is calculated from arrangement budgets × your organisation's
retail markup. See [Pricing and budgets](/florists/pricing-and-budgets/)
for how the math works.
</Aside>

## The primary CTAs

Two large cards dominate the middle of the page:

- **Orders** — your full order list, all weeks
- **New Order** — opens the *Add Event* modal, the start of every new
  piece of work

<HelpMedia id="florist-landing-primary-ctas" />

## The support shortcuts

Smaller cards underneath:

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Explore Flowers">
    Jumps to the [Flower Library](/florists/browsing-the-catalog/) — a
    read-only browse of the full catalog. Useful for inspiration before
    you build an arrangement.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Settings">
    Profile, organisation, notifications. See [Profile and
    notifications](/billing/profile-and-notifications/).
  </Card>
  <Card title="FAQs">
    The same FAQ entries as [/faqs/](/faqs/), inside the app.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Mobile

On phones, the layout collapses to a single column but the same tiles
and cards appear in the same order. The user menu moves into a hamburger
icon in the top-right.
