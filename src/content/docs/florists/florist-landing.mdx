When you sign in, you land on the **florist home page**. It answers
three things at a glance: what is in flight, what is coming up this
week, and how you are tracking financially.

## This week's three figures

| Figure | What it counts | Click to |
|---|---|---|
| **Open Drafts** | Draft orders you have not sent yet | The **Orders** page, drafts only |
| **Events This Week** | Events landing in the current week | This week on the **Orders** page |
| **Revenue This Week** | The customer price of this week's orders | This week on the **Orders** page |

<Aside type="note">
**Revenue This Week** is the customer-side value of this week's orders:
each event at its own markup, or your organisation's **Retail markup**
where an event has none. Weeks run Monday to Sunday. There is more in
[Pricing and budgets](/florists/pricing-and-budgets/).
</Aside>

## The main actions

Three large cards sit under the figures:

- **Orders**: "View and manage all your flower orders." Your full order
  list across every week, as a **Calendar** or an **Order Board**.
- **New Order**: "Create a new order and add event details." Opens the
  **Add Event to Order** window, which is where every new piece of work
  starts.
- **Invoices**: "View invoices and order amounts from your
  wholesalers." Your sent orders with their wholesaler, dates, amount
  and status, and a **View Invoice** button for each.

## Smaller cards

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Explore Flowers">
    Browse the full flower catalogue, with **Category** and **Season**
    filters and a pin for the flowers you use most. Handy for ideas
    before you build an arrangement. It also lives in **Settings** under
    **Ordering**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Settings">
    Your profile, your organisation and your subscription. See
    [Profile and notifications](/billing/profile-and-notifications/).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Help guides">
    Opens this help site in a new tab. For a quick answer, the round
    chat button at the bottom right answers from the same guides.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## On a phone

The phone home page is built for one hand. You see **Welcome** with your
name and business, the three figures as tiles, a large **New order**
button, then a list of rows: **Orders**, **Invoices**, **Explore
Flowers**, **Settings** and **Help guides**. There is no menu button. A
bar along the bottom of the screen has **Home**, **Orders**, **New
order**, **Search** and **Chat**, and your initials at the top right open
**Settings**, **What's new** and **Sign out**. See [StemCounts on your
phone](/getting-started/the-phone-app/).
