When you log in, you land on the **florist home screen**. It's built
to answer three things at a glance: what's in flight, what's coming
up this week, and how you're tracking financially.

<HelpMedia id="florist-landing-overview" kind="video" caption="A quick look at the florist home screen" />

## The three tiles up top

| Tile | What it counts | Click to |
|---|---|---|
| **Open Drafts** | Draft orders you haven't sent yet | Filtered Orders list — drafts only |
| **Events This Week** | Events landing in the current week | This week's Orders view |
| **Revenue This Week** | Customer-side total for the week, with your retail markup applied | Same as Events This Week |

<Aside type="note">
Revenue is calculated from your arrangement budgets multiplied by your
organisation's retail markup. There's more detail in [Pricing and
budgets](/florists/pricing-and-budgets/).
</Aside>

## The main actions

Two large cards sit in the middle of the page:

- **Orders** — your full order list across every week
- **New Order** — opens the *Add Event* modal, which is where every new piece of work starts

## Quick shortcuts

Smaller cards underneath:

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Explore Flowers">
    Opens the [Flower Library](/florists/browsing-the-catalog/) — a
    read-only browse of the full catalog. Handy for inspiration before
    you build an arrangement.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Settings">
    Your profile, organisation, and notifications. See
    [Profile and notifications](/billing/profile-and-notifications/).
  </Card>
  <Card title="FAQs">
    The same FAQ entries as the [FAQs section](/faqs/) here, also available
    inside the app.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## On mobile

The layout collapses to a single column on phones, with the same tiles
and cards in the same order. The user menu tucks behind a hamburger
icon in the top-right.
