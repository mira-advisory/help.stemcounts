An **arrangement** is one design within an event. A wedding might have
four: ceremony arch, bridal bouquet, reception centrepieces, cake flowers.
Each arrangement has its own name, quantity, budget and flower list, and
shows as a card on the event page.

## Add an arrangement

<Steps>

1. Open the event and click **Add Arrangement**.

   <HelpMedia id="add-arrangement-button" caption="Add Arrangement in the Arrangements header of an event" />

2. In **Add Arrangements**, fill in a row for each design:

   - **Name**: short and specific, for example "Centrepiece".
   - **Qty**: how many of this design you are making.
   - **Budget**: your customer price for one of them, ex GST. **Total
     Budget** shows Qty times Budget.

   Use **Add another arrangement** for more rows. If you have saved
   favourites, they show as pills above the rows: click one to add it with
   its budget and flowers. See [Favourite arrangements](/florists/favourites/).

   On a phone it is a step by step wizard instead: start **From scratch**
   or **From a favourite**, give it a name, then set how many and the
   **Budget each**.

If you leave a budget blank, StemCounts asks for it before adding, in a
window that says "All budget values are Ex GST". Adding the first
arrangement to an event also creates the event's **Spares** bucket. See
[The Spares bucket](/florists/the-spares-bucket/).

## Two ways to add flowers

| Button | Where | Adds flowers to |
|---|---|---|
| **Pick Flowers** | On an empty arrangement card | That arrangement only |
| **Add Flowers** | In the Arrangements header, once the event has at least one arrangement | Every arrangement in the event, except Spares |

Both open [the Flower Picker](/florists/the-flower-picker/). With **Add
Flowers**, every flower you pick is added to each arrangement at the same
quantity (1 stem each). It is not divided between them. If some
arrangements already have flowers, StemCounts tells you before adding on
top.

## Change the name, quantity or budget

Edit them in place on the card while the order is a draft:

- Click the arrangement name to rename it.
- Click **Qty:** to change how many of this design you are making.
- Click the **$spent / $budget** figure to change the budget.

Press Enter to save or Escape to cancel.

## Change flower quantities

Each flower row on a card has:

- small up and down arrows (**Increase quantity**, **Decrease quantity**),
- the number itself. Click it to type a new amount, then press Enter,
- an **X** (**Remove from this list**) to take the flower out. An **Undo**
  button shows for five seconds before the removal is saved.

Hover a flower's number to see its total stems for the event.

## Quantity and stems

Two different numbers:

- **Qty** on the card is how many of this design you are making.
- The number on each flower row is stems **per arrangement**.

The total stems for the event are one multiplied by the other: 5 roses in
an arrangement with Qty 12 is 60 roses.

## The 3-dot menu

Each arrangement card has a three-dot button (**More actions**) with:

- a star, **Save as favourite (with its flowers)**. See
  [Favourite arrangements](/florists/favourites/).
- a copy icon, **Clone**. See [Cloning arrangements](/florists/cloning-arrangements/).
- a bin, **Delete**.

## Change the price of one flower

Click a flower's name on the card to open its markup card. It shows
**Wholesale / bunch** and **Customer / bunch**. Enter a multiplier in
**Markup override** and click **Save**, or **Clear override** to go back
to the event or default markup. A flower with its own markup shows its
name in coral. See [Pricing and budgets](/florists/pricing-and-budgets/).

## Move or copy a flower between arrangements

Drag a flower row from one card and drop it on another arrangement, or on
Spares. **Transfer Flower** opens with two choices: **Copy stems** (enter
how many to copy and keep the source as it is) or **Move all**. If the
target already has that flower, the stems are added to its existing line.
You can also drag whole cards to reorder them; Spares always stays last.

## Counting in bunches inside an event

Arrangements normally count stems. To count in bunches, click the bouquet
button beside **Add Flowers** (**Add a Bunches arrangement**). **Add
Bunches Arrangement** asks for a **Name** and **Budget**. In a bunches
arrangement each picked flower goes in as one bunch and the arrows move a
bunch at a time. There is no switch to turn a stems arrangement into
bunches, so add a Bunches arrangement instead.

## Once the order is sent

Everything on the event becomes read-only: no adding, cloning or deleting
arrangements, and no changing flowers, quantities, budgets or markups.
See [Sending your order](/florists/sending-your-order/).
