An **arrangement** is one design within an event. A wedding might
have four: ceremony, bridal bouquet, reception centerpieces, cake
florals. Each arrangement is built separately with its own name,
budget, and flower list.

<HelpMedia id="event-details-with-arrangements" kind="video" caption="An event with three arrangements" />

## Add an arrangement

<Steps>

1. Open the event (from the landing page or the Orders list).

2. Click **Add Arrangement** on the event details page.

   <HelpMedia id="add-arrangement-button" />

3. Fill in the arrangement form:

   - **Name** — short and specific, e.g. "Ceremony arch", "Bridal
     bouquet", "Centerpiece x 12"
   - **Quantity** — how many of this design you need (e.g. 12
     centerpieces). Defaults to 1.
   - **Budget** — your **customer price** for this arrangement,
     excluding GST. StemCounts calculates the cost-side flower budget
     from your retail markup.

4. Click **Save**. The arrangement appears as a card on the event
   details page.

5. Click **Pick Flowers** inside the new arrangement card to start
   adding stems. See [The Flower Picker](/florists/the-flower-picker/).

</Steps>

## Two ways to add flowers

There are **two Pick Flowers buttons** on the event page. They do
different things:

| Button location | Adds flowers to |
|---|---|
| Inside a specific arrangement card | Just that arrangement |
| Next to **Add Arrangement** at the top of the event | **All arrangements in the event** (bulk mode) |

<Aside type="tip">
Bulk mode is useful when many arrangements share a flower — e.g. all
your centerpieces use the same roses. Add them once, distributed
proportionally across arrangements.
</Aside>

## Budget tracking

The arrangement card shows a live budget meter:

- **Budget** — what you set when you created the arrangement
- **Spent** — running total based on stem prices × quantities
- **Remaining** — budget minus spent

The colour of the meter shifts as you approach the limit. You can
exceed your budget — StemCounts won't stop you — but you'll see it
called out in red.

## Editing or removing an arrangement

Each arrangement card has a **3-dot menu** in the top-right:

- **Clone** — duplicate this arrangement. See [Cloning
  arrangements](/florists/cloning-arrangements/).
- **Delete** — remove it. Confirmation required.
- **Rename** — edit name and budget.

## Quantity vs stems

Two different "quantities" in StemCounts:

- **Arrangement quantity** — how many of *this design* you need. 12
  centerpieces = quantity 12.
- **Stem quantity** — how many stems of *each flower* you need *per
  arrangement*. 5 roses per centerpiece = 5 stems.

The total stem count = arrangement quantity × stem quantity. For 12
centerpieces with 5 roses each, you need 60 stems of roses.
