The **Spares Bucket** is an extra slot inside every event that holds
stems not allocated to a specific arrangement. It's where you put cover
for breakages, last-minute changes, and improvised additions on the day.

<HelpMedia id="spares-bucket-on-event" caption="The Spares Bucket sits alongside arrangements on the event page" />

## Why it exists

A wedding's bridal bouquet might call for 18 roses. Two will get
crushed in transit, one will look wrong on the day. If you ordered
exactly 18, you're short. If you build "spares" into each arrangement,
your budgets get distorted and you lose visibility on what's actually
contingency.

The Spares Bucket separates **planned stems** from **just-in-case
stems** without messing up your arrangement budgets.

## How to use it

Treat the Spares Bucket as its own pseudo-arrangement. You add flowers
to it the same way you'd add to a normal arrangement:

1. Open the event
2. Click **Pick Flowers** on the Spares Bucket card
3. Add the stems you want as cover
4. Save

The Spares Bucket has no budget — anything you add to it is over-and-above
your arrangement budgets.

## What gets reported where

| | Arrangements | Spares Bucket |
|---|---|---|
| Counts toward arrangement budget | Yes | No |
| Counts toward weekly order total | Yes | Yes |
| Appears on the printable event sheet | Yes | Yes (separate section) |
| Sent to the wholesaler | Yes | Yes |

The wholesaler doesn't see the conceptual split — they receive the
total stem count for the event. The split is purely for your
budgeting and on-day fulfilment.

<Aside type="tip">
Build a habit: 5–10% spares on every event. Cheaper than driving back
to the wholesaler at 7am on a Saturday.
</Aside>

## Adjusting spares after sending

Spares can be edited up to the cut-off (same as the rest of the order).
After the cut-off, contact your wholesaler directly to amend.
