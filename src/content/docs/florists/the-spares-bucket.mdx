Wholesalers sell in bunches. Your arrangements count stems. When the
stems of a flower across an event do not come to whole bunches, the extra
stems needed to complete the bunch go into **Spares**. Every event with
arrangements has a Spares card, shown after your arrangements.

<HelpMedia id="spares-bucket-on-event" caption="The Spares card on an event page, after the arrangements" />

## The Summary panel

The **Summary** panel on the event page adds up each flower across every
arrangement and Spares, including arrangement quantities, and compares
the total with the bunch size.

- **Unrounded Bunches**: flowers that do not come to a whole number of
  bunches. Each row has a red **Remove** button showing the extra stems,
  and a teal **Add** button showing the stems needed to complete the
  bunch.
- **Rounded Bunches**: flowers that are whole bunches, with a tick.

**Add** puts the missing stems into Spares. **Remove** takes the extra
stems back out of your arrangements. If a flower has no whole bunch at
all, you are asked to confirm removing it from the event.

## How stems get into Spares

The Spares card has no **Pick Flowers** button. Stems get in three ways:

- The **Add** button in the Summary's **Unrounded Bunches** table.
- Dragging a flower from an arrangement onto Spares.
- Automatically: when you change a flower's quantity in an arrangement
  and that flower already has a spares line, the line is reset to exactly
  what completes the bunch. If nothing is needed, it is removed.

## Removing or moving spare stems

Quantities in Spares cannot be typed in. Each spare row has two small
buttons instead:

- **X** (**Remove these stems from Spares**) removes that row outright.
  The same **Undo** as other rows appears for five seconds before the
  removal is saved.
- The two-way arrows (**Reallocate these stems**) move the stems into your
  arrangements. **Move spare stems into your arrangements** lists every
  arrangement in the event. Use each card's stepper to set how many stems
  go in, then click **Move N stems**. An arrangement that does not have
  the flower yet can still receive it. For an arrangement made several
  times (Qty above 1), stems move that many at a time, one into each.
  Leftovers that cannot be placed stay in Spares.

After removing spares, that flower may no longer be whole bunches. Check
the **Summary** panel before adding the event to the weekly order.

## Spares and your budget

Spares are ordered from the wholesaler with everything else and count
towards the event's total spend. The event header's **Spent** includes
them.

## Why Add to Weekly Order is greyed out

**Add to Weekly Order** takes you from the event to its weekly order,
ready to send. It stays greyed out until every flower in the event's
**Summary** is whole bunches. Hovering it says "Allocate all spares before
adding to the weekly order." To fix it, clear the **Unrounded Bunches**
table with **Add** or **Remove**. Bunch orders do not have this check.

<Aside type="note">
On a phone, the **Bunches** tab on an event shows the flowers still to
round up, with minus and plus buttons.
</Aside>

## After sending

Spares lock with the rest of the event when the order is sent. For a
change after that, contact your wholesaler.
