Every sent event has a printable **event sheet** that summarises the
whole event: the event's details on the first sheet, then each
arrangement with its flowers. Use it as a brief for your studio team or as a checklist on the
day.

<HelpMedia id="event-printable-page" caption="The event sheet: Event Details, All Flowers, Comments and Arrangements" />

## Printing or saving as PDF

<Steps>

1. Once the order is sent, open the event and click **Download PDF**. On
   a draft event this button reads **Add to Weekly Order** instead, so
   draft events cannot be printed yet.

   You can also click the printer icon (**Print event details**) on the
   event in the Orders calendar.

   <HelpMedia id="event-print-desktop-01-download-pdf" caption="On a sent event, Download PDF replaces Add to Weekly Order." />

2. The event sheet opens. Click **Download Order** to open your browser's
   print window, where you can print or save as PDF. **Close & Back**
   returns you to the app.

   <HelpMedia id="event-print-desktop-02-printable-top" caption="The printable page: the event's details on the first sheet, with Close & Back and Download Order above it." />

   <HelpMedia id="event-print-desktop-03-printable-arrangements" caption="The second sheet, Arrangements: every arrangement with its flowers, colours and stem counts." />

</Steps>

## What is on the sheet

- **Event Details**: name, dates, week, location and wholesaler.
- **All Flowers**: every flower across the event with stems and bunches
  (rounded up). This is the shopping list for the day.
- **Comments**: general and style comments, and any event comments.
- **Arrangements**: each one's quantity, per-arrangement and total stems
  ("N each (M total)") and flower notes. Spares print as the last
  arrangement.

## What is not on the sheet

- Prices. The sheet shows no wholesale or customer prices and no budgets.
- Fulfilment status. To check where each flower is up to, use the order
  page in the app: see [Tracking your order](/florists/tracking-your-order/).

<Aside type="note">
There is no PDF button on a phone. Print from a computer.
</Aside>

## Sharing the sheet

To share with someone who does not have a StemCounts login, save the
sheet as a PDF and email the file. There is no public share link for the
event sheet itself. The wholesaler receives their own order PDF, a
picking document with no prices, attached to the order email.
