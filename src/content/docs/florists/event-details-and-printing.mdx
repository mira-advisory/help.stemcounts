Every event has a **printable event sheet** that summarises the whole
event — flower list, arrangements, comments — on one page. Use it as
a brief for your studio team or as a checklist on the day.

<HelpMedia id="event-printable-page" caption="A printable event sheet, ready to send to a printer or PDF" />

## What's on the sheet

The sheet has four blocks:

### 1. Event details

- Event name and date
- Delivery date and week
- Venue / location
- General and style comments

### 2. All flowers

A single table listing every flower across every arrangement in the
event:

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Flower name | Display name |
| Colour | Colour variant |
| Total stems | Sum across all arrangements |
| Total bunches | Sum rounded up to whole bunches |

This is the **shopping list** for the day.

### 3. Arrangements (one section per arrangement)

For each arrangement:

- Name and quantity (e.g. "Centerpiece × 12")
- Per-arrangement flower table with **stems per arrangement** and
  **total across all of this design**
- Budget figure
- Notes

### 4. Spares

If you added stems to the Spares Bucket, they appear in a separate
section at the end.

## Printing or saving as PDF

<Steps>

1. Open the event details page.

2. Click **Print** (top-right) or use **Ctrl+P** / **Cmd+P**.

3. Your browser's print dialog opens. From here:

   - **Print to a physical printer** — pick your printer and hit print
   - **Save as PDF** — pick "Save as PDF" in the destination dropdown
   - **Email a PDF** — save as PDF first, then attach in your mail client

   <HelpMedia id="event-print-dialog" />

</Steps>

## Sharing without printing

The printable page has a stable URL: `/florist/events/{eventId}/print`.
You can bookmark it or share it with team members who have a StemCounts
login. Anyone signed in to your organisation can open it.

<Aside type="tip">
For sharing with someone who **doesn't** have a StemCounts login, save as
PDF and email the file. There's no public share link for the print
page itself.
</Aside>

## Mobile printing

On phones, the print dialog and "save as PDF" both work as long as
your browser supports them. The layout is tuned for paper sizing so
it looks the same on every device.

## What's NOT on the printable sheet

Things kept off the sheet for clarity:

- Wholesale prices (only your customer-side info is shown)
- Internal status indicators
- Per-line fulfilment status

To check fulfilment status mid-week, use the Order Detail page in the
app, not the printable sheet.
