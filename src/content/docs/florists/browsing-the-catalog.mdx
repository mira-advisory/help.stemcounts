**Explore Flowers** is the catalogue. It shows every flower on
StemCounts, with photos, colours and a price range across wholesalers.
It is for browsing and looking things up. You cannot order from it: for
that, use [the Flower Picker](/florists/the-flower-picker/) inside an
arrangement.

## Where it is

Open your name menu at the top right, click **Settings**, and choose
**Explore Flowers** under **Ordering**. It is also the **Explore Flowers**
tile on the florist home screen.

## When to use it

- **Inspiration** before building an arrangement: what is in season, what
  colours can I get?
- **A closer look** at a flower you saw in the picker.
- **Briefing a client**: open a flower's photo to confirm what they are
  picturing.

## Finding a flower

- **Search**: by name, common name or code. It also matches colour names.
- **Category**: narrow to one category, or all categories.
- **Colour**: choose one or more colours. **Similar shades** (on by
  default) also includes colours close to the ones you pick. Turn it off
  for exact matches only.
- **Season**: show flowers in season in the months you choose.

Filters combine. Pinned flowers always sort to the top.

## What you see per flower

Each flower shows its name, photo and colours. Because Explore Flowers
is not tied to an event, its price is a range across wholesalers, for
example "from $9.00 (5 stems) to $20.00 (10 stems)". These are wholesale
prices, not your customer prices.

Open a flower to see its **Category** and **Season**, and the **Pin to
the top** switch: "Pinned flowers come first, here and when you pick
flowers for an order."

## Explore Flowers and the Flower Picker

| | Explore Flowers | Flower Picker |
|---|---|---|
| **Where** | Settings, under **Ordering** | Inside an event, from **Pick Flowers** or **Add Flowers** |
| **What it is for** | Browse and look up | Add flowers to an arrangement |
| **Price shown** | A range across wholesalers | The event's preferred wholesaler's price, or the catalogue price |
| **What it lists** | The whole catalogue | Only flowers and colours a wholesaler currently offers |
| **Pinning** | **Pin to the top** switch on a flower | Heart on the photo, **Pin flower** |

<Aside type="tip">
The picker is where the work happens. Treat Explore Flowers as your
reference.
</Aside>
