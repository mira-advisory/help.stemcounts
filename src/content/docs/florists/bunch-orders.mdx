A **Bunch Order** is for buying flowers by the bunch: stock for the shop,
a standing weekly buy, anything with no occasion behind it. The form
describes it as "Buy flowers by the bunch. No event, no arrangements."
Everything is counted in whole bunches, so there is no rounding and no
Spares.

## Create a bunch order

<Steps>

1. Click **New Order** on the home screen and switch the **Add Event to
   Order** form to the **Bunch Order** tab.

   <HelpMedia id="add-event-modal" caption="The Add Event to Order form has an Event Order tab and a Bunch Order tab" />

2. Fill in a **Name** and the **Delivery / Pickup Date**. **Location** is
   optional ("if applicable"). **Preferred wholesaler** is optional too.

3. Click **Add and Edit** or **Add to Order**.

</Steps>

A bunch order goes into the weekly order for the week of its **Delivery /
Pickup Date**, alongside any events that week. From there it takes you
into the Flower Picker counting in bunches, so picking a colour means one
bunch rather than one stem.

## The Flowers list

A bunch order has one **Flowers** list instead of arrangement cards. Each
row has a photo and shows "N stems per bunch" (or **Sold per stem**), the
total stems and the price per bunch. The **+** and **-** buttons step in
whole bunches.

At the top:

- **Add Flowers** opens [the Flower Picker](/florists/the-flower-picker/)
  in bunch mode.
- **Favourites** copies in a saved favourite whose **Use it in** is
  **Bunch Order** or **Both**. See [Favourite arrangements](/florists/favourites/).
- **Set a budget** records your budget for the order.

There is no **Summary** or **Spares** panel, because everything is already
whole bunches.

## Preferred wholesaler and bunch sizes

If you set a **Preferred wholesaler**, the picker shows that wholesaler's
price and bunch size for each flower, and the bunch order is counted in
their bunch sizes where they have one. When a wholesaler lists a flower in
more than one length, the picker shows length pills such as **60cm**;
hover one to see who lists that length and at what price.

## Sending it

Click **Add to Weekly Order** when you are ready. The bunch order then
sits on the weekly order with any events for that week, and you choose a
wholesaler per flower and send in the usual way: see
[Sending your order](/florists/sending-your-order/). Bunch orders skip the
whole-bunches check, because they are already whole bunches.

<Aside type="tip">
Need bunches inside an event, next to your arrangements? Click the
bouquet button beside **Add Flowers** on the event page (**Add a Bunches
arrangement**). See [Building an arrangement](/florists/building-an-arrangement/).
</Aside>
