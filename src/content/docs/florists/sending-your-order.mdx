A **draft** weekly order is still yours to edit. Once you **send** it,
each wholesaler gets their own order and the event pages lock. Sending
happens from the weekly order page, in the **Weekly Order Summary**.

## Getting the event onto the weekly order

On the event page, click **Add to Weekly Order**. It is greyed out until
every flower in the event's **Summary** is whole bunches: see
[The Spares bucket](/florists/the-spares-bucket/). It takes you to the
weekly order, where the **Weekly Order Summary** lists every flower in
the order.

## Choosing a wholesaler for each flower

- **Wholesaler:** in the summary header sets a wholesaler on every row
  they stock (**Apply to eligible flowers**).
- Each row also has its own wholesaler list, showing each wholesaler's
  price. **Select wholesaler** clears the row.
- A row with no wholesaler is shaded yellow. A row showing **Default** has
  no wholesaler offering that flower.

A preferred wholesaler set on the event is filled in to start with. You
can use different wholesalers for different flowers.

### Why the bunches changed when you picked a wholesaler

Wholesalers do not all sell a flower in the same bunch size. When you
choose one, each row is recounted in that wholesaler's bunch size and
priced at their price. If your stems no longer make whole bunches, a
**Not whole bunches** window lists each flower with the extra stems and
what they cost. Pick one:

- **Round up** (it shows the stems and the total cost) adds the missing
  stems to Spares on the event.
- **Choose another wholesaler** closes the window so you can pick a
  different one.
- **Remove the extra stems** opens the event on its Bunches tab to trim
  them.

A part bunch cannot be sent, so **Send Order** stays disabled until it is
sorted.

## Comments and missing flowers

- **Comment on a flower:** click a row in the **Weekly Order Summary** to
  open **Comment for wholesaler**. Type your note and click the tick
  (**Save comment**). Comments are also saved when you send.
- **Order note:** click the speech-bubble icon next to the order reference
  (**Open order note**). **Order Note** says "Add a comment for the entire
  order." Type it and click **Save note**.
- **Missing flower:** if the wholesaler you chose does not carry a flower
  or colour, its cell shows an amber warning and a **Request missing
  flower** button appears in the header. The request window previews the
  message, takes an optional note and emails that wholesaler, with you
  copied.

If you filtered the summary to certain events with the eye icon on the
event cards, only those events are sent.

## What the wholesaler receives

Each wholesaler on the order gets:

- an email, **New order from [your business]: [order reference]**, with
  the order PDF attached and an **Accept this order** button. Any Canva
  mood boards pinned to the events are attached too. See
  [Canva mood boards](/florists/canva-mood-boards/).
- an in-app notification, **New order received**, if they have a
  StemCounts account.
- a text message with a link to view and accept, when order text
  messages are switched on.

You are copied on the wholesaler's email, which is your confirmation that
it went out. There is no separate "order sent" email to you. See
[Order messages and notifications](/florists/order-messages-and-notifications/).

<Aside type="note">
On a phone, the order page ends in a **Review and send** bar, with a
line above it saying what still needs doing. That sheet shows the
event, a **Delivery** date, a **Wholesaler**, and each flower's bunches
and stems, then sends. The button reads **Round up** until every line is
whole bunches.
</Aside>

## What is locked after sending

Once the order is sent, the order and event pages become read-only:

- no adding, cloning or deleting arrangements, and no changing flowers,
  quantities, budgets or markups,
- no **Add Event**, and the event **Edit** and **Delete** menu is gone,
- the order note and flower comments can no longer be edited; the note is
  shown read-only in an **Order Note** panel at the top of the order,
- the wholesaler lists become plain wholesaler names.

An event whose flowers have all been sent is locked even if the rest of
the order is still a draft. For a change after sending, contact your
wholesaler, or use **Chat** on the order.

## If the wholesaler rejects the order

While an order is new, the wholesaler can reject it. The order and its
flowers are marked **Rejected** and you get an in-app notification,
**Order rejected**. No email or text is sent for a rejection.

Rejecting does not turn the order back into a draft. Talk to the
wholesaler, or send the flowers again as a new order. If you need a sent
order cancelled, contact support@stemcounts.com or your wholesaler.
