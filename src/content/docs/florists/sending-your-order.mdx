A **draft order** is still yours to edit. Once you **send** it, the
order goes to your wholesaler and editing is locked.

<HelpMedia id="send-order-button" caption="The Send Order action on a weekly order" />

## Before you send: a quick checklist

- All events for the week added
- Each event has at least one arrangement
- Each arrangement has flowers (or you've intentionally left it empty for later)
- Budgets and quantities look right
- The Spares Bucket has cover if you want any
- Notes / special instructions filled in where needed

## Sending

<Steps>

1. Open the weekly order (from the Orders list or the landing page).

2. Review the **Weekly Totals Overview** card — total stems, total
   wholesale cost, total customer revenue.

3. Click **Send Order** at the bottom of the page.

4. Confirm the action. The order status flips from **Draft** to
   **Sent** and lands on your wholesaler's *Receive & Accept* board
   instantly.

   <HelpMedia id="order-status-sent" />

</Steps>

## What happens next

- Your order is locked from edits (except for notes — more on that below)
- We let your wholesaler know straight away
- The order shows up in your Orders list under **Sent**
- It'll move through fulfilment over the next few days — see [Tracking your order](/florists/tracking-your-order/)

## After sending — what you can still change

| Field | Editable after send? |
|---|---|
| Adding events | No |
| Adding arrangements to existing events | No |
| Changing flower quantities | No |
| Order **note** (free-text field) | Yes |
| Per-arrangement notes for the wholesaler | Yes |

If you need to change anything else after sending, contact your
wholesaler directly. They can reject the order, which sends it back to
draft for you to edit.

## Cut-offs

Each wholesaler has a **cut-off time** — usually a fixed day-of-week
and time. Orders sent before the cut-off land on the standard sourcing
window; orders sent after may miss it and need to be confirmed manually.

<Aside type="caution">
Check your wholesaler's cut-off in their welcome email or directly with
them. StemCounts doesn't enforce the cut-off but missing it can mean a
delayed delivery.
</Aside>

## Sending vs rejecting

If a wholesaler rejects your order, it returns to **Draft**. You can
edit and re-send. You'll see a status badge on the order indicating it
was rejected.
