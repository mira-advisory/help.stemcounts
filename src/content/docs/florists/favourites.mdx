A **favourite** is a saved arrangement: a name, a budget, and the flowers
that go in it. Save the designs you make every week once, then add them
to any event in a click. Favourites belong to your organisation, so
everyone at your business shares them.

## Save an arrangement as a favourite

<Steps>

1. On the arrangement card, click the three dots (**More actions**).

   <HelpMedia id="arrangement-3-dot-menu" caption="The star in the More actions menu saves the arrangement as a favourite" />

2. Click the star (**Save as favourite (with its flowers)**).

</Steps>

The arrangement needs a budget first. If it has none you will see **Add a
budget first**. Set a budget on the card and try again.

### If a favourite with that name already exists

StemCounts checks your favourites before saving. If one already carries
the name, a window titled **You already have a favourite called
"[name]"** offers two choices:

- **Update "[name]"**: replaces its saved flowers and budget with this
  arrangement's. Arrangements already made from it keep their flowers.
- **Save as a new favourite**: type a different name. A name that is
  already taken is refused with "You already have a favourite with this
  name. Pick another."

## Use a favourite on an event

There are three places to apply one.

### On an empty arrangement

Click **Fill with a Favourite** on an empty arrangement card and pick one.
Its flowers are copied in. As the window says, "Prices are today's, not
the prices when the favourite was saved." An arrangement that already has
flowers does not show the button.

### When adding arrangements

In the **Add Arrangements** form, your favourites show as pills above the
rows. Click one to add a row with its name and budget. Its flowers go in
when you click **Add Arrangement**, and if some arrangements already have
flowers StemCounts tells you before adding on top. Clicking a pill for a
row that is already in the list shows "[name] is already in the list
below" rather than adding it twice.

You can also save from this form: a star beside a typed name saves that
row as a favourite, once it has a budget.

### On a phone

The arrangement wizard starts with **From scratch** or **From a
favourite**. Pick a favourite and it fills in the name and budget.

<Aside type="note">
A favourite that was saved with flowers carries them in. One saved
without flowers, marked **Budget only** in Settings, gives you just the
name and budget.
</Aside>

## Manage your favourites

Open your name menu, click **Settings**, and choose **Favourite
Arrangements** under **Ordering**. Each row shows the favourite's name and
budget, and **Budget only** when it carries no flowers. If you have none
yet, the screen says **No favourites yet**: "Save one from an
arrangement's star on an order, or add one here."

On each row:

- **Edit favourite** opens a sheet with **Name**, **Budget ex GST**,
  **Use it in** (**Event Order**, counted in stems; **Bunch Order**,
  counted in bunches; or **Both**) and its **Flowers**, each with
  **Remove from recipe**.
- **Duplicate favourite** makes a copy to change.
- **Delete favourite** removes the saved favourite. Arrangements already
  made from it keep their flowers.

## Favourites and bunch orders

A [bunch order](/florists/bunch-orders/) is only offered favourites marked
for bunches. Set **Use it in** to **Bunch Order** or **Both** on the
Favourite Arrangements screen.
