**Canva** is an optional add-on that links your organisation's own Canva
account to StemCounts. Once it is on and connected, each event can carry
one Canva mood board. Pin a design you already have, or start a new board
straight from the event. When you send the order, the mood board goes to
the wholesaler as a PDF with it.

Everything happens in your own Canva account, as you. StemCounts does not
give you a Canva account or share designs between businesses. In the app
it is described as: "Bring your Canva along. Pin a moodboard to an event,
start a card or a sign straight from the order, and your boards travel to
the wholesaler with it."

## It is an add-on you request

Canva is not part of the standard subscription. It is a separate add-on
for your organisation, switched on for each business by the StemCounts
team. Until it is on, you see **Request access** instead of the Canva
controls. The app does not publish a price for the add-on, so ask the
team at support@stemcounts.com about cost before relying on it. There is
no self-serve checkout for Canva in the app.

To ask for it, click the Canva button on an event page, or open
**Settings > Your Organisation** and find the **Canva** section. A window
titled **Request access to Canva** explains the add-on. Click **Request
access** and the team is emailed; you see "Request sent". Your
organisation can ask once a day; asking again shows "Already requested".
The team will be in touch.

## Connect your Canva account

<Steps>

1. With the add-on on, open **Settings > Your Organisation**. The
   **Canva** section shows "Not connected yet." and a **Connect to Canva**
   button.

2. Click it. Your browser goes to Canva, where you sign in and approve
   StemCounts.

3. Canva sends you back. The section shows **Connected as** your Canva
   name, with the date.

   <HelpMedia id="canva-desktop-05-settings-canva-connected" caption="Settings, Your Organisation, Canva: shows Connected as your Canva name, with Disconnect Canva underneath." />

</Steps>

You can also start from an event: with no board pinned, the Canva button
takes you to Canva's login and brings you back to the same event. If it
fails you see "Canva was not connected" with a reason, for example that
you chose not to allow StemCounts, or that the attempt took longer than
ten minutes. Just try again.

Your organisation has one Canva connection, shared by everyone at your
business.

## Pin an existing design to an event

<Steps>

1. Open the event and click the button showing the Canva logo, next to
   **Add to Weekly Order**. It is on a computer or tablet only: the
   event page on a phone does not show it.

   <HelpMedia id="canva-desktop-01-event-canva-button" caption="On an event, the button with the Canva logo sits beside Add to Weekly Order. Click it to attach a mood board." />

2. With no board pinned yet, a window titled **Upload from Canva** shows
   your Canva designs as tiles. Type in **Search your designs, then press
   Enter** to narrow them, or use **Show more**.

   <HelpMedia id="canva-desktop-02-upload-from-canva" caption="Upload from Canva shows your own Canva designs. Click one, then Accept to pin it to the event." />

3. Click a tile to select it, then click **Accept**.

</Steps>

That design becomes the event's mood board and opens in a preview.
Nothing is copied or changed in your Canva. Each event holds one mood
board. If Canva is not connected yet you see "Connect your Canva first,
then come back here." with **Go to Settings**.

## Start a new board from an event

In the same window, under **Or start a new one**, there is a name box
already filled with the order reference and event name. Change it if you
like, then click **Create in Canva**. StemCounts creates a new design in
your own Canva account, sized as an A4 landscape page, pins it to the
event, and opens Canva's editor in a new tab. If you want a different
shape, change it in Canva.

<HelpMedia id="canva-desktop-03-or-start-a-new-one" caption="Under Or start a new one, the name is filled in from the order and event. Click Create in Canva to start a fresh board in your Canva." />

If nothing opens, your browser blocked the new tab: the app says "Your
browser blocked the Canva tab. Allow pop-ups for this site and try
again." Creating a new board replaces any board already pinned to the
event.

## See, edit, swap or remove the board

When an event has a mood board, the Canva button shows a purple count
beside the logo. Click it to open a preview of the board as a PDF,
described as "A preview of the latest version. Make changes in Canva."

<HelpMedia id="canva-desktop-04-event-with-board" caption="An event with a mood board shows a purple 1 on the Canva button." />

- **Edit in Canva** opens the design in Canva in a new tab. Edits show up
  the next time you open the preview.
- **Swap design** opens the picker as **Swap the mood board**, with the
  current board marked **Current**. Whatever you **Accept** (or create)
  replaces it.
- **Remove from event** takes the board off the event, after you confirm.
  "It will not be sent with the order. The design stays in your Canva
  exactly as it is."

Removing or swapping never deletes anything from your Canva account. On
the weekly order page, each event card with a board has a small Canva icon
among its top-right buttons (**Canva mood board**); click it for the same
preview with **Edit in Canva** and **Remove from event**.

If you see "Your Canva connection needs renewing before this can be
shown.", use **Go to Settings** and connect Canva again.

## How the board reaches the wholesaler

When you send an order, the mood boards pinned to the events in that order
are turned into PDFs and attached to the order email to the wholesaler.
Each file is named after the order, Moodboard_ followed by the order
reference, with _2 and _3 added if there is more than one. A wholesaler
only gets the boards for the events whose flowers are on their order.

If a board cannot be attached in time, or the email would be too large,
the order still sends, and the wholesaler can download the board from
their order page, under **Design boards from the florist, made with
Canva**. A design using premium Canva elements your account has not paid
for cannot be exported, so it is skipped. Wholesalers do not need Canva or
any add-on.

## After the order is sent

Once any of an event's flowers are on a sent order, the event's Canva
becomes read-only. The Canva button then opens the exact mood board that
was sent, described as "The mood board that went to the wholesaler with
this order." There is no **Edit in Canva**, **Swap design** or **Remove
from event**. If no board went with the order, the Canva button does not
appear at all. You can still edit the original design in your own Canva,
but it will not change what was sent.

## Disconnect Canva

In the **Canva** section under **Settings > Your Organisation**, click
**Disconnect Canva** and confirm. "StemCounts will forget the connection
and tell Canva to revoke it. Designs already in your Canva stay where
they are. You can connect again at any time." Disconnecting affects
everyone in your business. If the section warns "The last call to Canva
failed. If designs stop opening, disconnect and connect again.", doing
exactly that usually fixes it.

## Why can't I see the Canva button?

- Canva is for florists. Wholesalers never see it.
- It is a paid add-on, and it may not be on for your business yet.
- The Canva button on events, and the icon on the order page's event
  cards, only appear in the larger-screen layout, not on a phone.
- Once an event's order has been sent, the button only appears if a mood
  board went with it.

If none of these apply, ask the team at support@stemcounts.com.

<Aside type="note">
PDF previews open in your browser's own PDF viewer, and opening Canva in
a new tab needs pop-ups allowed for the site.
</Aside>
